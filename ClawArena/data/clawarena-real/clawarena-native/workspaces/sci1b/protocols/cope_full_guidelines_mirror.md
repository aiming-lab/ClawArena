# COPE Full Guidelines Mirror

Source: https://publicationethics.org

## Introduction

COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) was founded in 1997 by a group of medical journal editors who were concerned about the ethics of publication.

## Definitions

Fabrication: making up data or results. Falsification: manipulating data, equipment, or processes.

## Authorship

All authors must have made a substantial contribution to the work.

## Peer review

Peer review is a process used to assess the quality and validity of research before publication.

## Data sharing

Authors should make underlying data available to others on request.

## Corrections

All journals should have a clear policy on how to handle errors and corrections.

## Retractions

Editors should retract articles if findings are unreliable due to misconduct or honest error.

## Allegations of misconduct

Editors should take allegations of misconduct seriously and investigate them thoroughly.

## Competing interests

Authors should disclose any competing interests that might have influenced the study.

## Ethical oversight

Human studies should be conducted with appropriate ethical oversight.
