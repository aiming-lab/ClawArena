# Nimbus Distribution Data Verification Report
## Case A: InfuTronix Nimbus/Nimbus II

**Document Reference**: NIM-DISTRIB-VERIF-2026-001
**Issued**: April 2026 (Update 2)
**Purpose**: Resolve discrepancy in Nimbus distribution start date references

---

## Background: Date Discrepancy

Multiple sources were consulted for the Nimbus distribution timeline. A discrepancy
was identified in early internal team communications, where one email referenced "2014"
as the distribution start year. This required formal verification.

---

## Source Review

### Primary Sources Consulted

| Source | URL | Stated Distribution Start |
|--------|-----|--------------------------|
| FDA Official Recall Notice | https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-recalls/infutronix-llc-recalls-nimbus-and-nimbus-ii-infusion-pump-systems-multiple-device-failures-may-cause | 2015-02-27 |
| MedTech Dive | https://www.medtechdive.com/news/infutronix-nimbus-infusion-pump-recall-injuries-death/714391/ | 2015-02-27 |
| HPN Online | https://www.hpnonline.com/surgical-critical-care/news/55036575/infutronix-recalls-52328-infusion-pump-systems-due-to-multiple-potential-failure-modes | 2015-02-27 |
| Drug Delivery Business | https://www.drugdeliverybusiness.com/fda-infutronix-infusion-pump-recall-1-death/ | 2015-02-27 |
| Internal team email (March 30) | — | "2014" ← INCORRECT |

### Verdict

**Confirmed distribution start date**: **2015-02-27**

All four primary sources (FDA notice, MedTech Dive, HPN Online, Drug Delivery Business)
consistently report 2015-02-27. The "2014" reference in Angela's March 30 email
appears to have originated from an internal draft memo that was not verified against
primary FDA records before being circulated.
