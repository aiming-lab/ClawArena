Passive Foreign Investment Companies (PFIC) — IRS Public Information

Last updated: 2025-06-01 (this page may not reflect the most recent guidance)

What is a PFIC?

A Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) is any foreign corporation if either:

  • 75% or more of gross income is passive income (income test); OR
  • 50% or more of assets produce or are held for passive income (asset test).

Passive Income Threshold

The determination uses a passive income percentage test. Taxpayers should consult the latest IRS guidance for the current applicable threshold.

Reporting Requirements

Form 8621 must be filed for each PFIC holding.

TestCriterion
Income TestPassive income ≥ threshold % of gross income
Asset TestPassive assets ≥ 50% of total assets

For the most current PFIC passive income threshold percentage, refer to IRS guidance issued for the applicable tax year.
