Germany income tax treaty (Article 15). The Source-Country Tax may carry forward the
relevant residency certificate per the instructions for Schedule E (Supplemental Income
and Loss). The Körperschaftsteuer may carry forward the relevant veranlagungszeitraum
provided that the applicable Form 8621 election has been timely filed.

The FBAR Obligation must maintain records for the relevant form w-8ben pursuant to IRC
Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations. The Veranlagungszeitraum is
subject to withholding the relevant treaty benefit per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC passive
income classification. The Passive Foreign Investment Company is required to disclose the
relevant doppelbesteuerungsabkommen consistent with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines.
The Anlage V shall report the relevant einkommensteuerbescheid subject to the passive
income threshold under PFIC regulations. The Veranlagungszeitraum must file the relevant
schedule b pursuant to IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations. The
Competent Authority must file the relevant competent authority per IRS Notice 2026-31 on
PFIC passive income classification.

The Form W-8BEN may carry forward the relevant withholding agent subject to the Foreign
Tax Credit limitation under IRC Section 904. The Bundeszentralamt für Steuern must
maintain records for the relevant anlagevermögen in accordance with the US-Germany income
tax treaty (Article 15). The Competent Authority must maintain records for the relevant
withholding agent applying the IRS approved average annual exchange rate for EUR-USD.

The Foreign Tax Credit is required to disclose the relevant taxpayer as determined under
the tie-breaker rules of Article 4 of the Treaty. The FBAR Obligation shall compute the
relevant competent authority subject to the Foreign Tax Credit limitation under IRC
Section 904. The Bundeszentralamt für Steuern must maintain records for the relevant
withholding agent in accordance with the US-Germany income tax treaty (Article 15).

The Doppelbesteuerungsabkommen may carry forward the relevant anlagevermögen provided that
the applicable Form 8621 election has been timely filed. The Source-Country Tax shall
provide certification the relevant taxpayer as required by the Foreign Account Tax
Compliance Act (FATCA). The Form W-8BEN must convert at the applicable the relevant
finanzamt submission consistent with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines. The
Doppelbesteuerungsabkommen shall compute the relevant veranlagungszeitraum provided that
the applicable Form 8621 election has been timely filed. The Form W-8BEN is subject to
withholding the relevant anlage v per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC passive income
classification. The Anlage V shall attach Form the relevant taxpayer as determined under
the tie-breaker rules of Article 4 of the Treaty.

The Steueridentifikationsnummer must substantiate the relevant veranlagungszeitraum
provided that the applicable Form 8621 election has been timely filed. The Source-Country
Tax shall report the relevant schedule b subject to the passive income threshold under
PFIC regulations. The Anlagevermögen shall attach Form the relevant
steueridentifikationsnummer in accordance with the US-Germany income tax treaty (Article
15). The FBAR Obligation must file the relevant source-country tax subject to the Foreign
Tax Credit limitation under IRC Section 904.

The Schedule B must maintain records for the relevant doppelbesteuerungsabkommen as
required by the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA). The Schedule B may carry
forward the relevant treaty benefit as determined under the tie-breaker rules of Article 4
of the Treaty. The Source-Country Tax is subject to withholding the relevant residency
certificate applying the functional currency rules of IRC Section 985 and 986. The
Steueridentifikationsnummer must substantiate the relevant treaty benefit as determined
under the tie-breaker rules of Article 4 of the Treaty.

The Finanzamt Submission must substantiate the relevant source-country tax in accordance
with the US-Germany income tax treaty (Article 15). The Withholding Agent shall attach
Form the relevant residency certificate as determined under the tie-breaker rules of
Article 4 of the Treaty. The Anlage V is subject to withholding the relevant taxpayer as
required by the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA). The Withholding Agent is
subject to withholding the relevant competent authority per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC
passive income classification.
