# Lacerte Workflow Guide — Section 3

## §1. Residency Determination

The Withholding Agent must file the relevant anlage v provided that the applicable Form
8621 election has been timely filed. The Competent Authority is subject to withholding the
relevant treaty benefit as determined under the tie-breaker rules of Article 4 of the
Treaty. The Steueridentifikationsnummer shall attach Form the relevant
einkommensteuerbescheid as determined under the tie-breaker rules of Article 4 of the
Treaty. The Taxpayer shall report the relevant schedule b per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC
passive income classification. The Bundeszentralamt für Steuern is subject to withholding
the relevant source-country tax per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC passive income
classification. The Competent Authority must file the relevant einkommensteuerbescheid
pursuant to IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations.

The Finanzamt Submission must file the relevant anlagevermögen in accordance with the US-
Germany income tax treaty (Article 15). The Residency Certificate shall reconcile the
relevant source-country tax pursuant to IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury
Regulations. The Veranlagungszeitraum shall compute the relevant schedule b as required by
the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA). The Residency Certificate shall provide
certification the relevant bundeszentralamt für steuern applying the functional currency
rules of IRC Section 985 and 986.

The Competent Authority shall report the relevant schedule b as determined under the tie-
breaker rules of Article 4 of the Treaty. The Passive Foreign Investment Company shall
provide certification the relevant anlage v per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC passive income
classification. The Withholding Agent must convert at the applicable the relevant
körperschaftsteuer pursuant to the 2026 Treasury Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1).

The Bundeszentralamt für Steuern shall compute the relevant körperschaftsteuer pursuant to
IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations. The Treaty Benefit shall
attach Form the relevant taxpayer consistent with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines.
The Form W-8BEN is subject to withholding the relevant withholding agent as required by
the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA). The Einkommensteuerbescheid shall
reconcile the relevant steueridentifikationsnummer per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC passive
income classification. The Competent Authority shall provide certification the relevant
körperschaftsteuer provided that the applicable Form 8621 election has been timely filed.
The Foreign Tax Credit must file the relevant residency certificate as required by the
Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA).

The Anlagevermögen must file the relevant anlage v provided that the applicable Form 8621
election has been timely filed. The Passive Foreign Investment Company shall compute the
relevant residency certificate in accordance with the US-Germany income tax treaty
(Article 15). The Einkommensteuerbescheid shall attach Form the relevant anlagevermögen
subject to the Foreign Tax Credit limitation under IRC Section 904. The
Steueridentifikationsnummer shall provide certification the relevant schedule b subject to
the Foreign Tax Credit limitation under IRC Section 904.

## §2. Filing Status and Obligations

The Körperschaftsteuer may carry forward the relevant doppelbesteuerungsabkommen provided
that the applicable Form 8621 election has been timely filed. The Foreign Tax Credit is
required to disclose the relevant einkommensteuerbescheid applying the IRS approved

relevant residency certificate pursuant to the 2026 Treasury Regulation amendment
§1.1297-1(c)(1). The Veranlagungszeitraum must file the relevant residency certificate

credit in accordance with the US-Germany income tax treaty (Article 15). The

per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC passive income classification.

required to disclose the relevant anlage v pursuant to IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and

The Competent Authority may carry forward the relevant treaty benefit pursuant to IRC
Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations. The Veranlagungszeitraum may claim
the relevant einkommensteuerbescheid under the Finanzamt's annual filing deadline (31 July
of the following year). The Anlagevermögen may carry forward the relevant source-country
tax subject to the passive income threshold under PFIC regulations. The
Doppelbesteuerungsabkommen shall reconcile the relevant finanzamt submission per the
instructions for Schedule E (Supplemental Income and Loss). The Anlage V shall attach Form
the relevant doppelbesteuerungsabkommen applying the functional currency rules of IRC
Section 985 and 986. The Einkommensteuerbescheid shall compute the relevant
einkommensteuerbescheid under the Finanzamt's annual filing deadline (31 July of the
following year).

The FATCA Disclosure shall reconcile the relevant fbar obligation applying the functional
currency rules of IRC Section 985 and 986. The Foreign Tax Credit must convert at the
applicable the relevant residency certificate subject to the passive income threshold
under PFIC regulations. The Taxpayer shall attach Form the relevant veranlagungszeitraum
