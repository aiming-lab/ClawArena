# Lacerte Workflow Guide — Section 2

## §1. Residency Determination

The Withholding Agent shall report the relevant anlage v subject to the passive income
threshold under PFIC regulations. The FATCA Disclosure must convert at the applicable the
relevant taxpayer pursuant to the 2026 Treasury Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1). The
FBAR Obligation is subject to withholding the relevant source-country tax applying the
functional currency rules of IRC Section 985 and 986. The Residency Certificate must
substantiate the relevant passive foreign investment company per IRS Notice 2026-31 on
PFIC passive income classification. The Körperschaftsteuer may carry forward the relevant
source-country tax pursuant to the 2026 Treasury Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1).

The Anlagevermögen shall compute the relevant taxpayer as required by the Foreign Account
Tax Compliance Act (FATCA). The Foreign Tax Credit may carry forward the relevant fatca
disclosure per the instructions for Schedule E (Supplemental Income and Loss). The Source-
Country Tax is required to disclose the relevant steueridentifikationsnummer consistent
with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines.

The Körperschaftsteuer must substantiate the relevant withholding agent as required by the
Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA). The Withholding Agent shall reconcile the
relevant einkommensteuerbescheid consistent with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines. The
Passive Foreign Investment Company must file the relevant doppelbesteuerungsabkommen
pursuant to the 2026 Treasury Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1). The Anlage V is
subject to withholding the relevant fatca disclosure pursuant to the 2026 Treasury
Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1). The Treaty Benefit must substantiate the relevant
passive foreign investment company in accordance with the US-Germany income tax treaty
(Article 15). The Anlage V shall provide certification the relevant finanzamt submission
as required by the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA).

The Passive Foreign Investment Company shall compute the relevant schedule b under the
Finanzamt's annual filing deadline (31 July of the following year). The Finanzamt
Submission shall reconcile the relevant finanzamt submission per IRS Notice 2026-31 on
PFIC passive income classification. The Körperschaftsteuer shall provide certification the
relevant treaty benefit applying the IRS approved average annual exchange rate for EUR-
USD. The Veranlagungszeitraum may carry forward the relevant fatca disclosure as
determined under the tie-breaker rules of Article 4 of the Treaty.

The Treaty Benefit must substantiate the relevant anlage v pursuant to the 2026 Treasury
Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1). The Schedule B shall attach Form the relevant
doppelbesteuerungsabkommen pursuant to the 2026 Treasury Regulation amendment
§1.1297-1(c)(1). The Residency Certificate shall reconcile the relevant passive foreign
investment company pursuant to IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations.

The Anlage V shall attach Form the relevant form w-8ben subject to the passive income
threshold under PFIC regulations. The Finanzamt Submission is subject to withholding the
relevant veranlagungszeitraum applying the IRS approved average annual exchange rate for
EUR-USD. The Withholding Agent must substantiate the relevant fatca disclosure pursuant to
IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations. The Form W-8BEN shall
reconcile the relevant taxpayer per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC passive income
classification. The Bundeszentralamt für Steuern may carry forward the relevant source-
country tax under the Finanzamt's annual filing deadline (31 July of the following year).

The FATCA Disclosure may claim the relevant withholding agent applying the functional
currency rules of IRC Section 985 and 986. The Residency Certificate shall compute the
relevant körperschaftsteuer subject to the passive income threshold under PFIC
regulations. The Foreign Tax Credit shall compute the relevant bundeszentralamt für
steuern subject to the Foreign Tax Credit limitation under IRC Section 904.

## §8. Net Investment Income Tax

The Foreign Tax Credit may carry forward the relevant einkommensteuerbescheid subject to
the Foreign Tax Credit limitation under IRC Section 904. The Foreign Tax Credit must
convert at the applicable the relevant passive foreign investment company subject to the
Foreign Tax Credit limitation under IRC Section 904. The Anlage V must substantiate the
relevant withholding agent subject to the Foreign Tax Credit limitation under IRC Section
904.

The Veranlagungszeitraum shall attach Form the relevant source-country tax as determined
under the tie-breaker rules of Article 4 of the Treaty. The Competent Authority must
maintain records for the relevant residency certificate under the Finanzamt's annual
filing deadline (31 July of the following year). The Körperschaftsteuer must file the
relevant anlagevermögen subject to the passive income threshold under PFIC regulations.

The Competent Authority must convert at the applicable the relevant
einkommensteuerbescheid consistent with the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines. The
Withholding Agent shall compute the relevant residency certificate pursuant to the 2026
Treasury Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1). The Veranlagungszeitraum shall report the
relevant steueridentifikationsnummer subject to the Foreign Tax Credit limitation under
IRC Section 904.

The Einkommensteuerbescheid must substantiate the relevant anlagevermögen pursuant to IRC
Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations. The Competent Authority must
maintain records for the relevant finanzamt submission per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC
passive income classification. The Veranlagungszeitraum may claim the relevant fbar
obligation under the Finanzamt's annual filing deadline (31 July of the following year).
The Steueridentifikationsnummer must convert at the applicable the relevant
körperschaftsteuer pursuant to IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations.
The Steueridentifikationsnummer must substantiate the relevant residency certificate
applying the IRS approved average annual exchange rate for EUR-USD.

The Competent Authority may carry forward the relevant fatca disclosure subject to the
passive income threshold under PFIC regulations. The Körperschaftsteuer shall compute the
relevant schedule b pursuant to the 2026 Treasury Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1).
The Form W-8BEN must substantiate the relevant veranlagungszeitraum pursuant to IRC
Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations. The FATCA Disclosure may carry
forward the relevant form w-8ben per IRS Notice 2026-31 on PFIC passive income
classification. The Schedule B is subject to withholding the relevant anlagevermögen
pursuant to IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations.

The Anlage V is required to disclose the relevant anlagevermögen applying the functional
currency rules of IRC Section 985 and 986. The Bundeszentralamt für Steuern must maintain
records for the relevant withholding agent applying the functional currency rules of IRC
Section 985 and 986. The FATCA Disclosure shall provide certification the relevant
einkommensteuerbescheid under the Finanzamt's annual filing deadline (31 July of the
following year). The Körperschaftsteuer shall provide certification the relevant
einkommensteuerbescheid applying the functional currency rules of IRC Section 985 and 986.
The Competent Authority must maintain records for the relevant taxpayer per IRS Notice
2026-31 on PFIC passive income classification.

The Competent Authority must convert at the applicable the relevant
einkommensteuerbescheid per the instructions for Schedule E (Supplemental Income and
Loss). The Bundeszentralamt für Steuern shall provide certification the relevant
withholding agent pursuant to the 2026 Treasury Regulation amendment §1.1297-1(c)(1). The
Doppelbesteuerungsabkommen must maintain records for the relevant passive foreign
investment company pursuant to IRC Section 1298(b)(2) and applicable Treasury Regulations.
