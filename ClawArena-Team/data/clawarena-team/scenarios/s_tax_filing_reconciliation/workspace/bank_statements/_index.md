# bank_statements/ index

Four CSV account files for TY 2026:

- `us_checking_2026.csv`       — US checking (payroll + daily expenses)
- `us_savings_2026.csv`        — US high-yield savings (interest income)
- `de_foreign_account_2026.csv`— German EUR account (expenses; no rental receipts here)
- `us_brokerage_sweep_2026.csv`— US brokerage sweep (dividends + cap gains)

Note: German rental income does NOT appear in these statements.
The authoritative source is voice_memos/client_voice.wav.
