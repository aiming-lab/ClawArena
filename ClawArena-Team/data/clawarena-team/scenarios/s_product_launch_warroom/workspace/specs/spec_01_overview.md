## Summary

on 2026-06-17. Content draws on the original product brief and the engineering 
review held 2026-05-10.

## Goals

- Deliver the helix-7 platform overview capability to the mid-market SaaS segment by 
  the launch date.

  which is the headline positioning claim approved by the launch council.
- Maintain depth of historical context — Helix-7 retains 18 months of rolling 
  correlation data, exposed through the historical lens introduced in `spec_06`.

## Non-goals

- This document does NOT prescribe pricing — refer to `spec_09_pricing_tiers.md`.
- Customer-evidence claims are governed by `legal_review/memo_01_claims_substantiation.md`.
