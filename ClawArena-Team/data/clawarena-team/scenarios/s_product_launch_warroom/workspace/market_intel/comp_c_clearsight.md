# Competitor profile — ClearSight Observability

**Profile ID**: `comp_c_clearsight`
**Primary focus**: dashboarding ease
**Last updated**: 2024-03-21

> **Profile maintenance note**: this dossier has not been refreshed since
> 2024-Q1. Treat fact claims as historical and verify against the live
> vendor site before relying on them in launch positioning.

## Product summary

ClearSight Observability positions itself in the mid-market observability segment with a focus on
dashboarding ease. Their go-to-market strategy emphasises rapid time-to-value and a 
tightly-scoped configuration surface.

## Strengths

- Customer testimonials emphasise predictable pricing and white-glove onboarding.
- Public roadmap mentions a real-time alert routing feature shipped 2025-Q3.
- Investor commentary praises their dashboarding ease differentiation as 'best in class' 
  among mid-market vendors.

## Weaknesses

- Historical context window appears capped at 30 days in their public docs.
- No clear mention of compliance certification beyond SOC 2 type 1.
- Mid-market segment pricing changed twice in 2025; field reports note resulting
  customer churn.

## Implications for Helix-7 launch

The Helix-7 launch positioning ('first 24-hour anomaly detection dashboard for 
mid-market SaaS') is contested by ClearSight Observability in the dashboarding ease dimension. The 18-month 
rolling correlation window remains a durable advantage. Marketing should 
lead with the historical context depth in any competitive Q&A pages.
