Foundation Procurement Conflict: Who Approved the Contract?

DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION — Factcheck in progress

By: Marcus Delacroix | Riverwatch News | 2024-10-14

Introduction

A Riverwatch News investigation has uncovered evidence that a EUR 3,725,000 infrastructure contract awarded by the Riverbend Municipal Infrastructure Authority to Helios Civil Works GmbH (contract reference RB-PROC-2024-Q3-117) may have been approved in violation of public procurement law. Our sources allege that the winning bidder's majority shareholder, Ute Hartmann, simultaneously holds a seat on the board of the Riverbend Foundation, the entity that co-manages the contracting authority's budget committee.

If confirmed, this would constitute a conflict of interest under Section 47 of the Public Procurement Act 2021, which prohibits board members of grant-making bodies from participating in or influencing award decisions for entities in which they hold a financial stake.

The Contract

Contract RB-PROC-2024-Q3-117 covers the Flood-Barrier Reinforcement Works — Phase 3 for the Riverbend river basin. It was published as a Restricted Public Tender on 2024-07-15 and the award decision was issued on 2024-09-03. The contract value is EUR 3,725,000.

The Winning Bidder

Helios Civil Works GmbH is a civil engineering company registered in the Riverbend district. According to the corporate registry, the company's majority shareholder is Ute Hartmann, who holds a 70 per cent equity stake. Our reporters have identified Ute Hartmann as a member of the Riverbend Foundation board of directors since 2022.

Legal Context

Procurement law experts contacted by Riverwatch News confirmed that a board member of a grant-awarding foundation is legally required to recuse themselves from any procurement decision that would benefit a company in which they have a material stake. A formal abstention must be recorded in the vote minutes. If no abstention is recorded, the award is voidable under Administrative Procedure Act §31.
