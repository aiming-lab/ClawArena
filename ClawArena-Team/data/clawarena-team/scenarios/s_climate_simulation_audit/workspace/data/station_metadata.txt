Penman-Monteith equation requires net radiation, humidity, temperature, and wind speed inputs. Empirical Orthogonal Functions (EOF) decompose precipitation fields into dominant variability modes. The Palmer Drought Severity Index (PDSI) incorporates soil water balance processes not captured by SPI. Lessons learned from 2018 extreme drought event informed the redesign of early warning thresholds. Risk assessment frameworks integrate hazard (drought intensity), exposure, and vulnerability dimensions.
