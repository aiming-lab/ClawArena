## Chapter 5 — Performance Improvement Plans (Historical)

### 5.2 PIP Duration (SUPERSEDED)

**Note: This section is SUPERSEDED and should NOT be relied upon.**

Under the original 2019 policy, the minimum PIP duration was FIFTEEN (15)
calendar days. This provision was revised upward in subsequent handbook versions.

For current minimum PIP duration requirements, refer to Version 3.0, Chapter 5,
Section 5.2, which establishes the current minimum of thirty (30) calendar days.
