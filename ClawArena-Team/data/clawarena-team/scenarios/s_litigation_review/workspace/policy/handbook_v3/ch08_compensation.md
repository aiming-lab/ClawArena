# Chapter 8 — Compensation and Benefits

## 8.1 Salary Administration

Salaries are set with reference to the Company's salary bands, which are
reviewed annually by the Compensation Committee. Salary adjustments take effect
on the first pay period following the annual performance review cycle and are
based on the employee's overall rating, position in band, and Company
performance.

## 8.2 Payroll

Employees are paid on a bi-weekly basis. Payroll inquiries should be directed
to the Payroll Department within sixty (60) days of the payment in question;
claims of underpayment raised more than one (1) year after the relevant pay
period will be processed only where required by law.

## 8.3 Benefits

Full-time regular employees are eligible, subject to plan terms, for medical,
dental, and vision insurance; a retirement savings plan with Company matching
contributions; group life and disability insurance; and an annual allotment
of paid time off as described in Chapter 9. Part-time regular employees are
eligible for a prorated benefits package on the same terms.

## 8.4 Expense Reimbursement

Reasonable and necessary business expenses are reimbursable upon submission of
an expense report with appropriate substantiation, in accordance with the
Company's Expense Policy. Expenses submitted more than ninety (90) days after
the date of incurrence may be refused at the discretion of the Finance
Department.

## 8.5 Stock and Equity

Equity awards, where granted, are governed by the terms of the Company's equity
incentive plan and the individual award agreement. Nothing in this Handbook
varies the terms of any equity award.
