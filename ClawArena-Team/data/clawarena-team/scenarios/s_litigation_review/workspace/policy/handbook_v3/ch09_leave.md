# Chapter 9 — Leave Policies

## 9.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time regular employees accrue PTO at the rate of one and a quarter (1.25)
days per month of service, for an annual entitlement of fifteen (15) working
days. Part-time regular employees accrue on a prorated basis. PTO must be
scheduled in advance with the employee's supervisor and is granted subject to
operational requirements.

Unused PTO carries over to the next calendar year up to a maximum of ten (10)
days. PTO in excess of the carryover cap is forfeited unless otherwise required
by applicable law.

## 9.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to up to ten (10) days of paid sick leave per calendar
year. Sick leave of three (3) or more consecutive days requires medical
substantiation. Sick leave does not accrue or carry over and is not paid out at
separation.

## 9.3 Family and Medical Leave

The Company provides family and medical leave in accordance with applicable
statutory entitlements. Eligible employees may take up to twelve (12) weeks of
job-protected leave per twelve-month period for the qualifying reasons set out
in the applicable statute. The Human Resources Department administers all
family and medical leave requests.

## 9.4 Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to up to five (5) days of paid bereavement leave on the
death of an immediate family member and up to two (2) days on the death of an
extended family member, as those terms are defined in the Glossary.

## 9.5 Jury Duty and Civic Leave

Employees summoned to jury service or other compulsory civic duty are entitled
to paid leave for the duration of the service. Employees must provide the
summons or other official notice to HR as soon as practicable.

## 9.6 Unpaid Leave

Unpaid personal leave of up to thirty (30) days may be granted at the
discretion of HR for compelling personal circumstances not otherwise addressed
by this Chapter.
