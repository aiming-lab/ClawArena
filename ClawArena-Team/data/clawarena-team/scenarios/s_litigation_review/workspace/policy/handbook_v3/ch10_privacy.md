# Chapter 10 — Data Privacy and Confidentiality

## 10.1 Employee Personal Data

The Company collects, holds, and processes personal data concerning its
employees for the purposes of administering the employment relationship,
complying with statutory obligations, and conducting its lawful business. The
Company's processing of employee personal data is governed by the Employee
Privacy Notice, which is provided to each employee at onboarding and made
available through HR on request.

## 10.2 Confidential Business Information

Each employee owes a duty of confidence to the Company in respect of all
non-public information acquired in the course of employment, including but not
limited to client information, financial information, trade secrets, technical
know-how, business plans, and personnel information (including the existence
and content of PIPs and disciplinary records). This duty continues after the
termination of employment.

## 10.3 Client Confidentiality

Where the Company holds client information under a contractual or
professional duty of confidence, employees are bound by the same duty. Breach
of client confidentiality is gross misconduct under Chapter 6.

## 10.4 Acceptable Use of Information Systems

The Company's information systems are provided for business use. The Company
monitors use of its systems for security, compliance, and operational
purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy in their use of
Company information systems other than as expressly provided in the Employee
Privacy Notice or as required by law.

## 10.5 Reporting Data Incidents

Any actual or suspected breach of personal data or confidential information
must be reported to the Information Security team and to HR without delay, and
in any event within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.
