# Chapter 3 — Code of Conduct and Professional Standards

## 3.1 General Standards of Conduct

Every employee is expected to conduct themselves with integrity, professionalism,
and respect toward colleagues, clients, vendors, and members of the public. The
Company will not tolerate conduct that is dishonest, abusive, discriminatory, or
otherwise inconsistent with the values described in Chapter 1.

The following non-exhaustive list of conduct is prohibited and may give rise to
disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination:

(a) theft, fraud, or misappropriation of Company or client property;
(b) falsification of records, including time records, expense reports, and
    performance documents;
(c) unauthorized disclosure of confidential information (see Chapter 10);
(d) harassment or discrimination on a protected basis (see Chapter 12);
(e) violence, threats of violence, or intimidation;
(f) use or possession of controlled substances or alcohol on Company premises
    or while performing Company duties, except as expressly authorized;
(g) gross insubordination or repeated refusal to perform assigned duties;
(h) any violation of law committed in the course of employment.

## 3.2 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose to their supervisor and to the Human Resources Department
any outside activity, financial interest, or personal relationship that could
reasonably be perceived as creating a conflict with the interests of the Company.
Disclosure must be made promptly upon the employee becoming aware of the
potential conflict.

## 3.3 Use of Company Resources

Company resources — including premises, equipment, information systems,
intellectual property, and confidential information — are provided for business
purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted to the extent that it does not
interfere with the employee's duties, impose more than de minimis cost on the
Company, or contravene any other provision of this Handbook.

## 3.4 Reporting Misconduct

Employees who become aware of conduct that may violate this Chapter or applicable
law are expected to report the matter promptly through the channels described in
Chapter 7. Good-faith reports are protected from retaliation.
