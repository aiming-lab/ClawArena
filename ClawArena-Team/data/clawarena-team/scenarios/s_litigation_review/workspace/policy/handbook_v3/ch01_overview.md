# Chapter 1 — Introduction and Company Mission

Acme Corporation (hereinafter "the Company") is committed to fostering a workplace
characterized by mutual respect, professional excellence, and adherence to the
highest ethical standards. This Employee Handbook ("the Handbook") sets forth the
policies, procedures, and expectations that govern the employment relationship
between the Company and each of its employees.

The Company's mission is to deliver exceptional value to its clients, shareholders,
and communities while ensuring that every employee is treated with fairness,
dignity, and transparency. The policies contained herein reflect that commitment
and shall be interpreted in a manner consistent with applicable federal, state, and
local law.

## 1.1 Scope

This Handbook applies to all employees of the Company, including full-time,
part-time, temporary, and probationary employees, except where a specific
provision is expressly limited to a particular employee class. Where the
Handbook is silent, the Company's general operating procedures and applicable
law shall govern.

## 1.2 Relationship to Other Documents

In the event of a conflict between this Handbook and (i) an individual employment
agreement signed by an authorized officer of the Company, (ii) a collective
bargaining agreement, or (iii) a binding statute or regulation, the latter shall
prevail to the extent of the inconsistency. All other Company policies, manuals,
and standard operating procedures must be read consistently with this Handbook.

## 1.3 Interpretation

All employees are expected to read this Handbook carefully and to seek
clarification from the Human Resources Department whenever ambiguity arises.
Ignorance of Handbook provisions is not a valid defense to a disciplinary
finding. The masculine form includes the feminine and gender-neutral; the
singular includes the plural; and references to days mean calendar days unless
otherwise specified.
