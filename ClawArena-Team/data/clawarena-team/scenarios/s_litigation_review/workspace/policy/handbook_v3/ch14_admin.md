# Chapter 14 — General Administrative Provisions

## 14.1 Effective Date

This Handbook (Version 3.0) is effective as of January 1, 2025, and supersedes
all prior versions of the Employee Handbook.

## 14.2 Amendment

The Company reserves the right to amend, supplement, or revoke any provision
of this Handbook at any time, subject to applicable law and to any contrary
provision of an individual employment agreement. Amendments shall be
communicated to employees in writing and shall take effect on the date stated
in the amendment.

## 14.3 Severability

If any provision of this Handbook is held to be unenforceable by a court or
tribunal of competent jurisdiction, the remaining provisions shall continue
in full force and effect, and the unenforceable provision shall be construed,
to the extent possible, in a manner that gives effect to the parties'
intent and is consistent with applicable law.

## 14.4 Headings

Headings in this Handbook are provided for convenience only and do not affect
the interpretation of the provisions to which they relate.

## 14.5 Acknowledgement

Each employee shall sign an acknowledgement form confirming receipt of this
Handbook and agreement to abide by its terms. The acknowledgement does not
create an employment contract or vary the at-will nature of the employment
relationship as set out in Chapter 2.

## 14.6 Custody and Distribution

The authoritative version of this Handbook is maintained by the Human
Resources Department. Where there is any inconsistency between a printed copy
and the electronic version maintained by HR, the electronic version governs.
