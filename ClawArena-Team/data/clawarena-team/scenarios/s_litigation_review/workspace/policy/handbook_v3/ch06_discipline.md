# Chapter 6 — Disciplinary Action Procedures

## 6.1 Progressive Discipline

The Company ordinarily applies progressive discipline to address employee
misconduct or persistent performance deficiencies. The standard progression is
(i) verbal counseling, (ii) written warning, (iii) final written warning, and
(iv) termination. The Company reserves the right to skip steps where the
seriousness of the misconduct so warrants.

## 6.2 Gross Misconduct

The following conduct constitutes gross misconduct and may give rise to
immediate termination without recourse to the progressive discipline
procedure: theft, fraud, violence, harassment of a severe or pervasive
character, willful destruction of Company property, falsification of records,
intentional disclosure of confidential information, and any criminal act
committed in the course of employment.

## 6.3 Investigation

Before any disciplinary action above verbal counseling is imposed, the
supervisor, in consultation with HR, shall conduct a reasonable investigation
proportionate to the seriousness of the allegations. The investigation shall
include an opportunity for the employee to respond to the allegations in
person and in writing.

## 6.4 Documentation

All disciplinary actions must be documented in writing, signed by the
supervisor and an HR representative, and provided to the employee. The employee
may submit a written response, which shall be filed with the disciplinary
record.

## 6.5 Relationship to PIP

A PIP under Chapter 5 is a performance management instrument and is not, of
itself, a disciplinary action. However, failure to meet PIP objectives may be
followed by disciplinary action under this Chapter.
