# Chapter 12 — Anti-Discrimination and Harassment Policy

## 12.1 Policy Statement

Acme Corporation is committed to providing a workplace free of unlawful
discrimination and harassment. The Company prohibits discrimination,
harassment, and retaliation against any employee, applicant, contractor, or
visitor on the basis of race, color, ethnicity, national origin, religion,
sex, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, age, disability,
pregnancy, marital or family status, veteran status, or any other
characteristic protected by applicable law.

## 12.2 Prohibited Conduct

Prohibited conduct includes, without limitation:

(a) employment decisions made on the basis of a protected characteristic;
(b) unwelcome verbal, written, visual, or physical conduct directed at an
    individual on the basis of a protected characteristic that creates a
    hostile or intimidating work environment;
(c) sexual harassment, including quid pro quo conduct and hostile environment
    conduct;
(d) retaliation against any person for raising a complaint, participating in
    an investigation, or otherwise exercising rights under this Chapter.

## 12.3 Reporting

Employees who experience or witness conduct prohibited by this Chapter are
strongly encouraged to report the matter to their supervisor, to HR, or to
the General Counsel. Reports may be made anonymously through the Company's
confidential ethics line.

## 12.4 Investigation

All reports under this Chapter shall be investigated promptly and fairly. The
investigator shall be impartial and shall afford the subject of the complaint
an opportunity to respond. Findings shall be documented in writing.

## 12.5 Reasonable Accommodation

The Company will provide reasonable accommodation for disability, religion,
pregnancy, and any other ground for which accommodation is required by law,
unless to do so would impose undue hardship. Requests for accommodation are
processed by HR in consultation with the employee and, where applicable, with
the employee's medical or religious advisor.
