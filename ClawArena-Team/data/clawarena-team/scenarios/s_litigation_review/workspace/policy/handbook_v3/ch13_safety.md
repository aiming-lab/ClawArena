# Chapter 13 — Workplace Safety

## 13.1 General Duty

The Company is committed to providing a safe and healthful workplace. Every
employee is expected to comply with all applicable health and safety
requirements and to take reasonable care for their own safety and that of
others affected by their work.

## 13.2 Incident Reporting

All workplace incidents — including injuries, near misses, property damage,
and incidents of workplace violence — must be reported to the employee's
supervisor and to HR as soon as practicable, and in any event within
twenty-four (24) hours.

## 13.3 Emergency Procedures

Each Company facility maintains a written emergency action plan that addresses
evacuation, shelter-in-place, medical emergency, and active threat scenarios.
Employees are required to familiarize themselves with the plan applicable to
their primary work location and to participate in periodic drills.

## 13.4 Personal Protective Equipment

Where personal protective equipment is required by applicable law or by the
nature of the work, the Company shall provide such equipment at no cost to
the employee. Employees are required to use the equipment as instructed.

## 13.5 Right to Refuse Unsafe Work

An employee who has a good-faith and reasonable belief that the performance
of an assigned task would expose them to an imminent risk of serious injury
may refuse to perform the task and shall report the matter to their supervisor
and to HR. No employee shall be subjected to retaliation for the good-faith
exercise of this right.
