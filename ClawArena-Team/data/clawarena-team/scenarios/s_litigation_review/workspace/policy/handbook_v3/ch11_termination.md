# Chapter 11 — Termination and Offboarding Procedures

## 11.1 Grounds for Termination

The Company may terminate the employment relationship for any lawful reason,
including without limitation: completion of a PIP under Chapter 5 with a
"Failure to meet objectives" outcome; gross misconduct under Chapter 6;
redundancy or workforce restructuring; expiry of a fixed-term engagement; or
failure to meet the conditions of probationary employment under Chapter 2.

Termination decisions must be approved in advance by the relevant department
head and by HR. Where a termination is predicated in whole or in part on PIP
non-completion, HR shall confirm that the procedural requirements of Chapter 5
have been satisfied — including, in particular, expiry of the minimum PIP
duration set out in Section 5.2 and conduct of the mid-point review required
by Section 5.3.

## 11.2 Notice

Except in cases of gross misconduct, terminated employees are entitled to the
greater of (i) two (2) weeks' notice or payment in lieu, or (ii) the notice
period specified in any applicable employment agreement or by law.

## 11.3 Final Pay

Final pay, including any accrued but unused PTO that is payable on
separation, shall be provided in accordance with applicable wage payment law,
and in any event no later than the next regular payday following the
separation date.

## 11.4 Return of Company Property

On or before the separation date, the employee shall return all Company
property, including but not limited to keys, identification cards, laptops,
mobile devices, documents, and any media containing confidential information.

## 11.5 Continuing Obligations

The duty of confidence under Chapter 10 and any post-employment restrictive
covenant set out in the employee's individual employment agreement survive
termination.

## 11.6 References

External reference requests are handled exclusively by HR. Individual
supervisors are not authorized to provide employment references on behalf of
the Company.
