# Chapter 7 — Grievance and Appeals Process

## 7.1 Right to Grieve

Every employee has the right to bring forward a grievance concerning the
application of this Handbook, the conduct of a supervisor or colleague, or any
other matter affecting the terms and conditions of employment. The grievance
process is confidential to the extent permitted by law.

## 7.2 Informal Resolution

In the first instance, an employee is encouraged to raise the matter with their
direct supervisor. Where the grievance concerns the supervisor, the employee
may raise the matter with the supervisor's manager or with HR.

## 7.3 Formal Grievance

If informal resolution is unsuccessful or inappropriate, the employee may file
a written grievance with HR within thirty (30) calendar days of the event
giving rise to the grievance. The written grievance must state the facts
complained of, the relief sought, and any supporting documentation.

## 7.4 Grievance Panel

HR shall convene a Grievance Panel of not fewer than three (3) members, none
of whom shall have been directly involved in the matter complained of. The
Panel shall hear the grievance, receive evidence, and issue a written decision
within thirty (30) calendar days of receiving the grievance.

## 7.5 Appeal

The decision of the Grievance Panel may be appealed in writing to the General
Counsel within ten (10) business days. The General Counsel's decision is final
and binding within the Company, without prejudice to the employee's statutory
rights.

## 7.6 Non-Retaliation

No employee shall be subjected to retaliation, adverse employment action, or
informal reprisal for raising a grievance in good faith. Retaliation is itself a
disciplinable offense under Chapter 6.
