# HR Administrative Procedures

**Document ID:** HR-ADM-001 | **Revision:** 1.4 | **Effective:** March 1, 2025
**Owner:** People Operations | **Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the administrative procedures governing routine HR operations, including records management, new hire processing, offboarding, leave request workflows, and inter-departmental coordination. These procedures are intended for use by HR staff and department managers and do not constitute the full policy framework contained in the Employee Handbook.

---

## 2. New Hire Processing

### 2.1 Pre-Hire Checklist

Prior to the employee's first day, the assigned HR coordinator must complete the following:

- Confirm signed offer letter is on file.
- Initiate background check through the approved vendor portal (HR-Vendor-BGC).
- Submit IT access request (form IT-ACCESS-01) at least three business days before start date.
- Prepare welcome packet containing benefits overview, parking/building access information, and the Employee Handbook acknowledgment form.
- Schedule Day 1 orientation with the hiring manager.

### 2.2 Onboarding Window

All new hire paperwork (I-9 verification, direct deposit authorization, tax withholding elections, benefits enrollment) must be completed within the first five business days of employment. The HR coordinator is responsible for following up with employees who have not completed required forms by the end of day three.

### 2.3 Benefits Enrollment Deadline

Benefits elections must be submitted through the HR portal within 30 calendar days of the hire date. Employees who miss this window may only make elections during the annual open enrollment period or following a qualifying life event.

## 3. Records Management

### 3.1 Employee Files

HR maintains two categories of employee files:

- **Personnel File:** Contains offer letter, performance reviews, commendations, corrective action documentation, and separation records. Accessible to the employee upon written request with 48-hour notice.
- **Confidential File:** Contains medical information, background check results, EEO data, and accommodation requests. Maintained separately from the personnel file; access restricted to authorized HR staff and legal counsel.

### 3.2 Retention Schedule

Employee records are retained as follows:

| Record Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| I-9 Forms | 3 years from hire or 1 year post-separation, whichever is longer |
| Performance Reviews | 7 years post-separation |
| Corrective Action Documents | 7 years post-separation |
| Benefits Enrollment Forms | Duration of employment + 6 years |
| Payroll Records | 7 years |
| Accommodation Requests | Duration of employment + 7 years |

Records scheduled for destruction must be approved by the HR Director and disposed of via certified shredding vendor.

## 4. Leave Request Workflow

### 4.1 Vacation and Personal Leave

Employees submit leave requests through the HR portal at least five business days in advance for planned absences. Requests are routed to the direct manager for approval or denial within two business days. Approved requests are automatically recorded in the timekeeping system.

### 4.2 Sick Leave

Unplanned sick leave is reported to the direct manager by telephone or message before the start of the scheduled shift. Documentation (medical certification) may be required for absences exceeding three consecutive business days. HR coordinates FMLA designation where applicable.

### 4.3 Leave of Absence (Non-FMLA)

Employees requesting a personal leave of absence beyond accrued PTO must submit form HR-LOA-02, accompanied by a written explanation of the reason for the request and the anticipated duration. Requests are reviewed by the HR Director in consultation with the department head. Approval is not guaranteed.

## 5. Offboarding Procedures

### 5.1 Voluntary Resignation

Upon receipt of a resignation notice, the HR coordinator must:

1. Acknowledge the resignation in writing within one business day.
2. Confirm the employee's last day and final paycheck date.
3. Schedule an exit interview (optional for the employee).
4. Initiate IT access revocation effective at the close of the final workday.
5. Collect all company property (badge, equipment, access cards) on or before the last day.
6. Process COBRA notification within the legally required timeframe.

### 5.2 Involuntary Termination

Involuntary terminations require written authorization from the department head and HR Director prior to execution. The termination meeting must include an HR representative. The employee receives a written separation notice documenting the effective date and final pay information. IT access is revoked at the time of the termination meeting.

---

## 6. Inter-Departmental Coordination

### 6.1 Headcount Approvals

All new positions and backfills require approval through the headcount request form (FIN-HC-01), routed through Finance and signed by the relevant Vice President before HR may initiate recruiting.

### 6.2 Compensation Adjustments

Merit increases, promotions, and off-cycle adjustments require completed form HR-COMP-03, approved by the department head and HR Director, and submitted to Payroll by the 15th of the month to take effect in the following pay cycle.
