# New Employee Onboarding Training Module

**Module ID:** TRN-ONB-01 | **Version:** 3.0 | **Effective:** January 15, 2025
**Audience:** All new full-time and part-time employees | **Duration:** Approximately 90 minutes

---

## Welcome to the Team

Congratulations on joining our organization. This onboarding module is designed to provide you with a foundational understanding of our workplace culture, key policies, and the resources available to support your success during your first weeks and months on the job.

Please read through each section carefully. At the end of this module you will be asked to sign an acknowledgment form confirming that you have reviewed and understood the content.

---

## Section 1: Our Culture and Values

### 1.1 Core Values

Our organization is guided by four core values that inform every aspect of how we work:

**Integrity** — We act honestly and transparently in all interactions, whether with colleagues, clients, or community partners. We hold ourselves accountable for our commitments and our mistakes.

**Respect** — We treat every individual with dignity, regardless of role, background, or tenure. Respectful communication is a baseline expectation, not an aspiration.

**Collaboration** — We believe our best work happens when we share knowledge, support one another, and work toward shared goals rather than siloed outcomes.

**Continuous Improvement** — We embrace feedback and view challenges as opportunities to learn. We expect every team member to be curious, adaptive, and open to change.

### 1.2 Communication Norms

Our workplace culture emphasizes direct, respectful communication. Employees are encouraged to raise questions and concerns with their managers or HR without fear of retaliation. The organization maintains an open-door policy at all levels of management.

## Section 2: Your First 30 Days

### 2.1 Immediate Priorities

During your first week, your primary priorities are:

- Complete all required HR paperwork (I-9, tax withholding, direct deposit, benefits enrollment).
- Attend your department orientation with your hiring manager.
- Set up and verify access to all required systems (email, HR portal, project management tools).
- Review the Employee Handbook and sign the acknowledgment form.
- Meet your assigned onboarding buddy — a peer colleague who will help you navigate day-to-day questions.

### 2.2 Benefits Enrollment Reminder

**Important:** Your window to enroll in medical, dental, vision, and other elective benefits is **30 calendar days** from your start date. Log in to the HR portal at hr.internal to review your options and make your elections before this window closes. Coverage elected during onboarding takes effect on the first day of the following month. If you miss the enrollment window, you must wait until annual open enrollment in November.

### 2.3 Probationary Period

Your first 90 days of employment are considered a probationary period. During this time, your manager will provide regular check-ins to discuss your progress and integration into the team. Successful completion of the probationary period is a natural milestone, not a formal evaluation event — you will continue to receive regular performance feedback after this period concludes.

## Section 3: Workplace Policies Overview

### 3.1 Attendance and Punctuality

Regular attendance is essential. Employees are expected to report to work on time for their scheduled shifts. If you are unable to report to work or will be late, contact your direct manager before your scheduled start time via telephone. Do not rely solely on email or messaging apps for same-day absence notifications.

Patterns of unexcused absence or tardiness may result in corrective action under the Attendance Policy (HR-ATTEND-01).

### 3.2 Remote Work and Hybrid Arrangements

Remote and hybrid work arrangements are available for eligible roles, as determined by the department head and HR. Employees with approved remote work arrangements are still expected to be available and responsive during core business hours (9:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time) and to attend required in-person meetings or events with reasonable advance notice.

Requests for remote work arrangements must be submitted through the HR portal and approved before the arrangement commences.

### 3.3 Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to conduct themselves in accordance with the Code of Conduct, available in full in the Employee Handbook (Chapter 2). Key provisions include:

- Prohibition on discrimination and harassment of any kind.
- Requirement to maintain confidentiality of proprietary and client information.
- Prohibition on conflicts of interest without prior written disclosure and approval.
- Responsible use of company systems, equipment, and resources.
- Zero tolerance for workplace violence or threats of violence.

Violations of the Code of Conduct are subject to corrective action up to and including termination, depending on the severity of the violation.

### 3.4 Reporting Concerns

Employees who observe or experience behavior that may violate company policy or applicable law are encouraged to report it promptly using one of the following channels:

- **Direct manager** (if the concern does not involve the manager).
- **HR Department** — available by email, phone, or in-person visit during business hours.
- **Anonymous Hotline** — available 24/7 for those who wish to report concerns without identifying themselves.

Retaliation against any employee who makes a good-faith report is strictly prohibited and is itself a violation of company policy.

## Section 4: Compensation and Benefits Summary

### 4.1 Pay Schedule

Employees are paid bi-weekly (every other Friday). Pay stubs are available electronically through the HR portal. Direct deposit is strongly encouraged and is the default for all new hires.

### 4.2 Leave Entitlements

New hires accrue paid time off (PTO) from their first day of employment at the following rates:

| Tenure | Accrual Rate |
|---|---|
| 0 – 2 years | 10 days per year (3.08 hours/pay period) |
| 2 – 5 years | 15 days per year (4.62 hours/pay period) |
| 5+ years | 20 days per year (6.15 hours/pay period) |

PTO may be used for vacation, personal days, or sick leave. A maximum balance of 1.5× the annual accrual rate may be carried over into the following calendar year; balances above this cap will be forfeited.

### 4.3 Additional Benefits

- **Health Insurance:** Medical, dental, and vision plans available. Employee-only coverage is fully employer-subsidized; dependent coverage is available at employee cost.
- **Retirement:** 401(k) plan with 4% employer match, vesting over three years.
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Confidential counseling, financial, and legal services at no cost to the employee.
- **Tuition Reimbursement:** Up to $5,000 per calendar year for approved programs. Application required in advance.

To complete this module, please sign and return the **Onboarding Acknowledgment Form (HR-ONB-ACK)** to your HR coordinator within five business days of your start date. Failure to return the signed acknowledgment may delay access to certain systems and benefits.

If you have questions about anything covered in this module, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR coordinator or your onboarding buddy.
