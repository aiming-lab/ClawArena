## Performance Rating Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 5 — Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all expectations; performance at this level is clearly distinguishable from peers |
| 4 — Exceeds Expectations | Frequently exceeds expectations; demonstrates strong results across most performance dimensions |
| 3 — Meets Expectations | Consistently meets expectations; performance is solid and reliable |
| 2 — Needs Improvement | Partially meets expectations; improvement is required in one or more areas |
| 1 — Unsatisfactory | Does not meet expectations in most areas; immediate corrective action required |
