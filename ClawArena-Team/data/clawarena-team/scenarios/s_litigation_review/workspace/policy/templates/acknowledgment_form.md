## Employee Acknowledgment Statement

I, the undersigned, acknowledge and confirm the following:

1. **Receipt:** I have received a copy of each document checked above.

2. **Review:** I have read (or have had an opportunity to read) each document listed above in full.

3. **Understanding:** I understand that compliance with the policies set forth in these documents is a condition of my employment.

4. **Questions:** I have had the opportunity to ask questions about any policy or provision I did not understand, and any questions I raised have been addressed to my satisfaction.

5. **Updates:** I understand that company policies may be updated from time to time and that it is my responsibility to review policy updates when they are communicated to employees. Updated versions of company policies are available on the HR portal.

6. **At-Will Statement** *(if applicable):* I understand that my employment is at-will, meaning either I or the company may end the employment relationship at any time, for any lawful reason, with or without advance notice. Nothing in any company policy or handbook constitutes a contract of employment or a guarantee of continued employment.
