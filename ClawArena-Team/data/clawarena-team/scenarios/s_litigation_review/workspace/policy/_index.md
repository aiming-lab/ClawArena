# Policy Library — Index

This directory consolidates the substantive policy materials governing the
employment relationship at Acme Corporation. Each entry below points to a folder
or file containing the operative text; large works are split into chapter or
article files so they can be read selectively.

| path | approx tokens | gist |
|---|---|---|
| `handbook_v3/` | ~14k | Internal company policy (Employee Handbook v3, effective 2025-01-01). 14 chapters; the binding 30-day PIP minimum lives in `ch05_pip.md` Section 5.2. |
| `labor_law_basic/` | ~10k | Statutory baseline — internal reference compendium of general labor law. 8 parts; the federal-baseline 30-day PIP minimum lives in `part05_performance_improvement.md` Section 5.3. |
| `admin_procedures.md` | ~1.5k | HR administrative procedures, intake forms, and routing rules. |
| `glossary.md` | ~1.5k | Defined terms used across the handbook and law compendium. |
| `precedents/` | ~3k | Internal case digests and prior arbitration outcomes. |
| `templates/` | ~2k | Form templates (PIP, notice of termination, accommodation request). |
| `training_modules/` | ~2k | HR training curriculum outlines. |

## Reading order for a PIP-duration question

1. `handbook_v3/ch05_pip.md` — internal minimum (Section 5.2).
2. `labor_law_basic/part05_performance_improvement.md` — statutory baseline (Section 5.3).
3. `precedents/` — apply any binding internal precedent.

If a question concerns events on or after March 1, 2026, also consult any
regulatory addendum that has been published after this index was last revised.
