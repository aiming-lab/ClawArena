# Labor Law Fundamentals — Reference Compendium
# Acme Corporation Legal Affairs Department — Internal Reference Only

This compendium summarizes the statutory baseline against which the Company's
internal policies in `handbook_v3/` are measured. Where the compendium and the
handbook diverge, the more protective rule applies. The federal-baseline
minimum PIP duration is set out in `part05_performance_improvement.md`,
Section 5.3.
