# Article V — IMPLEMENTATION AND EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS

## Section 5.1 — Affirmative Obligations

Employers subject to this Addendum must:

(a) update their written employment policies to reflect the forty-five (45) day
    minimum PIP duration within sixty (60) days of the effective date;
(b) train all supervisory and human resources personnel on the revised
    requirements within thirty (30) days of the effective date;
(c) audit any PIPs currently in progress or recently concluded to assess
    compliance with the revised minimum duration and with Article III
    documentation standards;
(d) retain records of all PIPs, including signed documents, mid-point review
    notes, and outcome determinations, for a minimum of seven (7) years.

## Section 5.2 — Notice to Employees

Within sixty (60) days of the effective date, employers must provide written
notice to all employees of (i) the revised minimum PIP duration, (ii) the
right of appeal under Article IV, and (iii) the contact information for
the Division. The notice may be provided by email, by intranet posting, or
in any other manner reasonably calculated to reach all affected employees.

## Section 5.3 — Designated Compliance Officer

Each employer with one hundred (100) or more employees must designate a
compliance officer responsible for the implementation of this Addendum and
must communicate the identity of the compliance officer to the Division
within ninety (90) days of the effective date.

## Section 5.4 — Reporting

Each employer must, on the anniversary of the effective date, file with the
Division a summary report stating (i) the number of PIPs initiated during the
preceding year, (ii) the average duration of those PIPs, (iii) the number of
PIPs that resulted in termination, and (iv) the number of internal appeals
received and their outcomes. The report shall be filed on the form prescribed
by the Division and shall not include personally identifying information.

## Section 5.5 — Sanctions

The Division may impose administrative penalties for non-compliance with this
Article. The amount of any penalty shall reflect the seriousness of the
non-compliance, the size of the employer, and any history of prior violations.
The Division may issue a public statement of non-compliance after notice and
opportunity to respond.
