# Labor Standards Addendum 2026 — Case Reference Index

*This index identifies illustrative case references on the application of the
revised minimum PIP duration. The references are organized by issue and are
intended as a starting point for legal research; they do not exhaust the
authority on any point.*

## 1. Minimum Duration — Termination Before Expiry

| Case | Issue | Holding |
|---|---|---|
| *Re Adams* (Div. 2026-04-12) | Termination on day 20 of a PIP initiated April 1, 2026. | Termination non-compliant with Article II Section 2.1; reinstatement and back pay ordered. |
| *Re Bertolino* (Div. 2026-05-03) | Termination on day 44 of a PIP initiated March 15, 2026. | One day short of the minimum; non-compliant; voidable at the employee's election. |
| *Re Chowdhury* (Div. 2026-06-18) | Termination on day 50 of a PIP initiated March 1, 2026. | Compliant with Article II; appeal dismissed. |

## 2. Documentation — Missing Mid-Point Review

| Case | Issue | Holding |
|---|---|---|
| *Re Diaz* (Div. 2026-07-09) | No mid-point review documented. | Material procedural defect under Article III Section 3.1; termination voidable. |
| *Re Eze* (Div. 2026-08-22) | Mid-point review documented but on day 30 of a 45-day PIP. | Substantially compliant; appeal dismissed. |

## 3. Retroactive Application

| Case | Issue | Holding |
|---|---|---|
| *Re Fontaine* (Div. 2026-09-14) | PIP initiated March 10, 2026; employer relied on handbook's 30-day rule. | Forty-five day minimum applies regardless of unaltered handbook; non-compliant. |
| *Re Goh* (Div. 2026-10-02) | PIP initiated February 25, 2026 (pre-effective date); not extended. | Prior 30-day minimum continues to apply under Article VI Section 6.2; compliant. |
| *Re Halim* (Div. 2026-11-19) | PIP initiated February 1, 2026; extended on March 20, 2026. | Extension governed by 45-day minimum under Article VI Section 6.3; non-compliant. |

## 4. Calculation of the PIP Period

| Case | Issue | Holding |
|---|---|---|
| *Re Iqbal* (Div. 2026-12-05) | Termination communicated on day 35, effective day 50. | Communication date governs under Article II Section 2.5; non-compliant. |
| *Re Jansen* (Div. 2027-01-11) | Employee declined to sign PIP document; deemed commencement date applied. | Deemed commencement date five business days after presentation valid under Article VII; appeal dismissed. |

## 5. Remedies

| Case | Issue | Holding |
|---|---|---|
| *Re Kovach* (Div. 2027-02-08) | Reinstatement ordered; employee declined to return. | Front pay ordered under Article IV Section 4.4(c); compensatory damages assessed. |
| *Re Lin* (Div. 2027-03-21) | Retaliatory adverse action following internal appeal. | Separate violation under Article IV Section 4.5; aggravated penalty imposed under Article V Section 5.5. |

## 6. Note on Citation

All case references in this index are issued by the Division of Employment
Standards in its adjudicative capacity. Decisions of the Division are
persuasive but not binding on courts of general jurisdiction. Where a court
has addressed the same issue, the judicial authority should be consulted.
