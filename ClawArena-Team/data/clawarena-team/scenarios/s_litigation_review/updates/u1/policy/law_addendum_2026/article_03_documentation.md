# Article III — MID-POINT REVIEW AND DOCUMENTATION

## Section 3.1 — Mandatory Mid-Point Review

All PIPs issued under this Addendum must include a formal written mid-point review
at approximately the midpoint of the PIP period (i.e., no later than the
twenty-third (23rd) day of a forty-five day PIP). The mid-point review must be
documented in writing and provided to the employee no later than two (2) business
days after the review meeting.

Failure to conduct and document the mid-point review constitutes a material
procedural defect that may render a subsequent adverse employment action voidable
at the election of the affected employee.

## Section 3.2 — Content of the Mid-Point Review

The mid-point review document must, at a minimum, address each of the following:

(a) the employee's progress against each stated PIP objective, with reference to
    the measurable success criteria recorded in the PIP document;
(b) any obstacles to progress identified by either the supervisor or the
    employee;
(c) any modification to the support to be provided to the employee, including
    additional training, coaching, mentoring, or resources;
(d) the supervisor's preliminary assessment of whether the employee is on
    track to satisfy the PIP objectives by the PIP closure date.

## Section 3.3 — Documentation of the PIP Itself

The PIP document must record (i) the specific performance deficiencies being
addressed, (ii) the measurable improvement objectives, (iii) the PIP
commencement date and PIP closure date, (iv) the support the employer
commits to provide, and (v) the consequences of failure to meet the
objectives. The document must be signed by the employee, the supervisor,
and a human resources representative.

## Section 3.4 — Retention

PIP documents, mid-point review records, and outcome determinations must be
retained for a minimum of seven (7) years from the date of issue. Retention
in machine-readable form is permitted provided the records are accessible
to the affected employee and to the Division on request.

## Section 3.5 — Evidentiary Effect

In any proceeding arising under this Addendum, the absence of contemporaneous
documentation that satisfies this Article is construed against the employer.
The employer may not rely on retroactively prepared documentation to
demonstrate compliance.
