# Article I — Purpose and Preamble

## Preamble

The Division of Employment Standards ("the Division"), having reviewed evidence
submitted by employee advocacy organizations, employer associations, and
independent labor economists, has determined that the existing minimum performance
improvement plan duration of thirty (30) calendar days is insufficient to afford
employees a meaningful remediation opportunity in the context of modern
performance management practices, which increasingly rely on multi-phase
evaluation frameworks.

The Division further notes that a thirty-day minimum period does not adequately
account for the time required for employees to receive, understand, and act upon
performance feedback, particularly in roles involving complex technical,
analytical, or client-facing responsibilities.

Accordingly, the Division hereby promulgates this Addendum, which supersedes all
prior guidance on the minimum PIP duration for full-time employees.

## 1.1 Statement of Purpose

The purpose of this Addendum is to (a) establish a revised regulatory floor for
the minimum duration of a performance improvement plan issued to a full-time
employee, (b) prescribe procedural requirements for mid-point review and
documentation, and (c) set out the implementation obligations of employers
subject to the regulatory regime.

## 1.2 Authority

This Addendum is issued under the authority of the General Labor Standards
enabling provisions and is binding on all employers subject to those Standards.

## 1.3 Relationship to Prior Guidance

To the extent that any prior regulatory guidance, advisory opinion, or industry
practice is inconsistent with this Addendum, this Addendum prevails. All
inconsistent prior guidance is hereby withdrawn as of the effective date.

## 1.4 Effective Date and Retroactive Applicability

This Addendum is effective March 1, 2026, and applies retroactively to all
PIPs initiated on or after that date. The specific application of the
retroactive rule to PIPs in progress at the effective date is governed by
Article VI (Transition).
