# Article VI — TRANSITION

## Section 6.1 — General Principle

This Article governs the application of the Addendum to PIPs in progress at,
and to adverse employment actions taken near, the effective date of
March 1, 2026.

## Section 6.2 — PIPs Initiated Before the Effective Date

A PIP that was formally initiated — by execution of the PIP document by both
the employer and the employee — before March 1, 2026 continues to be
governed, for the duration of that specific PIP, by the prior thirty (30) day
minimum. The mid-point review and documentation requirements of Article III
do not apply retroactively to such PIPs, but employers are encouraged to
comply with them as a matter of best practice.

## Section 6.3 — Extension or Re-Issue

Where a PIP that was initiated before March 1, 2026 is extended on or after
March 1, 2026, or where a new PIP is issued to the same employee on or after
March 1, 2026, the extended or new PIP is governed by the forty-five (45)
day minimum and by Article III in full.

## Section 6.4 — PIPs Initiated On or After the Effective Date

A PIP that was formally initiated on or after March 1, 2026 is governed by
the forty-five (45) day minimum and by Article III in full, regardless of
whether the employer's handbook or written policies have been formally
updated. This rule applies to PIPs that are the subject of pending
employment disputes initiated before the publication of this Addendum.

## Section 6.5 — Adverse Actions Taken Before Publication

Where an adverse employment action was taken on or after March 1, 2026 but
before the publication of this Addendum, the affected employee has the right
to invoke this Addendum on appeal under Article IV. The limitation period
under Article IV Section 4.6 runs from the date of publication of the
Addendum, not from the date of the adverse action, for actions falling
within this Section.

## Section 6.6 — Conflicts with Existing Handbooks

Where an employer's handbook specifies a minimum PIP duration of fewer than
forty-five (45) days, the handbook is deemed amended by operation of this
Addendum. The employer is nevertheless required by Article V Section 5.1(a)
to formally update the handbook within sixty (60) days, and failure to do
so is a separate violation.

## Section 6.7 — Severability of Transition Rules

If any provision of this Article is held to be unenforceable, the remaining
provisions of this Article shall continue in effect, and the unenforceable
provision shall be construed, to the extent possible, to give effect to the
purposes of this Addendum.
