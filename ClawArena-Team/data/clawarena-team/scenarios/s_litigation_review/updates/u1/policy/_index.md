# Policy Library — Index (updated 2026-03)

This directory consolidates the substantive policy materials governing the
employment relationship at Acme Corporation. Each entry below points to a folder
or file containing the operative text; large works are split into chapter or
article files so they can be read selectively. This index supersedes the prior
workspace index following the publication of the Labor Standards Addendum 2026.

| path | approx tokens | gist |
|---|---|---|

| `labor_law_basic/` | ~10k | Statutory baseline — internal reference compendium of general labor law. 8 parts; the prior 30-day baseline lives in `part05_performance_improvement.md` Section 5.3. **Now superseded for full-time employees by the 45-day floor in `law_addendum_2026/article_02_pip_duration.md` Section 2.1.** |

## Reading order for a PIP-duration question

1. `law_addendum_2026/article_02_pip_duration.md` — binding regulatory floor (Section 2.1, effective 2026-03-01, 45 days).
2. `handbook_v3/ch05_pip.md` — internal minimum (Section 5.2, 30 days; superseded for post-2026-03-01 PIPs).
3. `labor_law_basic/part05_performance_improvement.md` — statutory background (Section 5.3).
4. `precedents/` and `law_addendum_2026/case_reference_index.md` — applied authority.

For PIPs initiated **on or after March 1, 2026**, the 45-day minimum in the
Addendum controls regardless of any contrary handbook provision. For PIPs
initiated before March 1, 2026, see `law_addendum_2026/article_06_transition.md`.
