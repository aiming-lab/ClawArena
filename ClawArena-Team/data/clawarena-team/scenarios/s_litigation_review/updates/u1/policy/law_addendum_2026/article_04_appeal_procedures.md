# Article IV — APPEAL PROCEDURES

## Section 4.1 — Right of Appeal

An employee who is subject to an adverse employment action that the employee
contends was taken in contravention of this Addendum — including, but not
limited to, a termination taken before the expiry of the forty-five (45) day
minimum PIP duration under Article II Section 2.1 — has the right to appeal.

## Section 4.2 — Internal Appeal

In the first instance, the appeal shall be made to the employer in writing
within thirty (30) calendar days of the adverse employment action. The
employer must acknowledge the appeal within five (5) business days and must
issue a written decision within thirty (30) calendar days of acknowledgement.

## Section 4.3 — Division Review

Where the internal appeal is unsuccessful, or where the employer fails to
acknowledge or decide the appeal within the periods prescribed by Section
4.2, the employee may file a complaint with the Division. The Division has
jurisdiction to investigate the complaint, to compel production of the PIP
documentation, and to issue orders giving effect to this Addendum.

## Section 4.4 — Remedies

Where the Division or a court of competent jurisdiction finds that an adverse
employment action contravened this Addendum, the available remedies include:

(a) reinstatement to the former position;
(b) back pay from the date of the adverse action to the date of reinstatement
    or, if reinstatement is not ordered, the date of the final order;
(c) front pay in lieu of reinstatement where reinstatement is not feasible;
(d) compensatory damages for any consequential loss;
(e) an order expunging the PIP from the employee's personnel file;
(f) such other and further relief as the adjudicator deems appropriate.

## Section 4.5 — Non-Retaliation

No employer shall take any adverse action against an employee for exercising
the right of appeal under this Article, or for cooperating with a Division
investigation. Retaliation is itself a violation of this Addendum and gives
rise to the remedies described in Section 4.4.

## Section 4.6 — Limitation Period

A complaint to the Division must be filed within one (1) year of the
adverse employment action. The limitation period is tolled during the
pendency of an internal appeal.
