# Labor Standards Addendum 2026 — Transition Guidance

*This document is non-binding implementation guidance prepared by the
Division for HR and Legal Affairs personnel. The operative transition rules
are set out in `article_06_transition.md`.*

## 1. First Sixty Days After the Effective Date

Within the first sixty (60) days after March 1, 2026, employers should
complete the following actions:

(a) circulate to all supervisors a written notice of the revised forty-five
    (45) day minimum PIP duration, including a plain-language summary of
    Article III mid-point review requirements;
(b) inventory all PIPs currently in progress, classify each as
    pre-March-1-2026 or post-March-1-2026, and confirm that the applicable
    minimum duration is correctly set;
(c) update the standard PIP template to (i) record the PIP commencement date
    and PIP closure date explicitly, (ii) include a mid-point review
    schedule, and (iii) reference the forty-five day minimum;
(d) provide notice to all employees under Article V Section 5.2.

## 2. PIPs in Progress at the Effective Date

For PIPs that were formally initiated before March 1, 2026, the prior
thirty (30) day minimum continues to apply, but employers are encouraged to:

(a) confirm in writing to the affected employee that the prior minimum
    applies for the duration of the current PIP;
(b) where the PIP has not yet reached its mid-point, schedule a mid-point
    review consistent with Article III, even though the requirement does not
    formally apply retroactively;
(c) if the PIP is subsequently extended or re-issued, apply the new
    forty-five day minimum and Article III in full to the extended or new
    PIP.

## 3. Adverse Actions Taken in the Pre-Publication Window

For adverse employment actions taken on or after March 1, 2026 but before
the publication of the Addendum, employers should:

(a) review each such action against the requirements of Article II and
    Article III;
(b) where the action is non-compliant, consider voluntary remedial action
    — including reinstatement, back pay, and rescission of any termination
    decision — to mitigate the risk of an enforcement order;
(c) document the review and any remedial action taken, and retain the
    documentation with the relevant personnel file.

## 4. Handbook Updates

Employers should update the relevant chapter of the employee handbook to
state the revised forty-five day minimum. The updated chapter should be
issued under a clear effective date and the prior version should be
preserved in the document control system, both for internal audit
purposes and to evidence the date of the update.

## 5. Training

All supervisors, HR business partners, and Legal Affairs personnel involved
in PIP issuance and administration should receive training on the Addendum
within thirty (30) days of the effective date. The training should cover, at
minimum, (i) the revised minimum duration, (ii) the Article III mid-point
review and documentation requirements, (iii) the Article IV appeal procedure,
and (iv) the consequences of non-compliance under Article II Section 2.6.

## 6. Audit

Employers are encouraged to conduct an audit of PIP practice — including a
review of randomly selected closed PIPs — within six (6) months of the
effective date. The audit should assess compliance with this Addendum and
identify any systematic shortcomings warranting remedial action.
