# Labor Standards Addendum 2026 — Index
# Issued by: Division of Employment Standards, Department of Labor
# Reference: Addendum No. 2026-DES-004
# Effective: March 1, 2026 (retroactive)

> **IMPORTANT — RETROACTIVE APPLICABILITY**
> This addendum takes effect retroactively from March 1, 2026. All employment
> actions, including performance improvement plan initiations and termination
> decisions, that occurred on or after March 1, 2026 must comply with the
> provisions set forth herein, regardless of whether the action predated the
> formal publication of this addendum.
