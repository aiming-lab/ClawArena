# Article II — REVISED MINIMUM PIP DURATION

## Section 2.1 — Amended Minimum Duration for Full-Time Employees

**Effective March 1, 2026, the minimum Performance Improvement Plan duration for
any full-time employee shall be FORTY-FIVE (45) calendar days.**

This figure supersedes and replaces all prior regulatory guidance, including the
thirty (30) day minimum set forth in the General Labor Standards (base edition)
and any company handbook provisions that specified a shorter minimum duration.

Employers whose existing written policies specify a minimum PIP duration of fewer
than forty-five (45) days must update those policies within sixty (60) days of
the effective date of this Addendum and must apply the forty-five day minimum to
all PIPs initiated on or after March 1, 2026, regardless of whether the
employer's written policy has been formally updated.

## Section 2.2 — Part-Time Employees

The minimum PIP duration for part-time employees (fewer than thirty-five hours
per week) is amended to TWENTY-TWO (22) calendar days, effective March 1, 2026.

## Section 2.3 — Probationary Employees

No minimum PIP duration applies to employees within their first ninety (90) days
of employment; however, even during the probationary period, employers are
encouraged to provide a minimum of fourteen (14) days' written notice of
performance concerns before termination.

## Section 2.4 — Non-Retroactive Application to Pre-Existing PIPs

For PIPs that were formally initiated (i.e., signed by both parties) before
March 1, 2026, the prior thirty (30) day minimum continues to apply for the
duration of that specific PIP. However, if the PIP is extended or a new PIP is
issued after March 1, 2026, the forty-five (45) day minimum applies to the
extended or new PIP.

For PIPs initiated on or after March 1, 2026 — including the PIP that is the
subject of any currently pending employment dispute — the forty-five (45) day
minimum applies in full, regardless of whether the employer's handbook has been
updated to reflect this change.

## Section 2.5 — Calculation of the PIP Period

The forty-five (45) day period runs from the PIP commencement date (as defined
in Article VII) to the date on which the employer first takes, or first
communicates an intention to take, adverse employment action predicated on the
employee's failure to meet the PIP objectives. A termination decision
communicated to the employee before the expiry of the forty-five day period is
non-compliant with this Article irrespective of the calendar date on which the
termination is given effect.

## Section 2.6 — Consequences of Non-Compliance

A termination, demotion, or other adverse employment action that contravenes
this Article is voidable at the election of the affected employee under the
appeal procedure set out in Article IV. The available remedies include
reinstatement, back pay, and any other relief that the adjudicator considers
necessary to give effect to this Addendum.

## Section 2.7 — Floor, Not Ceiling

The minimum durations established by this Article are floors. Employers
remain free to specify, by contract or by handbook, longer minimum durations,
and a longer duration so specified is enforceable against the employer as a
matter of implied contract.
