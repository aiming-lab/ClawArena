# Article VII — DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Addendum, the following defined terms have the
meanings set out below. Defined terms appear in this document in **bold** on
first introduction.

## 7.1 Core Definitions

**"Full-time employee"** means any individual employed under a contract of service
for a minimum of thirty-five (35) hours per week on an ongoing basis.

**"Part-time employee"** means any individual employed under a contract of
service for fewer than thirty-five (35) hours per week on an ongoing basis.

**"Probationary employee"** means an employee within the first ninety (90)
days of employment.

**"Performance Improvement Plan" (PIP)** means any formal written plan issued by
an employer to an employee that identifies specific performance deficiencies and
establishes measurable improvement objectives, a defined remediation period, and
consequences for failure to meet those objectives.

**"Minimum PIP Duration"** means the shortest permissible period from the date on
which a PIP is formally initiated (as evidenced by the employee's signed
acknowledgement of the PIP document) to the date on which the employer may take
adverse employment action based on the employee's failure to meet PIP objectives.

**"PIP commencement date"** means the date on which the employee signs the PIP
document acknowledging its terms, or, where the employee declines or
unreasonably delays signing, a deemed date no earlier than five (5) business
days after the PIP document was first presented to the employee in writing.

**"PIP closure date"** means the date on which the PIP is formally closed by
the employer following a written determination of outcome.

**"Adverse employment action"** includes termination of employment, involuntary
demotion, involuntary transfer, or any other material reduction in the terms and
conditions of employment.

## 7.2 Procedural Definitions

**"Mid-point review"** means the formal written review required by Article III
Section 3.1 at approximately the midpoint of the PIP period.

**"Outcome determination"** means the written determination, at the close of
the PIP period, that the employee has (i) successfully completed the PIP,
(ii) made partial progress warranting extension, or (iii) failed to meet the
PIP objectives.

## 7.3 Institutional Definitions

**"The Division"** means the Division of Employment Standards.

**"Compliance officer"** means the individual designated by an employer under
Article V Section 5.3 as responsible for implementation of this Addendum.

## 7.4 General Rules of Interpretation

References to "days" mean calendar days unless expressly stated otherwise.
The singular includes the plural, and the masculine includes the feminine
and gender-neutral. Headings are provided for convenience only and do not
affect the interpretation of the provisions to which they relate.
