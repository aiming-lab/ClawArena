# Legal Hold Management Policy — Mercator Robotics

This policy governs the identification, notification, and management of
litigation holds at Mercator Robotics. It applies to all personnel who
may be designated as custodians in connection with legal proceedings,
regulatory investigations, or disputes involving Mercator Robotics.

## Scope

All electronically stored information (ESI) and physical documents that
may be relevant to actual or reasonably anticipated litigation must be
preserved under a legal hold notice issued by the General Counsel's office.

## Custodian Responsibilities

Custodians must: preserve all potentially relevant documents; suspend
auto-delete policies for covered materials; notify the GC of any new
relevant materials discovered after hold issuance; and report any
accidental destruction immediately.

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with a legal hold may result in: adverse inference
instructions in litigation; sanctions; and/or disciplinary action.
