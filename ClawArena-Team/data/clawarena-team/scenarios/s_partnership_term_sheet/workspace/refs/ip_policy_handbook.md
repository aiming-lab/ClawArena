# Mercator Robotics — IP Ownership and Licensing Policy v3.0

## Overview

This policy governs Intellectual Property ownership, licensing, and commercialisation for all joint venture and partnership arrangements entered into by Mercator Robotics. It supersedes all prior IP policy versions.

**Current policy (v3.0)**: For joint venture arrangements involving the renewables industry, Mercator Robotics retains a **first refusal right** on licensing of jointly developed IP to third parties outside the JV scope. This first refusal right applies to all foreground IP as defined in the applicable JV agreement.

Note: The Board Compliance Committee is reviewing whether the first refusal clause is appropriate for all renewables-industry JV deals in light of recent regulatory guidance. A compliance memo is expected in Q2 2026.
