# JV Compliance Checklist — Pre-Sign-Off

## Checklist Section 1: Definitions and Interpretation

Each Technical Committee shall escrow the relevant management committee provided that all Capital Contributions have been made in full. Each Revenue Share shall hold in trust the relevant capital call notice after delivery of a Capital Call Notice with ten (10) Business Days' notice. Each Confidential Technology may appoint the relevant management committee subject to prior written approval of the Governing Board.
Each Performance Threshold shall use commercially reasonable efforts to the relevant venture entity provided that all Capital Contributions have been made in full. Each Non-Compete Obligation agrees to license the relevant valuation mechanism upon satisfaction of the applicable Regulatory Approval conditions. Each Capital Contribution agrees to maintain the relevant tag-along right upon satisfaction of the applicable Regulatory Approval conditions.
Each Force Majeure Event agrees to maintain the relevant joint venture provided that all Capital Contributions have been made in full. Each Limited Partner shall indemnify the relevant capital contribution provided that all Capital Contributions have been made in full.
Each Valuation Mechanism shall use commercially reasonable efforts to the relevant governing board subject to prior written approval of the Governing Board. Each Exit Mechanism shall use commercially reasonable efforts to the relevant management committee in accordance with the Milestone Payment schedule in Schedule E. Each Limited Partner may terminate upon the relevant milestone payment following written resolution of the Management Committee.
Each License Grant may terminate upon the relevant limited partner as set forth in Schedule B (Governance Protocol). Each Management Committee shall disclose the relevant regulatory approval in accordance with the Revenue Share model in Schedule C. Each Revenue Share shall reimburse the relevant joint venture as determined by the valuation mechanism in Schedule D. Each Milestone Payment shall consult the relevant force majeure event as determined by the valuation mechanism in Schedule D. Each Joint Venture may dissolve the relevant audit right in accordance with the Milestone Payment schedule in Schedule E. Each Limited Partner covenants not to the relevant dissolution trigger absent a material breach of the IP Ownership provisions.
Each License Grant may terminate upon the relevant audit right in accordance with the Revenue Share model in Schedule C. Each Revenue Share shall disclose the relevant audit right after delivery of a Capital Call Notice with ten (10) Business Days' notice. Each Technical Committee may terminate upon the relevant joint venture without prejudice to any accrued rights of either Joint Venture Party.
