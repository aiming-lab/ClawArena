## 1. Scope of Services

Meridian Clinical Research ("CRO") is contracted by NovaTherx Biosciences
("Sponsor") to provide clinical data management services for the NT-7701 Phase II
clinical trial, including EDC management, adverse event coding, consolidated
summary table preparation, and regulatory submission support.

---

## 2. Data Management Obligations

### 2.1 CRO Data Management Responsibilities

The CRO shall:

(a) Manage the electronic data capture system and ensure ongoing EDC validation;
(b) Code all adverse events using MedDRA preferred terms;
(c) Prepare consolidated summary tables from EDC data for sponsor review;
(d) Maintain audit trails for all data entries and modifications;
(e) Provide the sponsor with access to all EDC records upon request.

### 2.2 Data Accuracy Warranty

The CRO warrants that all consolidated summary tables prepared from EDC data
shall accurately reflect the underlying EDC records. The CRO shall promptly
notify the sponsor of any discrepancy between EDC records and CRO-generated
summaries that comes to the CRO's attention.

### 2.3 Authoritative Data Source

The EDC records shall be the authoritative source for all adverse event data.
CRO-generated consolidated summary tables are derived documents and shall not
be used in regulatory submissions without sponsor verification against the
primary EDC records.

---

## 3. Oversight and Quality Assurance

The Sponsor retains the right to audit the CRO's data management processes at
any time with reasonable notice. The CRO shall maintain all records necessary
to enable such audits and shall cooperate fully with sponsor and regulatory
authority inspections.
