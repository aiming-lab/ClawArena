# Reference: EMA/121059/2012 (synthesised for internal reference)
# Reproduced for internal reference — NovaTherx Regulatory Affairs

> This document is a synthesised reference document for training and
> background purposes. It is not an official EMA publication.

---

## 1. Introduction

The European Medicines Agency's guidance on sponsor oversight of clinical trials
and contracted research organisations establishes minimum standards for the
quality oversight of clinical trial data generated in EMA-regulated territories.
These standards apply to the NT-7701 Phase II trial insofar as investigator sites
in Berlin, Germany are subject to EMA oversight.

---

## 2. Sponsor Responsibilities

The sponsor of a clinical trial is ultimately responsible for the quality and
integrity of all trial data, regardless of the extent to which data management
and reporting functions have been contracted to a CRO. Delegation of functions
does not transfer the sponsor's legal and regulatory responsibility.

---

## 3. CRO Oversight Requirements

Sponsors must maintain documented oversight procedures for all functions delegated
to a CRO. These procedures must include:

(a) Quality assurance audits of CRO data management processes;
(b) Verification of CRO-generated summary tables against primary EDC data;
(c) Documented reconciliation procedures for any discrepancies identified;
(d) Clear contractual provisions establishing data quality standards.
