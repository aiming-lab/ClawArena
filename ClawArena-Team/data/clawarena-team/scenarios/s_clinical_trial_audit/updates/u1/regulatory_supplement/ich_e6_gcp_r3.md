## 1. Introduction

ICH E6(R3), the Good Clinical Practice Integrated Addendum, provides consolidated
guidance on the design, conduct, performance, monitoring, auditing, recording,
analysis, and reporting of clinical trials. Compliance with GCP provides public
assurance that the rights, safety, and wellbeing of trial participants are protected
and that clinical trial data are credible.

---

## 2. Glossary of Key Terms

**Adverse Event (AE):** Any untoward medical occurrence in a subject administered
an investigational product.

**Serious Adverse Event (SAE):** An adverse event that results in death, is
life-threatening, requires inpatient hospitalisation or prolongation of existing
hospitalisation, results in persistent or significant disability/incapacity,
is a congenital anomaly/birth defect, or is a medically important event.

**Case Report Form (CRF):** A printed, optical, or electronic document designed
to record all required information on each trial subject.

**Electronic Data Capture (EDC):** A validated computerised system for the
collection of clinical trial data.

**Contract Research Organisation (CRO):** A person or an organisation (commercial,
academic, or other) contracted by the sponsor to perform one or more sponsor's
trial-related duties and functions.

---

## 3. Sponsor Responsibilities

### 3.1 Quality Assurance and Quality Control

The sponsor is responsible for implementing and maintaining quality assurance (QA)
and quality control (QC) systems with written SOPs to ensure that trials are
conducted and data are generated, documented, and reported in compliance with the
protocol, GCP, and applicable regulatory requirements.

### 3.2 CRO Oversight

The sponsor may transfer any or all of the sponsor's trial-related duties and
functions to a CRO. However, the ultimate responsibility for the quality and
integrity of the trial data always resides with the sponsor. The sponsor must
ensure oversight of any trial-related duties and functions carried out by a CRO.

### 3.3 Investigational Product Accountability

The sponsor is responsible for providing investigational product to investigators
and maintaining accountability records for all investigational product.

---

## 4. Investigator Responsibilities

### 4.1 Medical Care of Trial Subjects

The investigator is responsible for all trial-related medical decisions. The
investigator's assessment of adverse event grade (per CTCAE or other applicable
scale) is the primary clinical determination. Administrative reclassification
of adverse events by CRO data management teams does not supersede the treating
physician's documented clinical assessment.

### 4.2 Adverse Event Reporting

All serious adverse events must be reported promptly to the sponsor. The
investigator must report SAEs within the timeframes specified in the protocol,
which must comply with applicable regulatory requirements.
