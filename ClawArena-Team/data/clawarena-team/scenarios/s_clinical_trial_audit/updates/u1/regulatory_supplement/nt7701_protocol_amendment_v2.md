## 2. Background and Rationale

NT-7701 is a novel, selective kinase inhibitor with demonstrated anti-tumour
activity in preclinical models. The Phase II study is designed to evaluate the
efficacy and safety of NT-7701 in adult patients with advanced solid tumours
who have received at least one prior line of systemic therapy.

Protocol Amendment v2.1 modifies the SAE electronic reporting timelines at
investigator sites. The amendment reduces the window for site-level electronic
confirmation of SAE onset from 72 hours to 48 hours, in response to the data
monitoring committee's recommendation following the interim safety analysis.

The amendment does not alter the CTCAE Grade classification criteria, the
SAE definition, or the overall study design.

---

## 3. Study Objectives

### 3.1 Primary Objective

To evaluate the objective response rate (ORR) of NT-7701 compared to placebo
in patients with advanced solid tumours at 24 weeks.

### 3.2 Secondary Objectives

- To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS).
- To characterise the safety and tolerability of NT-7701, including the incidence
  and severity of adverse events per CTCAE v5.0.
- To assess patient-reported outcomes (PRO) using validated instruments.

## 5. Amendment v2.1 — Specific Changes

### 5.1 SAE Electronic Reporting Timeline (Modified)

**Previous text (Protocol v2.0, Section 8.3.1):**
"The site must electronically confirm SAE onset in the EDC within 72 hours
of the investigator becoming aware of the event."

**New text (Protocol v2.1, Section 8.3.1):**
"The site must electronically confirm SAE onset in the EDC within 48 hours
of the investigator becoming aware of the event. This change is effective
immediately upon receipt of IRB approval (IRB-2026-NT7701-003)."

### 5.2 Rationale for Amendment

The data monitoring committee's interim safety review recommended tightening
the SAE reporting window to improve pharmacovigilance responsiveness, particularly
for Grade-3 and Grade-4 events. The 48-hour timeline is consistent with current
ICH E2A recommendations and with the sponsor's revised pharmacovigilance SOP.
