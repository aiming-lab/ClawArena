## Preamble

I am submitting this formal allegation memorandum in my capacity as a former
data-entry contractor who worked on the NT-7701 Phase II clinical trial from
October 2025 through March 2026. During my engagement, I had access to both the
raw electronic data capture (EDC) export files and the CRO's consolidated summary
tables. The discrepancies I observed during that period form the basis of this
allegation.

I am providing this memorandum in addition to the initial email complaint submitted
on the same date. This memorandum contains greater detail regarding the specific
nature of the data integrity concerns and the corroborating evidence I have retained.

---

## Allegation 1 — Systematic Grade-3 SAE Under-Reporting

### 1.1 Nature of the Discrepancy

During my work on the study, I conducted a manual comparison of the Grade-3 serious
adverse event (SAE) counts as they appeared in the raw EDC export files for each
of the five investigator sites against the corresponding figures in the CRO's
consolidated SAE summary table. In every case, the CRO's summary table reported
fewer Grade-3 SAEs than the EDC records showed.

Based on my analysis, the discrepancy is consistent and systematic across all
five sites. It is not attributable to isolated coding errors or individual data
entry mistakes. The pattern strongly suggests a systematic process in the CRO's
data consolidation workflow that results in the exclusion of certain Grade-3 SAE
records from the summary table.

### 1.2 Verbatim Allegation Statement

I hereby formally allege as follows:

**"The CRO summary table systematically omits two Grade-3 serious adverse events per site, resulting in a total under-report of ten events across the five-site study."**

This allegation is supported by the cross-reference data I have retained and
provided in the accompanying supporting data notes document.

### 1.3 Supporting Evidence

The supporting data notes document (`supporting_data_notes.md`) cross-references
specific EDC subject IDs and row numbers where Grade-3 SAEs appear in the EDC but
are absent from the CRO's consolidated summary. I have independently verified
these cross-references against the EDC export files I had access to during my
engagement.

### 1.4 Potential Regulatory Implications

If the Grade-3 SAE under-count is confirmed, the corrected total across all five
sites would be materially higher than the figure reported in the CRO's consolidated
table. The under-counted events may affect the sponsor's regulatory reporting
obligations under 21 CFR 312.32 and ICH E2A, depending on whether any of the
under-counted events are unexpected serious adverse drug reactions.

## Allegation 2 — IRB Meeting Minutes Date Discrepancy

### 2.1 Nature of the Discrepancy

The scanned IRB meeting minutes document for the meeting at which Protocol
Amendment v2.1 was approved contains a date in the document header that appears
to be a month/day transposition. The date as rendered on the document does not
correspond to the actual meeting date when cross-referenced with the IRB approval
certificate issued for the same approval event.

I was not able to reproduce the exact text of the erroneous date from memory, but
I am confident that the discrepancy is visually apparent upon inspection of the
scanned document image. The IRB approval certificate should be used to establish
the correct meeting date.

The IRB approval number for this approval event is IRB-2026-NT7701-003. The IRB
chairperson is Dr. Renata Okonkwo (IRB_CHAIR).

## Allegation 3 — Retention of Superseded Draft on Shared Drive

A version 1 DRAFT of the integrated safety summary was formally withdrawn and
superseded on 2026-04-10. As of the date of my departure from the project,
this withdrawn draft remained on the shared drive in a location where it could
be accessed by audit team members and potentially mistaken for the final version.

The figures in the v1 draft are incorrect. In particular, the Grade-3 SAE total
shown in the withdrawn draft is the same as the CRO's under-counted figure, not
the correct EDC-based total. If any audit deliverable or regulatory submission
relies on figures from the withdrawn draft, the resulting submission will be
erroneous.

I have included the withdrawn draft in this packet for reference only, clearly
labelled as superseded. It must not be used for any audit purpose.
