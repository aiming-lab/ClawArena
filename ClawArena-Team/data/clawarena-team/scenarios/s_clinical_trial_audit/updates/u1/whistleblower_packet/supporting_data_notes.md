## Introduction

These notes provide cross-references between specific EDC subject IDs and the
Grade-3 SAE entries that appear in the EDC export but are absent from the CRO's
consolidated summary table. The cross-references are based on my review of the
EDC export files during my engagement on the project.

I have verified each cross-reference to the best of my ability given my access
at the time. The audit team should independently verify these references against
the current EDC export files.

---

## Site 01 (Bangalore) — Missing Grade-3 SAEs

**CRO reported:** 12 Grade-3 SAEs
**EDC records showed (at time of my review):** 14 Grade-3 SAEs
**Discrepancy:** 2 events

Cross-reference notes:
- The two Grade-3 SAEs absent from the CRO summary at Site 01 appear to be events
  coded in the EDC under subject IDs in the BLR series. The ae_terms for the absent
  events were consistent with Grade-3 AEs commonly reported for kinase inhibitors
  (pulmonary and haematological events).
- Both events had `sae_flag = "true"` and `grade = 3` in the EDC records.

---

## Site 02 (Berlin) — Missing Grade-3 SAEs

**CRO reported:** 10 Grade-3 SAEs
**EDC records showed (at time of my review):** 12 Grade-3 SAEs
**Discrepancy:** 2 events

Cross-reference notes:
- Prof. Braun (Site 02 PI) separately communicated to the sponsor in April 2026 that
  she counted 12 Grade-3 events at her site, consistent with the EDC records.
- The two absent events appear to involve subjects in the BER series with hepatic
  and renal Grade-3 AEs.

---

## Site 03 (Toronto) — Missing Grade-3 SAEs

**CRO reported:** 9 Grade-3 SAEs
**EDC records showed (at time of my review):** 11 Grade-3 SAEs
**Discrepancy:** 2 events

Cross-reference notes:
- The CRO downgraded three "Elevated liver enzymes" events from Grade 3 to Grade 2.
  However, the treating physician's EDC entries for subjects TOR-0112, TOR-0189, and
  TOR-0247 clearly record Grade 3, based on hospitalisation in each case.
- The query resolution (documented in `email_site_queries.md`) confirms Grade 3.
- If the three "Elevated liver enzymes" events are correctly counted as Grade 3,
  Site 03's count is 11 (not 8 or 9). The discrepancy of 2 from the CRO's reported
  9 is consistent with the broader pattern.
