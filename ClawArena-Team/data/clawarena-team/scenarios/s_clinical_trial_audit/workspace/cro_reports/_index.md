## Methodology Note

The CRO's consolidated SAE summary (`cro_sae_summary.csv`) was generated by the
CRO's data management team from the EDC export data. The CRO applied their
internal adverse event coding procedures before aggregating site-level counts.
**Important:** The CRO's figures should be cross-checked against the raw EDC
export before use in any regulatory submission. The CRO's data freeze reference
date is 2026-04-16, which is one day after the final site locked their data.

Known limitation: The CRO's summary reflects their internal coding conventions,
which may differ from the treating physician's recorded grade in the EDC for
certain borderline events. Sponsors are advised to use the EDC as the authoritative
source per the data hierarchy established in the Master Services Agreement.
