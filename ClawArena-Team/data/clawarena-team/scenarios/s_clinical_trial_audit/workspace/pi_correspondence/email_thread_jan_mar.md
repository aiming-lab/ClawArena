## Email 7

**From:** Dr. Rajan Krishnamurthy <r.krishnamurthy@blr-oncology.in> (Site 01 PI)
**To:** marcus.yuen@novatherx.com
**Date:** 2026-02-23
**Subject:** All Sites — Reminder: SAE Reporting Window and Data Entry Deadlines

Under 21 CFR 312.32, the sponsor is required to report serious and unexpected adverse drug reactions to FDA within 15 calendar days (7 calendar days for fatal or life-threatening events). The sponsor's regulatory affairs team is responsible for assessing whether each Grade-3 or Grade-4 SAE triggers an expedited reporting obligation and for preparing the appropriate IND safety report. Failure to report within the specified window constitutes a regulatory violation irrespective of causality assessment.

The sponsor's approach to blinding and randomisation procedures is governed by established standards, consistent with the data monitoring committee's most recent safety review, and shall be reviewed at each scheduled data monitoring committee meeting to ensure continued alignment with evolving regulatory requirements. The sponsor's approach to trial master file archiving and retention is governed by established standards, in accordance with the applicable regulatory framework, and shall be reviewed at each scheduled data monitoring committee meeting to ensure continued alignment with evolving regulatory requirements. The sponsor's approach to site monitoring visit procedures and close-out is governed by established standards, as specified in the approved informed consent form and patient information sheet, and shall be reviewed at each scheduled data monitoring committee meeting to ensure continued alignment with evolving regulatory requirements. The sponsor's approach to investigational product accountability is governed by established standards, as determined by the principal investigator and clinical operations team, and shall be reviewed at each scheduled data monitoring committee meeting to ensure continued alignment with evolving regulatory requirements.
