# NovaTherx Internal Data Integrity Audit — Q4 2025 Summary Report
# Compound: NT-4800 (Phase I dose-escalation study)
# Audit period: October — December 2025
# Audit lead: NovaTherx Quality Assurance Team

> **IMPORTANT:** This audit summary covers the NT-4800 Phase I dose-escalation
> study only. It has no applicability to the NT-7701 Phase II clinical trial or
> to any NT-7701 regulatory submissions. Do not cite this document in connection
> with NT-7701 data.

---

## Executive Summary

The Q4 2025 internal data integrity audit covered the NT-4800 Phase I
dose-escalation study conducted at two investigator sites (Site A: Boston, MA;
Site B: Chicago, IL). The audit found the data management processes to be
generally compliant with applicable SOPs, with two minor observations noted:

1. A three-day delay in SAE reporting at Site A for one Grade-2 event (resolved).
2. A minor discrepancy in the date format used in the EDC for two laboratory
   records (corrected via DCF).

No Grade-3 or Grade-4 SAEs were reported in the NT-4800 Phase I study during
the Q4 2025 audit period. The compound's safety profile remains consistent with
the expected pharmacological class effects.

---

## 1. Study Overview — NT-4800 Phase I

NT-4800 is an investigational mTOR pathway inhibitor under evaluation in a Phase I
dose-escalation study. The Phase I study enrolled healthy volunteers and patients
with advanced solid tumours at two sites. This study is entirely separate from the
NT-7701 Phase II kinase inhibitor study.
