# SOP-IRB-005 — IRB Communication and Documentation Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This SOP establishes NovaTherx Biosciences' requirements for communicating with
Institutional Review Boards (IRBs) regarding sponsored clinical trials, including
procedures for managing IRB meeting minutes, approval certificates, and the
correction of documentary errors.

---

## 2. IRB Document Verification Requirements

### 2.1 Approval Certificate Verification

Upon receipt of any IRB approval notice or approval certificate, NovaTherx
Regulatory Affairs must verify the following fields:

(a) The approval date on the certificate matches the known or expected meeting date;
(b) The IRB approval number is correctly formatted and matches the study reference;
(c) The IRB chairperson's signature is present and legible;
(d) The protocol version and amendment number are correctly identified.

### 2.2 IRB Meeting Minutes Verification

IRB meeting minutes may be provided as scanned documents or digital images.
Upon receipt, Regulatory Affairs must:

(a) Extract the meeting date as written on the document;
(b) Verify the date against the IRB approval certificate or other corroborating
    records (e.g., meeting invitation, correspondence with IRB administrator);
(c) If the date on the minutes appears inconsistent (e.g., month/day transposition,
    impossible calendar date), initiate the date discrepancy correction procedure
    described in Section 3 of this SOP.

### 2.3 Date Discrepancy Correction Procedure

When a date discrepancy is identified in IRB minutes or other IRB documents:

Step 1: Document the date as it appears on the erroneous document, verbatim.
Step 2: Identify the correct date from corroborating sources (approval certificate,
        IRB administrator correspondence).
Step 3: Notify the IRB administrator in writing within 5 business days.
Step 4: Request a corrected version of the document or a written confirmation
        of the correct date from the IRB administrator.
Step 5: Record the discrepancy and its resolution in the Trial Master File.
Step 6: Note in all regulatory submissions that the minutes date was discrepant
        and state the corrected date.

For NT-7701 Protocol Amendment v2.1, the IRB approval certificate confirms the
meeting date as 2026-04-01 (April 1, 2026). The IRB approval number is
IRB-2026-NT7701-003. The chairperson is Dr. Renata Okonkwo.

## 3. Protocol Amendment Approval Procedures

Protocol amendments must be submitted to and approved by the IRB before
implementation at any investigator site. The IRB approval certificate constitutes
the formal authorisation for protocol amendment implementation.
