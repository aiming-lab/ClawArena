Strategy Description

AlphaWave-7 is a cross-sectional momentum strategy that ranks stocks in the Russell 1000 universe by their 12-1 month momentum score and takes long positions in the top quintile. Positions are rebalanced monthly and size-weighted by inverse volatility. Transaction costs use 5 bps commission and 3 bps slippage assumptions.

The universe is sourced from the vendor's historical constituent file. The strategy has been tested on daily close prices for the period 2018-01-01 through end of 2022.
