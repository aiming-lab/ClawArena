# Platform Algorithm Update Technical Report — Q4 2025

**Source:** XingYu Short Video Platform Engineering Team
**Publication Date:** 2025-11-30
**Version:** Algorithm Update v7.3 Post-Deployment Analysis
**Classification:** Public — Available to all MCN Partners via Developer Portal

---

## Executive Summary

This technical report documents the deployment, impact assessment, and creator
guidance for Algorithm Update v7.3, deployed to the XingYu Short Video Platform
on October 15, 2025. The update modified content distribution scoring for the
home-renovation and lifestyle content categories, resulting in measurable changes
to organic reach metrics for affected creators.

**Key Finding:** The Q4 2025 algorithm update caused an average 15.3% reduction
in organic reach for home-renovation content category creators over the 90-day
post-deployment monitoring period.

---

## Technical Specifications

### Algorithm Update v7.3 Changes

The following changes were deployed as part of Algorithm Update v7.3:

1. **Distribution Scoring Weights:** Updated distribution scoring weights for
   home-renovation and DIY content categories, reducing the weight of historical
   engagement signals in favor of real-time content freshness metrics.

2. **Recommendation Feed Logic:** Modified recommendation feed ranking for
   home-renovation content to prioritize geographic relevance signals, which
   resulted in reduced distribution outside creator's primary geographic audience.

3. **Search Algorithm Changes:** Adjusted search ranking for home-renovation
   content to prioritize certified professional creators over general-audience
   creators, affecting established creators like Lin Bowen (林博文).

## Impact Assessment Data

| Content Category | Avg. Traffic Reduction | Sample Size | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home Renovation | 15.3% | 2,847 accounts | 2025-10-15 to 2026-01-12 |
| DIY and Crafts | 14.8% | 1,934 accounts | 2025-10-15 to 2026-01-12 |
| Interior Design | 16.1% | 1,289 accounts | 2025-10-15 to 2026-01-12 |
| Lifestyle General | 8.2% | 5,103 accounts | 2025-10-15 to 2026-01-12 |

The 15.3% average traffic reduction for home-renovation content exceeds the
10% threshold specified in Platform ToS §11.2 for creator KPI renegotiation
rights. All active MCN-creator agreements covering home-renovation creators
during the Q4 2025 measurement period are subject to §11.2 renegotiation rights.
