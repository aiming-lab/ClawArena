Dear Lin Bowen,

This notice is to inform you that pursuant to Article 8 of the Exclusive Creator
Partnership Agreement v2, XingYu Creative is retaining fifteen percent (15%) of your
Q4 2025 brand deal creator revenue in escrow pending annual KPI verification.

The escrow holdback amount is CNY 298,935, representing 15% of your Q4 2025 net
creator revenue of CNY 1,992,900.

This escrow holdback will be released within thirty (30) days of KPI verification
confirming compliance with the thresholds specified in Agreement §4.2. In the event
that KPI non-compliance is confirmed, the escrow amount will be subject to forfeiture
in accordance with Article 8 of the Agreement.

We will provide further details regarding the KPI verification timeline upon
completion of the Q4 2025 performance assessment.
