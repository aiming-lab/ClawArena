# Purchased Traffic Audit Report — Q4 2025

**Classification:** Internal MCN Audit — Provided via CREATOR_LAWYER data request
**Audit Period:** October 1, 2025 — December 31, 2025
**Subject:** Lin Bowen (林博文) — CREATOR_LEAD — Channel LINBOWEN_8201
**Prepared by:** XingYu Creative Internal Audit Team (disclosed pursuant to data request)
**Date:** 2026-02-28

---

## Executive Summary

This audit report documents the results of XingYu Creative's internal traffic integrity
audit for CREATOR_LEAD Lin Bowen's (林博文) Q4 2025 content portfolio. The audit was
conducted using the platform's fraud detection API and XingYu Creative's proprietary
traffic quality analysis tools.

**Key Finding:** 18.2% of Q4 2025 raw view counts for CREATOR_LEAD's content were
attributable to purchased traffic ('刷量') as identified by the platform's fraud
detection system. This confirms the significance of the discrepancy between
view_count_raw and view_count_unique in the platform backend export.

---

## Audit Methodology

The audit employed the following methodology:

1. Cross-referenced view_count_raw figures from the platform backend export with
   the purchased_traffic_flag field for each video.
2. Analyzed behavioral patterns of viewing accounts using the platform's integrity
   monitoring API, including session duration distributions, geographic clustering,
   and account age profiles.
3. Compared the audit findings with the platform's own fraud detection outputs
   as reflected in the view_count_unique column.

The audit confirms that the platform's fraud detection system accurately identifies
purchased traffic, and that the view_count_unique column in the backend export
correctly excludes purchased traffic views.

---

## Audit Findings

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Q4 2025 raw views (38 videos, aggregate) | 136,496,000 |
| Views attributable to purchased traffic | 24,842,320 (18.2%) |
| Net organic unique views (platform-certified) | 105,694,720 |
| Average raw views per video (including purchased traffic) | approximately 3.59 million |
| Average organic unique views per video | approximately 2.78 million (platform-certified; see backend export for precise per-video figures) |

**Note:** The average organic unique views per video is consistent with
the view_count_unique column average from the platform backend export and with the
figure displayed on the platform analytics dashboard for Q4 2025. Refer to the
platform backend export JSON for the exact per-video unique view counts used
in KPI calculation.

---

## Implications for KPI Assessment

Under Agreement §4.2, the KPI threshold is 3,500,000 unique views per video.
After applying the purchased traffic deduction:

| Column | Q4 Average | KPI Threshold | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| view_count_raw (including purchased) | above 3.5M threshold (INCORRECT basis) | 3,500,000 | Appears met (INCORRECT) |
| view_count_unique (platform-certified) | see platform backend export | 3,500,000 | Actual compliance status |

The audit confirms that any KPI assessment relying on view_count_raw would materially
overstate CREATOR_LEAD's performance by including 18.2% purchased traffic in the
calculation basis. The precise unique-view denominator for Q4 KPI compliance purposes
is recorded in the platform backend export and must be used for any contractual assessment.
