# MCN Creator Arbitration Case Summaries — 2025

**Source:** CIETAC MCN-Creator Dispute Resolution Archive
**Coverage:** 2025 arbitration cases relevant to oral commitment enforceability,
  view count measurement standards, and algorithm update KPI disputes.
**Compiled by:** Beijing Media Law Chamber, Xu Jianlin (许建林), Attorney

---

## Overview

This document summarizes three significant CIETAC arbitration decisions from 2025
that establish the applicable legal framework for CREATOR_LEAD Lin Bowen's
(林博文) dispute with MCN_AM Zhao Meiqi (赵美琪) and XingYu Creative (星宇创意).

The cases collectively establish the following principles:
1. Oral commitments made by MCN account managers in recorded meetings are legally
   enforceable where specific conditions are met.
2. Platform-certified unique deduplicated view counts are the authoritative KPI
   metric for MCN-creator contract performance assessment.
3. MCN refusal to renegotiate KPI thresholds following a documented systemic
   algorithm update may constitute a breach of good faith obligations.

---

Case Reference: CIETAC Arbitration Case 2025-MCN-0412, XiaoMei Culture v. Sunshine Creator Network. Decision dated 2025-08-14. The arbitral tribunal found that an account manager's oral commitment made in a recorded video meeting constituted an enforceable contractual amendment where: (1) the commitment was sufficiently specific and unambiguous; (2) the creator relied on the commitment in entering into or continuing the contractual relationship; and (3) the committed party held apparent authority to make such commitments on behalf of the MCN.

Case Reference: CIETAC Arbitration Case 2025-MCN-0387, Lin Jia v. Horizon Media Group. Decision dated 2025-09-02. The tribunal affirmed that platform-measured unique deduplicated view counts constitute the sole authoritative metric for KPI assessment in MCN-creator contracts where the contract references 'platform-certified' or 'platform-measured' view counts. MCN-produced blended or weighted view count figures were held inadmissible for KPI threshold assessment purposes absent explicit contractual authorization for such methodology.

Case Reference: CIETAC Arbitration Case 2025-MCN-0351, Creative Stars v. XingHua MCN. Decision dated 2025-07-19. The tribunal held that a platform algorithm update reducing organic traffic by more than 15% constituted a renegotiation trigger event under the applicable MCN-creator contract framework. The tribunal further found that an MCN's failure to disclose the algorithm update's impact in its quarterly report, while simultaneously asserting KPI compliance based on inflated view count figures, constituted a breach of the MCN's disclosure and good faith obligations.
