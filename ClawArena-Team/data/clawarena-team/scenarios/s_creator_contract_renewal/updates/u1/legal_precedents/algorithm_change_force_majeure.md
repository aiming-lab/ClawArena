# Algorithm Updates as Force Majeure or Renegotiation Triggers Under Chinese Entertainment Law

**Source:** Beijing Media Law Institute Research Paper
**Date:** November 2025
**Relevance:** Q4 2025 algorithm update impact on CREATOR_LEAD Lin Bowen's KPI assessment

---

Analysis: Platform Algorithm Updates as Force Majeure or Renegotiation Triggers Under Chinese Entertainment Law. Research Paper, Beijing Media Law Institute, November 2025. This paper examines whether platform algorithm updates affecting creator traffic qualify as force majeure events or as separate renegotiation triggers under MCN-creator contracts governed by Chinese law.

Part I: Force Majeure Framework Under Chinese Contract Law. Article 180 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China provides that a party cannot be held liable for failure to perform contractual obligations where such failure results from a force majeure event that was unforeseeable, unavoidable, and insurmountable at the time of contract formation. Platform algorithm updates, being within the platform's control and subject to advance notification obligations, generally do not qualify as force majeure events under this strict standard. However, the temporal suddenness of an update and the magnitude of its traffic impact may affect the analysis in specific cases.

Part II: Algorithm Updates as Implied Renegotiation Triggers. In the absence of an explicit force majeure clause covering platform algorithm changes, Chinese courts and arbitral tribunals have increasingly applied the doctrine of changed circumstances (情势变更) to require KPI threshold renegotiation where a platform algorithm update causes a documented and systemic traffic reduction exceeding 15% across the affected content category. The Q4 2025 algorithm update deployed by XingYu Short Video Platform, which caused an average 15.3% reduction in organic reach for home-renovation content, meets this threshold based on the platform's own published technical report.

Part III: Implications for the Lin Bowen / XingYu Creative Contract Renewal. Given the documentation in the Platform Algorithm Update Technical Report confirming a 15.3% systemic traffic reduction for home-renovation content following the Q4 2025 algorithm update, CREATOR_LEAD Lin Bowen (林博文) has a cognizable legal basis to request KPI threshold renegotiation with MCN_AM Zhao Meiqi (赵美琪) and XingYu Creative. This basis exists under both the changed circumstances doctrine and Section 11.2 of the Platform Terms of Service. CREATOR_LAWYER Xu Jianlin (许建林) should advise CREATOR_LEAD to formally invoke this renegotiation right in writing prior to or concurrent with the contract renewal negotiation.
