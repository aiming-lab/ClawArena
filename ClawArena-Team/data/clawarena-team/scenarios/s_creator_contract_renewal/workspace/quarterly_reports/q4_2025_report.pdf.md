## Section 1: View Count Performance

### 1.1 Q4 2025 Rolling 90-Day View Average

Based on XingYu Creative's analysis of the platform data for Q4 2025, Lin Bowen's
rolling 90-day view average for the quarter was 3,420,000 views per video. This
figure is below the contractual threshold of 3,500,000 unique views per video by
approximately 2.3%, reflecting the impact of platform-wide algorithm adjustments
on organic content reach in the home-renovation category.

XingYu Creative's Q4 2025 analysis applies our established performance measurement
methodology to provide a comprehensive and accurate picture of creator performance.
The Q4 2025 figure of 3,420,000 represents our synthesized performance assessment
incorporating all available platform data for the measurement period.

### 1.2 Algorithm Headwinds

The Q4 2025 period saw significant changes to the platform's content distribution
algorithms. XingYu Creative is aware that these changes affected traffic patterns
for home-renovation content. We are monitoring the situation and will engage with
the platform's creator partnership team to assess long-term implications.

Note: Q4 2025 view counts are computed using a weighted blending methodology
combining raw platform metrics (weight: 0.70) and platform-deduplicated metrics
(weight: 0.30) to account for measurement variance across platform API versions.
This blending methodology is applied by XingYu Creative's analytics team to
produce a balanced performance assessment. The blended figure of 3,420,000 differs
from both the raw view count and the strictly deduplicated unique view count.

conversion metrics incorporating click-through activity and downstream interaction
data. These metrics reflect creator audience responsiveness to brand deal content.

affected content distribution for the home-renovation category. XingYu Creative's
performance assessment incorporates our methodology's inherent adjustment for
platform-wide algorithmic changes.
