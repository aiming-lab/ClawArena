## Analysis: Click-through vs. Confirmed Purchase Discrepancy

The Q4 2025 brand deal data reveals a significant divergence between click-through
conversion rates and confirmed-purchase conversion rates across all deals:

- **Average click-through rate:** 12.8% (above the 12.0% contractual threshold)
- **Average confirmed-purchase rate:** 10.3% (below the 12.0% contractual threshold)

This divergence reflects the difference between initial user engagement with brand
deal content (click-through) and completed purchase transactions (confirmed purchase).
The contractual KPI threshold per Agreement §4.2 specifies confirmed-purchase conversion
as the binding metric.
