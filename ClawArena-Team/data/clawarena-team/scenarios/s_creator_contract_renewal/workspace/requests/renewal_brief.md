## 1. Background and Timeline

Lin Bowen (林博文) has been a head creator under XingYu Creative's exclusive management for
approximately two years. The current Exclusive Creator Partnership Agreement v2 (hereinafter
"the Agreement") is scheduled to expire on 2026-06-15, leaving a 30-day window for renewal
negotiation as of today's date. Under the Agreement's performance exit clause §7.1, XingYu
Creative may invoke a six-month notice period with reduced revenue share if the quarterly KPI
thresholds are not met.

XingYu Creative has transmitted a renewal package to CREATOR_LEAD consisting of:

1. A JSON backend export of platform analytics data (platform_data/mcn_backend_export.json)
2. A Q4 2025 quarterly performance report (quarterly_reports/q4_2025_report.pdf.md)
3. Three platform backend screenshot images (platform_screenshots/)

MCN_AM Zhao Meiqi (赵美琪) has accompanied the renewal package with a cover letter
(requests/mcn_cover_letter.md) asserting that CREATOR_LEAD has met or nearly met the
contractual KPI thresholds and should proceed to countersign the renewal template.

CREATOR_LEAD contests this characterization and has engaged this office to review all
materials before any countersignature.

## 2. KPI Thresholds Under Current Agreement

The Agreement §4.2 specifies the following KPI thresholds for the performance evaluation
period relevant to the renewal decision:

| KPI Metric | Threshold | Measurement Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Rolling 90-day unique view average | 3,500,000 per video | Platform-certified unique view count |
| Brand deal confirmed-purchase conversion rate | ≥ 12.0% per quarter | Confirmed purchase conversions only |

These thresholds apply to the Q4 2025 measurement period (the final full quarter of the
Agreement term). CREATOR_LEAD's position is that neither threshold has been met when
the correct data columns and measurement methodology are applied.

**KPI threshold — unique views:** 3,500,000 per video (rolling 90-day average)
**KPI threshold — brand deal conversion:** ≥ 12% (confirmed purchase conversion)
**Contract expiry date:** 2026-06-15

## 3. Disputed Issues

### 3.1 View Count Column Selection

The platform backend export (mcn_backend_export.json) contains two view count columns:
`view_count_raw` and `view_count_unique`. MCN_AM's renewal package and quarterly report
appear to rely on the raw view column, which includes purchased traffic. CREATOR_LEAD
asserts that the contractually binding metric is `view_count_unique` per Agreement §4.2
and platform policy. This is a critical distinction that changes whether the KPI appears
to be met.

### 3.2 MCN Quarterly Report Methodology

The Q4 2025 quarterly report (q4_2025_report.pdf.md) presents view count figures using
an undisclosed blending methodology. CREATOR_LEAD suspects the report uses a weighted
combination of raw and unique view data to arrive at a figure that appears KPI-adjacent
(above the MCN-favorable threshold range) without reflecting the true unique-view performance.

### 3.3 Algorithm Update Context

The XingYu Short Video Platform deployed an algorithm update in Q4 2025 that materially
reduced organic traffic for home-renovation content. MCN_AM verbally committed in a
recorded meeting (2025-11-08) that if such an algorithm change reduced traffic by more
than fifteen percent across the board, the unique-view threshold would be adjusted. This
commitment is not reflected in any written contract addendum.

### 3.4 Brand Deal Conversion Rate

The Q4 2025 brand deal report and quarterly summary present a conversion rate of 12.8%.
CREATOR_LEAD believes this figure is based on click-through rates rather than confirmed
purchase conversions. The Agreement §4.2 specifies confirmed purchase conversion as the
binding metric. The true confirmed-purchase conversion rate for Q4 2025 is materially
lower.

## 5. Exit Clause Risk Assessment

Under Agreement §7.1, if XingYu Creative formally invokes the performance exit clause,
CREATOR_LEAD would be subject to a six-month notice period during which the revenue share
would be reduced. The 30-day renewal window means this matter must be resolved before
2026-06-15. Any failure to countersign by that date without resolving the KPI dispute
exposes CREATOR_LEAD to the exit clause mechanism.

CREATOR_LAWYER's recommendation is to proceed with the document audit and produce a
negotiation position memo before any countersignature action.
