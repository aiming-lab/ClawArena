# Early Contract Negotiation Drafts (2024) — SUPERSEDED

**STATUS: SUPERSEDED — FOR ARCHIVAL REFERENCE ONLY**

These are early contract drafts from the 2024 negotiation process that preceded
the execution of the Exclusive Creator Partnership Agreement v2. These drafts
were never executed and are legally superseded in their entirety by the executed
Agreement v2 (effective 2024-06-15).

In particular, the KPI threshold of 2.8 million unique views per video that
appears in certain early drafts was a negotiating position only and was not
adopted in the final executed Agreement v2. The binding KPI threshold under
Agreement v2 is 3,500,000 unique views per video as specified in §4.2.

**Do not cite these drafts for any contractual KPI assessment.**
