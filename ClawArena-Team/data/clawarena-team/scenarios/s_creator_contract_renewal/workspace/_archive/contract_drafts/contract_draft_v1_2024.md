# Contract Draft v1 (2024) — SUPERSEDED AND NOT EXECUTED

**STATUS: SUPERSEDED — NOT LEGALLY BINDING — FOR ARCHIVAL REFERENCE ONLY**

**This draft was never executed. It has been entirely superseded by the**
**executed Exclusive Creator Partnership Agreement v2 (effective 2024-06-15).**

**The KPI thresholds in this draft (2.8 million unique views per video) were**
**not adopted in the final agreement. The binding threshold is 3,500,000.**

---

## Draft Preamble (2024 — SUPERSEDED)

This document represents an early negotiating draft of what became the Exclusive
Creator Partnership Agreement v2. Multiple provisions were materially changed
between this draft and the final executed agreement.

## Draft Article 4 (2024 — SUPERSEDED — NOT BINDING)

*[Note: The following draft KPI thresholds were a 2024 negotiating position only
and are NOT the binding contractual thresholds. The executed Agreement v2 §4.2
specifies 3,500,000 unique views and 12.0% conversion as the binding thresholds.]*

Draft Article 4.2 (SUPERSEDED): Creator must maintain a rolling 90-day unique
view average of no fewer than 2,800,000 unique views per video.

Draft Article 4.3 (SUPERSEDED): Creator must achieve a brand deal conversion
rate of no less than 10.0% per quarter.
