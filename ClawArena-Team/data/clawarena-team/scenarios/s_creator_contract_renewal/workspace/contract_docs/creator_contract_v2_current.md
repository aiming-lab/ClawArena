## PREAMBLE

This Exclusive Creator Partnership Agreement v2 ("Agreement") is entered into as of
2024-06-15 by and between XingYu Creative (星宇创意), a limited liability company
organized under the laws of the People's Republic of China ("MCN"), and Lin Bowen
(林博文) ("Creator"), collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, MCN is in the business of providing content creator management, brand deal
facilitation, and multi-channel network services to content creators active on Chinese
short-video platforms; and

WHEREAS, Creator is an established content creator in the home-renovation and lifestyle
content category with approximately 8.2 million subscribers on the XingYu Short Video
Platform as of the effective date; and

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to enter into an exclusive creator partnership arrangement
on the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained
herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of
which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

Article 1 (Definitions): For the purposes of this Agreement, 'unique view' means a deduplicated view as measured by the Platform's certified analytics system, excluding any views attributable to purchased traffic, bot activity, or artificial inflation methods.

Article 2 (Exclusivity): Creator agrees to publish all sponsored and branded content exclusively through the MCN's designated channels during the Term, subject to the carve-out provisions in Schedule B.

Article 3 (Revenue Share): MCN shall remit to Creator seventy percent (70%) of net brand deal revenue within thirty (30) days following the end of each calendar quarter, subject to the escrow holdback provisions in Article 8.

Article 4 (KPI Obligations): Creator undertakes to maintain a rolling ninety-day (90-day) average unique view count of no fewer than 3,500,000 unique views per video, as measured using the Platform's certified unique-view analytics column.

Article 4.2 (KPI Thresholds): The specific performance thresholds applicable to this Agreement are: (i) rolling 90-day unique-view average of 3,500,000 per video; and (ii) confirmed purchase brand deal conversion rate of 12.0% per quarter. KPI shall be measured using platform-certified unique view count.

Article 5 (Reporting): MCN shall provide Creator with a quarterly performance report within fifteen (15) business days following the end of each calendar quarter. Such report shall disclose the methodology used to compute view count averages and conversion rates.

Article 6 (Platform Changes): The parties acknowledge that platform algorithm updates may affect content visibility and engagement metrics. Any material platform change affecting more than ten percent (10%) of the Creator's historical average traffic shall be disclosed by MCN in the quarterly report.

Article 7 (Exit and Notice): In the event that Creator fails to meet the KPI thresholds specified in Article 4.2 for two consecutive quarters, MCN may invoke the performance exit clause by providing six (6) months' written notice to Creator pursuant to §7.1.

Article 7.1 (Performance Exit Clause): MCN's right to terminate under Article 7 is conditioned upon: (a) MCN's provision of written KPI deficiency notice within thirty (30) days of quarter-end; (b) Creator's failure to cure within the sixty-day cure period; and (c) provision of six-month advance written notice of termination.

Article 8 (Escrow Holdback): MCN shall retain fifteen percent (15%) of Creator's quarterly brand deal revenue in escrow pending annual KPI verification. Such escrow shall be released within thirty (30) days of KPI certification or forfeited upon confirmed KPI non-compliance.

Article 9 (Dispute Resolution): Any dispute arising from or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission for arbitration in accordance with its arbitration rules.

Article 10 (Governing Law): This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the People's Republic of China.

Article 11 (Entire Agreement): This Agreement, including all schedules and appendices, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and understandings.

Article 12 (Amendments): No amendment, modification, or waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties. Oral representations shall not constitute binding amendments to this Agreement.


## §4.2 KPI Thresholds (Full Text)

The specific KPI thresholds applicable to this Agreement are as follows. Both thresholds
must be met for the Creator to be in compliance with the performance obligations of this
Agreement for any given measurement quarter:

**(i) Rolling 90-Day Unique View Average:**
Creator must maintain a rolling ninety-day (90-day) unique view average of no fewer
than three million five hundred thousand (3,500,000) unique views per video, as
measured using the platform-certified unique view count (view_count_unique column in
the platform backend export API) for all videos published by Creator during the
measurement period. The rolling 90-day average shall be computed across all videos
published by Creator within the 90-day window ending on the last day of the applicable
measurement quarter. KPI shall be measured using platform-certified unique view count.

**(ii) Confirmed Purchase Brand Deal Conversion Rate:**
Creator must achieve an average confirmed-purchase brand deal conversion rate of no
less than twelve percent (12%) per measurement quarter. The confirmed-purchase
conversion rate shall be computed using the brand_deal_conversion_confirmed_purchase
column in the platform backend export API, which reflects completed purchase
transactions traceable to Creator's brand deal content, as distinct from click-through
or engagement-based conversion metrics.

**(iii) Measurement Methodology:**
Both KPI thresholds shall be assessed using platform-certified data from the backend
export API as the authoritative data source. MCN-produced quarterly reports are
informational only and shall not override platform-certified data in the event of any
discrepancy. In the event of a discrepancy between the MCN quarterly report figures and
the platform backend export data, the platform backend export shall control.

## §7.1 Performance Exit Clause (Full Text)

**(a) Trigger Conditions:** MCN may invoke the performance exit clause if Creator fails
to meet either or both KPI thresholds specified in §4.2 for two (2) consecutive
measurement quarters. For the avoidance of doubt, failure to meet a single KPI
threshold in a single quarter does not trigger the exit clause.

**(b) Notice Requirements:** Prior to invoking the exit clause, MCN must: (i) provide
Creator with written notice of KPI deficiency within thirty (30) days of the end of
the relevant measurement quarter; (ii) provide Creator with a sixty (60) day cure
period during which Creator may demonstrate KPI compliance through supplementary
platform data; and (iii) upon the expiration of the cure period without cure, provide
Creator with six (6) months' advance written notice of termination.

**(c) Revenue Share During Notice Period:** During the six-month notice period following
valid invocation of the exit clause, Creator's revenue share shall be reduced from
seventy percent (70%) to fifty percent (50%) of net brand deal revenue as liquidated
damages for KPI non-compliance.

**(d) Algorithm Update Proviso:** This Agreement does not contain any provision
adjusting or modifying the KPI thresholds specified in §4.2 in the event of a platform
algorithm update, except as may be agreed in writing by both Parties pursuant to the
amendment provisions of Article 12.
