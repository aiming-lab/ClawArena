# MCN Renewal Contract Template v3

**Template Type:** Exclusive Creator Partnership Renewal
**Version:** v3 (MCN proposed terms — not yet executed)
**Proposed Effective Date:** 2026-06-15
**Proposed Term:** Three (3) years (expiring 2029-06-15)
**Prepared by:** XingYu Creative (星宇创意) Legal and Account Management Team

---

## PREAMBLE

This Exclusive Creator Partnership Renewal Agreement v3 ("Renewal Agreement") is
proposed by XingYu Creative (星宇创意) ("MCN") for execution by Lin Bowen (林博文)
("Creator") as a continuation and extension of the Exclusive Creator Partnership
Agreement v2, which expires on 2026-06-15.

The MCN proposes the following terms for the renewal term, which maintains the existing
partnership framework while extending the exclusivity period and reflecting current
market conditions.

---

## Article 1: Term Extension

The renewal term shall commence on 2026-06-15 and continue for three (3) years,
expiring on 2029-06-15, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the provisions
of this Renewal Agreement.

---

## Article 4: KPI Thresholds (Proposed Renewal Terms)

The following KPI thresholds shall apply for the renewal term. These thresholds are
unchanged from the executed Agreement v2:

**(i) Rolling 90-Day Unique View Average:** 3,500,000 per video (unchanged from v2)

**(ii) Confirmed Purchase Brand Deal Conversion Rate:** ≥ 12.0% per quarter (unchanged from v2)

The MCN maintains that these thresholds remain appropriate and achievable based on
our assessment of Creator's performance trajectory and platform conditions.

---

## Article 5: Exclusivity Extension

The exclusivity provisions of the existing Agreement v2 are extended for the renewal
term, covering all commercial and sponsored content published on Chinese short-video
platforms. The carve-outs specified in Schedule B of Agreement v2 continue to apply.
