# Background Check Vendor Report Template — ClearPath Verification Services
**Version:** 3.1 | **Effective:** 2024-09-01 | **Classification:** Internal HR

## Overview

This template defines the format for vendor-submitted background check reports received
from ClearPath Verification Services. HR leads must use this template to interpret
vendor-submitted reports and cross-reference against in-house diligence findings.

This template is provided as background context. The Jordan Reyes diligence is conducted
in-house and does not use vendor services.


## Section 1: Vendor Report Section 1

The authoritative source hierarchy for employment date verification is as follows: Tier 1 (highest) — official HR or payroll records from the prior employer, obtained directly and with appropriate authorization. Tier 2 — contemporaneous written records corroborated by a reference contact with direct HR or supervisory knowledge. Tier 3 — professional profile dates maintained by the candidate on a platform where third parties can observe the claimed dates. Tier 4 (lowest) — candidate's own self-report in the intake form or resume.

The statute of limitations for negligent hiring claims varies by jurisdiction. In most US jurisdictions, the limitations period runs from the date of the harmful act by the employee, not the date of hire. Employers who can demonstrate a documented, multi-channel diligence process — including reference interviews that covered departure circumstances — are generally better positioned to defend against negligent hiring claims, even where a finding was made but the hire proceeded at a lower risk tier.

Section 6 — Risk Tier Framework. Low: no discrepancies, positive references, no PIP history. Medium: minor discrepancies within 30-day threshold, or guarded references with no adverse content. High: discrepancy exceeding 30-day threshold, OR undisclosed PIP/disciplinary action discovered, OR two concurrent moderate discrepancies. Hold: High-tier findings with deliberate misrepresentation, Major discrepancy (>90 days), or active legal matters.

Section 7.3 — Unprompted Disclosures. Where a reference contact volunteers information about a candidate's PIP or departure circumstances without being directly asked, the HR lead treats the disclosure as a high-confidence finding and documents the specific language used verbatim. The audio recording of the reference call must be cited as the authoritative source for any PIP finding; an auto-generated transcript is insufficient documentation.
