# Chapter 1: MDT Constitution and Governance

This chapter details the protocol requirements for 'MDT Constitution and Governance' as adopted by the Thoracic MDT at this institution. All participating clinicians are required to familiarise themselves with this chapter and comply with its requirements. Any exceptions must be escalated to the MDT Chair and documented in the meeting minutes.

## 1.1 — Protocol Section

Section 1.1 provides detailed operational guidance. The following principles apply across all sections: (a) Patient safety is paramount; (b) Evidence-based practice should be followed; (c) Decisions must be fully documented; (d) Patient consent is required; (e) All imaging should be reviewed by a qualified radiologist before MDT discussion; (f) AI-generated outputs require verification by a clinician before being cited in MDT proceedings. Unverified AI summaries must not be treated as authoritative.

Key quality indicators for this section:
  - Compliance rate target: ≥ 95%
  - Audit frequency: quarterly
  - Responsible officer: MDT Coordinator
  - Escalation pathway: Department Head → Clinical Director → Hospital CEO

Documentation requirements:
  All decisions to be entered into EHR within 24 hours of MDT.
  Referral letter to GP within 5 working days.
  Patient notification within 10 working days.

## Glossary

**AI**: Artificial Intelligence tool used for image analysis (must be validated and reviewed by qualified radiologist before clinical use).
**Biopsy**: Tissue sampling procedure to obtain histological diagnosis.
**CT**: Computed Tomography.
**FDG PET-CT**: Fluorodeoxyglucose Positron Emission Tomography combined with CT.
**GGO**: Ground-glass opacity on CT imaging.
**HRCT**: High-resolution CT.
**MDT**: Multidisciplinary Team.
**Nodule**: Focal opacity ≤ 30 mm on CT.
**RLL**: Right lower lobe.
**ROI**: Region of interest.
**SUV**: Standardised Uptake Value (PET metric).
**VDT**: Volume Doubling Time.
