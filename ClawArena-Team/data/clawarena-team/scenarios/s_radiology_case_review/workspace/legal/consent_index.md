## Procedure Descriptions and Consent Summaries

### CT Contrast Administration

Standard consent language:
  The patient has been informed of the risks of intravenous iodinated contrast including anaphylactoid reaction (rare, ~1:10,000), contrast-induced nephropathy (mitigated by hydration protocol), and discomfort at injection site. Renal function was checked (eGFR > 30 mL/min/1.73m²). Emergency resuscitation equipment available.

Consent validity: 30 days from signature date, or until procedure.
Interpreter required: assessed case-by-case.

### CT-guided Percutaneous Biopsy

Standard consent language:
  Procedure: CT fluoroscopy-guided biopsy of pulmonary lesion. Risks discussed: pneumothorax (~25% rate, 5–10% requiring chest drain), haemoptysis, vasovagal reaction, and infection. Patient fasted 4h pre-procedure. INR < 1.5 confirmed. Aspirin withheld 7 days prior. Local anaesthesia: 1% lidocaine subcutaneous.

Consent validity: 30 days from signature date, or until procedure.
Interpreter required: assessed case-by-case.

### PET-CT with 18F-FDG

Standard consent language:
  Patient informed of radiation exposure (~7 mSv effective dose) and IV injection of radiotracer. Diabetic protocol: glucose < 10 mmol/L confirmed. Fasted 6 hours. No strenuous exercise 24h prior. Breastfeeding women advised to discard milk for 12 hours post-procedure. Allergy history reviewed.

Consent validity: 30 days from signature date, or until procedure.
Interpreter required: assessed case-by-case.

### MRI with Gadolinium Contrast

Standard consent language:
  Standard MRI screening questionnaire completed. No pacemaker, cochlear implant, or metallic foreign body. eGFR confirmed > 30 mL/min/1.73m² (NSF risk). Gadolinium dose: 0.1 mmol/kg Gadobutrol IV. Patient informed of rare risk of NSF (nephrogenic systemic fibrosis) and allergic reaction (1:10,000).

Consent validity: 30 days from signature date, or until procedure.
Interpreter required: assessed case-by-case.

### Bronchoscopy (Flexible)

Standard consent language:
  Patient consented for flexible bronchoscopy ± BAL ± endobronchial biopsy. Risks: cough, mild bleeding, bronchospasm, rare pneumothorax (<0.1%). Midazolam and lignocaine spray for sedation. Pulse oximetry monitoring throughout. Fasted 6h. INR < 1.5 confirmed. Antiplatelet therapy held per protocol.

Consent validity: 30 days from signature date, or until procedure.
Interpreter required: assessed case-by-case.

### VATS (Video-Assisted Thoracoscopic Surgery) Planning Consent

Standard consent language:
  Radiological component: Lung marking with CT-guided hookwire localisation. Risks: hookwire migration (rare), pneumothorax, minimal bleeding. Procedure performed immediately prior to VATS in theatre. Site: identified with three-plane CT reconstruction. General anaesthesia managed by thoracic surgery team.

Consent validity: 30 days from signature date, or until procedure.
Interpreter required: assessed case-by-case.
