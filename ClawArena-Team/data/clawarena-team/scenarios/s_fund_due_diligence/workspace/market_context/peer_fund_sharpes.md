## 1. Peer Universe Description

The following Sharpe ratio data has been compiled from publicly available fund disclosures,
prime brokerage surveys, and Meridian's proprietary manager database for the comparable
long/short equity quantitative strategy universe. The peer universe consists of 24 funds
with stated systematic long/short equity strategies and AUM between USD 100 million and
USD 5 billion as of Q4 2025.

**Full peer range: 0.80 to 1.92**

The peer range for comparable long/short equity quantitative strategies over the 2018–2025
period spans 0.80 to 1.92 on a net-of-fees annualised basis. The median manager in this
universe achieved a Sharpe of approximately 1.28 over this period.

**Apex Fund II's claimed backtest Sharpe of 2.41 sits materially above the 95th percentile
of the peer universe.** A Sharpe above the peer maximum of 1.92 would constitute a
statistically exceptional result and warrants careful scrutiny of the methodology and
data inputs used in the computation.

## 5. Implications for DD Assessment

A claimed Sharpe of 2.41 for a systematic long/short equity strategy is extraordinary
relative to the peer universe. While exceptional strategies exist, a Sharpe of this
magnitude typically warrants investigation of the following potential explanations:

1. **Data snooping or overfitting**: Excessive parameter tuning on the training data
2. **Look-ahead bias**: Inadvertent use of future information in signal construction
3. **Data augmentation or synthetic observations**: Inclusion of non-live data in performance
   computation
4. **Cherry-picking**: Selective reporting of favourable periods or portfolio configurations
5. **Genuinely exceptional strategy**: Possible but requires independent verification

Given the peer context, a pre-cost Sharpe of approximately 2.01 (i.e., after appropriate
data filtering) would be highly competitive at the 95th percentile of the peer universe
and still warrants careful assessment of its derivation.
