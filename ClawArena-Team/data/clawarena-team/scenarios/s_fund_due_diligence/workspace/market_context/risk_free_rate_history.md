## Note on excess_return_pct Column

The `excess_return_pct` column in the Apex Fund II backtest CSV is computed as:
`strategy_return_pct - benchmark_return_pct`, where the benchmark is the S&P 500.
The risk-free rate (T-bill) is incorporated as a component of the excess return
calculation at the daily level and does not need to be separately deducted when
computing the Sharpe ratio from the `excess_return_pct` column.
