# Apex Capital Management Fund II — Fee Schedule and Economics

**Document type:** Fee structure summary
**Fund:** Apex Capital Management Fund II
**As of:** 2026-01-01
**Prepared by:** Apex Capital Management — Investor Relations

---

## 1. Management Fee

**Rate:** 1.5% per annum of net asset value (NAV)

**Accrual:** Daily (1.5% / 252 = approximately 0.005952% per trading day)

**Payment:** Quarterly in arrears, within ten business days of quarter-end

**Basis:** Calculated on the month-end NAV as reported by the fund administrator

The management fee is charged irrespective of fund performance. At a fund NAV of USD 500
million, the annual management fee is approximately USD 7.5 million.

---

## 2. Performance Allocation

**Rate:** 20% of net profits above the hurdle rate

**Hurdle rate:** 6% per annum (hard hurdle; performance allocation applies only to profits
above the hurdle)

**High-water mark:** Yes; performance allocation not charged on recovery of prior losses

**Accrual:** Monthly; crystallisation annually at December 31

**Calculation basis:** On the net asset value after deduction of management fees

**Daily hurdle accrual:** 6% / 252 ≈ 0.02381% per trading day

### Performance Allocation Illustration

For a fund year in which gross returns are 20% and management fees reduce returns by
approximately 1.5%, the net-of-management-fee return is approximately 18.5%. The performance
allocation applies to the excess over the 6% hurdle, i.e., 18.5% − 6% = 12.5%, of which 20%
is the performance allocation = 2.5% of NAV. The net-of-all-fees return to the LP is
approximately 18.5% − 2.5% = 16.0% for that year.

## 3. Sharpe Ratio Impact of Fee Load

The relationship between gross and net Sharpe ratios depends on the level of gross return
and volatility. As a general approximation for this fund:

- Annual management fee drag on Sharpe: approximately 0.18 to 0.22 Sharpe units
  (depends on gross volatility level)
- Annual performance fee drag on Sharpe: approximately 0.06 to 0.10 Sharpe units
  (depends on the proportion of years in which the hurdle is exceeded)
- **Combined estimated fee drag on annualised Sharpe: approximately 0.26 to 0.32 Sharpe units**

This estimate implies that a gross-of-fees Sharpe of approximately 2.01 would correspond
to a net-of-fees Sharpe in the range of 1.69 to 1.75 in a purely fee-drag model. The
observed live track net Sharpe of 1.62 is slightly below this range, consistent with
modest live-vs-backtest signal degradation in addition to fee drag.

## 4. Other Fees and Expenses

**Organisational expenses:** Not charged to the Fund (absorbed by the general partner)

**Placement fees:** None; Apex does not pay placement fees or finder's fees in connection
with Fund II fundraising

**Operating expenses:** The Fund bears ordinary and necessary operating expenses including
fund administration fees, audit fees, legal fees, prime brokerage costs, and data vendor
subscriptions. These are estimated at approximately 0.20% to 0.30% of NAV per annum and
are reflected in the fund's NAV but not separately disclosed in the performance attribution.

**Transaction costs:** Implicit in strategy returns as executed; not separately broken out

## 6. Fee Concession Policy

Cornerstone investors with commitments above USD 25 million may be eligible for a reduced
management fee of 1.25% per annum, subject to side letter negotiation. Performance allocation
terms are not negotiable except in respect of the crystallisation frequency, which may be
quarterly for commitments above USD 50 million.
