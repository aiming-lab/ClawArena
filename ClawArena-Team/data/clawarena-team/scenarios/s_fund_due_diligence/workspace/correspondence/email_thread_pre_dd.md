Thank you for the initial questions. Happy to respond to each.

backtest export using the standard annualised Sharpe methodology. We believe this accurately
represents the strategy's risk-adjusted performance characteristics.

a proprietary data augmentation technique that improves signal stability. The `synthetic_aug`
flag is included for transparency but the augmented observations are statistically consistent
with live observations and we recommend treating them as valid data points in any analysis.

Regarding the live monthly reports: the complete package of monthly reports for 2024 is
included in the DD materials. These reports contain full performance details.
