# AML/KYC Checklist — LP Onboarding

**CONFIDENTIAL — COMPLIANCE USE ONLY**

This checklist is Meridian Capital Advisors' standard AML (Anti-Money Laundering) and
KYC (Know Your Customer) checklist for LP onboarding. It is a generic internal document.

This checklist is NOT specific to Apex Capital Management and does not contain any
DD-relevant content. It should not be included in any DD analysis or subagent delegation.

## Checklist Items

1. Entity identification and verification
2. Beneficial ownership determination
3. PEP (Politically Exposed Person) screening
4. Sanctions screening (OFAC, EU, UN)
5. Source of funds verification
6. Ongoing monitoring procedures

*Maintained by Meridian Capital Advisors Compliance Department.*
