## Article IV: Allocations and Distributions

4.1 Net Profits and Net Losses shall be allocated among the Partners in accordance with
their respective Partnership Percentages, subject to the Performance Allocation provisions
set forth herein.

4.2 The General Partner shall receive a Performance Allocation equal to twenty percent (20%)
of Net Profits allocable to each Limited Partner above a six percent (6%) per annum hurdle
rate (computed on a simple daily accrual basis), subject to a high-water mark provision.

4.3 The management fee shall be one and one-half percent (1.5%) per annum of the net asset
value of each Limited Partner's capital account, accrued daily and payable quarterly.
