## 1. Investment Philosophy

Apex Capital Management's investment philosophy is grounded in the belief that systematic
exploitation of well-documented equity market anomalies, combined with rigorous risk
management and disciplined execution, can generate consistent risk-adjusted returns
that are uncorrelated with traditional equity benchmarks.
