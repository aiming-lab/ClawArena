## 1. Performance Summary

| Metric | September 2024 | Year-to-date |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy return (gross) | +1.98% | +15.49% |
| Benchmark return (S&P 500) | +2.14% | — |
| Trailing annualised Sharpe (gross, since inception) | 2.01 | — |

The Fund generated a gross return of **+1.98%** in September 2024, compared to the S&P 500
benchmark return of +2.14%. The trailing annualised Sharpe ratio computed on a
gross-of-fees basis since the Fund's inception stands at **2.01**.

Performance during the month was driven primarily by momentum and earnings revision factors,
which contributed positively to the long book. Quality factor positioning contributed modestly.
Sector allocation was broadly neutral with the portfolio maintaining close to zero net market
exposure consistent with the long/short mandate.
