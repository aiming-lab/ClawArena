# Apex Capital Management Fund II — 2024 Annual Live Performance Summary

**Fund:** Apex Capital Management Fund II
**Period:** January 1, 2024 — December 31, 2024
**Report type:** Annual live performance summary
**Prepared by:** Apex Capital Management — Investor Relations
**Date:** January 15, 2025

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Apex Capital Management Fund II delivered strong gross performance in the 2024 live period,
generating an annualised gross return of approximately 23.15% for the calendar year. The Fund's
systematic long/short equity strategy navigated a challenging macro environment characterised
by Federal Reserve policy uncertainty, sector rotation dynamics, and elevated cross-sectional
equity volatility. The trailing annualised Sharpe ratio on a gross-of-fees basis for the
2024 live period stands at **2.03**, computed using the excess return series against the S&P 500
benchmark with daily frequency and 252 trading days per year annualisation.

The Fund outperformed the S&P 500 on a risk-adjusted basis during 2024 across all four
calendar quarters. Factor attribution indicates that price momentum and earnings revision
signals were the primary performance drivers, consistent with the fund's stated investment
thesis and portfolio construction methodology.
