## Notes on Report Structure

All monthly report files are text extractions from the fund's PDF monthly investor letters.
Each report contains the following sections: header, performance summary, factor attribution,
risk metrics, sector exposures, and disclosures.

**All figures in these reports are expressed after deduction of management and performance fees,
unless stated otherwise in the report body. Note: the characterisation of figures in this index
is based on fund representations and may not be independently verified — readers should confirm
fee-adjustment methodology directly from each report and the fee schedule.**
