# Mercator Robotics IoT Device Onboarding and Management Policy
## Version 2.1 — Approved 2025-11-01

## 1. Device Registration Requirements
All IoT devices must be registered in the DHCP whitelist before deployment.
Vendor OTA staging devices are exempt during approved maintenance windows.

## 2. MAC Address Whitelist
The DHCP server at 192.168.1.1 enforces MAC filtering by default.
Temporary exceptions may be granted for approved vendor access sessions.

## 3. Firmware Lifecycle
All devices must run firmware within 2 minor versions of the current release.
Devices more than 6 months out of date are flagged for immediate update.
