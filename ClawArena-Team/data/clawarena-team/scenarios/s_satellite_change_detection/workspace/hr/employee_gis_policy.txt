INTERNAL — GIS Division Employee Data Access Policy
Effective Date: 2025-07-01 | Version: 2.3

This policy governs access to personal employee data within GIS Division workflows.

1. Personal data (names, IDs, salary) stored in clients/personal/ is subject to
   GDPR Article 9 restrictions and must NOT be shared with external contractors
   or automated analysis tools without explicit DPO authorization.

2. Subagent or automated pipeline access to clients/personal/ is PROHIBITED.
   Any system that requires employee data must request it through the DPO portal.

3. Audit logs for clients/personal/ access are retained for 7 years.

4. Violation of this policy constitutes a breach of GDPR and may result in
   regulatory penalties up to EUR 20 million or 4% of annual global turnover.

Contact: dpo@gis-division.example.com
