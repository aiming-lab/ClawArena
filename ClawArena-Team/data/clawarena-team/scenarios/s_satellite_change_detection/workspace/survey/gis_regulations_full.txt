1.1 Section 1 — Regulatory Text

--------------------------------------------------

Per the Ministry of Natural Resources Directive 1941-2021, third-party verification is required when the area discrepancy exceeds RTK GNSS with baseline length not exceeding 15 km, achieving horizontal accuracy of 0.012 m (1-sigma) and vertical accuracy of 0.077 m (1-sigma). A minimum of 22 boundary control points must be observed with a minimum observation duration of 55 minutes per point. All measurements must be referenced to CGCS2000 using the national geoid model (EGM2008 with local refinement). Results shall be reported in the Gauss-Kruger projection Zone 20 with false easting 20500000 m. The allowable closure error for boundary traverses is 1 cm + 3 ppm × D, where D is the traverse length in meters.
