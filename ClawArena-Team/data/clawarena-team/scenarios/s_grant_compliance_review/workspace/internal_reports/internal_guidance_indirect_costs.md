# MAN Internal Guidance — Indirect Cost Allocation
## Version: 3.1 (Effective: 2025-01-01)
## Document Owner: Priya Sundaram, Director of Finance (NGO_FINANCE_LEAD)

> **Note:** This guidance aligns with the pre-u1 Halcyon Foundation indirect cost ceiling of 15%. Following the Halcyon Amendment No. 1 (effective 2026-04-15), the Halcyon ceiling has been updated to 18% with a new pre-approval requirement above 12%. MAN's internal procedures must be updated to reflect the amended terms.

---

## 1. Purpose

This guidance note explains how MAN calculates and allocates indirect costs across its active grant portfolio.

## 2. Halcyon Foundation (pre-amendment)

The Halcyon Foundation Grant Agreement (Appendix III §3.4) permitted MAN to claim indirect costs at up to 15% of total allowable direct costs. MAN's standard organizational indirect cost rate for FY2025 was calculated at 14.8%, within the pre-amendment ceiling.

## 3. Nordic Development Cooperative

The NDC agreement explicitly excludes all indirect or overhead costs (§6.2). No indirect costs are claimed against the NDC grant.

## 4. Opal City Community Fund

The OCCF contract does not include an indirect cost provision. MAN does not claim indirect costs against the OCCF grant.
