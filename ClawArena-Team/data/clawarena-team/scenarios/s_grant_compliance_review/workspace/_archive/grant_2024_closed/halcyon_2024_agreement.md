## Appendix III — Indirect Cost Policy (FY2024)

### Section 3.4 — Indirect Cost Rate Ceiling (FY2024)

Grantees may claim indirect costs at a rate not exceeding twelve percent (12%) of total allowable direct costs for the FY2024 grant period. This rate applies only to FY2024 and is not indicative of any future grant terms.
