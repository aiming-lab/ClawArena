## Recitals

WHEREAS, the Halcyon Foundation (hereinafter "Foundation") is a private philanthropic organization established for the purpose of supporting sustainable development programmes;

WHEREAS, Meridian Aid Network (hereinafter "Grantee") is a non-governmental organization with demonstrated expertise in delivering health, education, and community development programmes;

WHEREAS, the Foundation wishes to support Grantee's FY2025 programmatic activities subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Foundation and the Grantee agree as follows:

## Part I — General Terms

### §1. Definitions

1.1 "Grant Funds" means the financial resources provided by the Foundation to the Grantee pursuant to this Agreement.
1.2 "Grant Period" means the period from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025, inclusive.
1.3 "Approved Budget" means the budget attached hereto as Appendix I, as may be amended by mutual written agreement.
1.4 "Eligible Expenditure" means expenditure that falls within the categories listed in §4 and that complies with the documentation requirements of §6.
1.5 "Programme Activities" means the activities described in Appendix II — Programme Description.
1.6 "Programme Officer" means Felix Hartmann (GRANTOR_A_OFFICER), or such other officer as the Foundation may designate in writing.
1.7 "Reporting Periods" means the quarterly periods for financial reporting as set out in §8.

### §2. Grant Obligations

2.1 The Foundation shall disburse Grant Funds in accordance with the disbursement schedule in Appendix I.
2.2 The Grantee shall use Grant Funds solely for Eligible Expenditure in furtherance of Programme Activities.
2.3 The Grantee shall maintain accurate and complete financial records of all Grant Fund expenditure.
2.4 Any unspent Grant Funds at the close of the Grant Period shall be returned to the Foundation within thirty (30) days unless otherwise agreed in writing.
2.5 The Grantee shall not transfer, assign, or sub-grant any portion of the Grant Funds without the prior written consent of the Foundation.

### §3. Financial Management Standards

3.1 The Grantee shall maintain a dedicated project bank account for all Grant Fund transactions.
3.2 All transactions must be supported by original documentation and approved by authorized signatories designated in the Grantee's internal procedures.
3.3 The Grantee's external auditors shall conduct an annual audit of Grant Fund expenditure, and the audit report shall be submitted to the Foundation within ninety (90) days of the Grant Period close.
3.4 — Indirect Cost Recovery: Grantees may claim indirect costs at a rate not exceeding fifteen percent (15%) of total allowable direct costs for the grant period. Claims must be accompanied by a completed indirect cost schedule. This provision applies to all expenditure incurred during the FY2025 grant period.
3.5 Budget reallocation requests exceeding 10% of any budget line require prior written approval from the Programme Officer before implementation.
3.6 Foreign currency transactions shall be converted to USD at the prevailing mid-market rate on the date of expenditure.

## Part II — Eligible Expenditure

### §4. Eligible Expenditure Categories

4.1 **Travel**
4.1.a Domestic and international travel costs incurred by MAN staff in furtherance of approved Programme Activities are eligible for reimbursement.
4.1.b International travel requires prior written approval from the Programme Officer (GRANTOR_A_OFFICER) before expenditure is incurred. Approval requests must be submitted at least ten (10) business days before the planned travel date.
4.1.c Economy class airfares are eligible; business class airfares are eligible only for journeys exceeding eight (8) hours of flying time.
4.1.d Travel reimbursement is subject to per diem rates published in Appendix IV.

4.2 **Accommodation**
4.2.a Hotel accommodation costs for approved programme travel are eligible at actual cost, subject to the per diem rates in Appendix IV.
4.2.b Receipts must identify the traveller, the date range, and the venue.

4.3 **Training and Capacity Building**
4.3.a Training materials, course fees, and facilitation costs for programme-related training events are eligible.
4.3.b Training events must be documented with a signed attendance list and a post-event report submitted to the Programme Officer within fifteen (15) days.

4.4 **Catering and Hospitality**
4.4.a Catering costs are eligible only at formally organized programme events with an approved event agenda.
4.4.b Catering at non-programme events, including purely social or staff functions, is not eligible.
4.4.c Catering costs must be supported by a vendor invoice and a copy of the event agenda.

4.5 **Stationery and Consumables**
4.5.a Stationery, printing, and consumables directly related to programme activities are eligible.

4.6 **Equipment**
4.6.a Equipment purchases necessary for programme implementation are eligible without a per-item cap under this Agreement.
4.6.b All equipment purchased with Grant Funds must be recorded in MAN's asset register.
4.6.c Disposal of grant-funded equipment requires prior written approval from the Programme Officer.

4.7 **Staff Costs**
4.7.a Salaries and benefits for staff whose roles are directly funded under the Approved Budget are eligible.
4.7.b Staff costs must be supported by certified time-and-effort records.

4.8 **Indirect Costs**
4.8.a Indirect costs are eligible subject to the ceiling set out in §3.4 and Appendix III.

### §5. Non-Eligible Expenditure

5.1 The following expenditure categories are not eligible for reimbursement regardless of circumstances:
5.1.a Fines, penalties, and interest charges.
5.1.b Capital expenditure on land or buildings.
5.1.c Expenditure incurred outside the Grant Period.
5.1.d Contributions in kind.
5.1.e Entertainment expenses at non-programme events.
5.1.f Expenditure not supported by original documentation.

## Part III — Documentation Requirements

### §6. Documentation Standards

6.1 All expenditure must be supported by original vendor invoices or receipts.
6.2 Invoices must bear the vendor name, date, a description of goods or services, and the amount charged.
6.3 For travel, supporting documentation must include the approved travel request, boarding passes (or equivalent), and per diem claims certified by the traveller and their line manager.
6.4 For equipment purchases, documentation must include the purchase order, invoice, and evidence of delivery.
6.5 For training, documentation must include the training agenda, attendance list, and trainer invoice.
6.6 For programme events including catering, documentation must include the event agenda and a signed attendance list.

### §7. Procurement

7.1 All procurement above USD 5,000 must follow MAN's competitive tendering procedures as described in Appendix V.
7.2 Sole-source procurement above USD 5,000 requires a written justification approved by the Finance Director.
7.3 Procurement records must be retained for the full document retention period.

10.2 The Foundation may require remediation of non-compliant expenditure, including repayment of ineligible amounts.

## Appendix II — Programme Description

The FY2025 Halcyon-funded programme focuses on community health and economic inclusion activities in Nairobi, Kenya and surrounding regions. Programme activities include:

1. Community health worker training (target: 300 workers trained).
2. Mobile health clinic operations (target: 12 clinic days per quarter).
3. Economic inclusion workshops for women entrepreneurs (target: 150 participants).
4. Stakeholder convenings with national health ministry representatives.
5. Documentation and dissemination of programme learnings.

## Appendix III — Indirect Cost Policy

### Section 3.1 — Scope

This Appendix governs the recovery of indirect (overhead) costs by the Grantee under this Agreement. Indirect costs include administrative overheads, shared facility costs, and other costs not directly attributable to a specific programme activity.

### Section 3.2 — Eligible Indirect Cost Components

Eligible indirect cost components include:
- A proportional share of executive leadership salaries for time allocated to programme oversight.
- Office rent and utilities allocated on a programme-space basis.
- Shared IT infrastructure and communications costs.
- Finance and HR staff costs allocated on a programme-headcount basis.
- Legal and compliance costs directly incurred in furtherance of programme activities.

### Section 3.3 — Documentation Requirements

The Grantee must maintain a complete indirect cost schedule supporting the claimed rate, including:
- The total direct cost base for the period.
- An itemized breakdown of indirect cost components.
- The calculation methodology for allocating shared costs.
- Certification by the Finance Director that the schedule is accurate.

### Section 3.4 — Indirect Cost Rate Ceiling

Section 3.4 — Indirect Cost Recovery: Grantees may claim indirect costs at a rate not exceeding fifteen percent (15%) of total allowable direct costs for the grant period. Claims must be accompanied by a completed indirect cost schedule. This provision applies to all expenditure incurred during the FY2025 grant period.

### Section 3.5 — Submission Process

The indirect cost schedule must be submitted to the Programme Officer as an annex to the final financial report. The Foundation may request additional documentation to verify the claimed rate.
