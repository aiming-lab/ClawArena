# Opal City Community Fund — Grant Contract FY2025
## Contract Reference: OCCF-MAN-2025-014

**Grantor:** Opal City Community Fund (OCCF), administered by the Opal City Office of Community Development
**Grantee:** Meridian Aid Network (MAN)
**Grants Administrator:** Darnell Cross (GRANTOR_C_OFFICER), Grants Administrator
**Finance Lead (Grantee):** Priya Sundaram, Director of Finance (NGO_FINANCE_LEAD)
**Grant Amount:** USD 195,000
**Contract Period:** 15 January 2025 — 14 January 2026
**Date of Execution:** 2025-01-08

---

## Article 1 — Purpose and Scope

1.1 This Contract governs the disbursement and use of grant funds provided by the Opal City Community Fund (OCCF) to Meridian Aid Network (MAN) for the implementation of community health and civic engagement activities within Opal City.
1.2 All programme activities must take place within Opal City boundaries or directly serve Opal City residents.
1.3 Darnell Cross (GRANTOR_C_OFFICER) is the primary contact for all compliance matters.

## Article 2 — Eligible Expenditure

2.1 Staff salaries and benefits for staff allocated to OCCF-funded activities per the approved staffing plan.
2.2 Office supplies and stationery directly related to programme implementation.
2.3 Training costs for programme staff, including course fees and materials (§3.2).
2.4 Venue hire and event logistics for community-facing programme activities.
2.5 Excluded categories: entertainment expenses of any nature are excluded from OCCF grant eligibility. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the event is described as a programme activity. Entertainment includes but is not limited to: social gatherings, celebratory events, hospitality beyond the functional minimum for programme delivery, and gifts to beneficiaries or third parties.

## Article 3 — Equipment and Supplies

3.1 Office supplies and consumables: eligible at actual cost with receipt.
3.2 Training materials and resources: eligible at actual cost.
3.3 Community programme supplies: eligible where directly tied to a specific approved programme activity.
3.4 Equipment: eligible for individual items up to a maximum of USD 500 per item. Items above the USD 500 per-item threshold are not eligible without prior written approval from GRANTOR_C_OFFICER. Requests for approval must include a written justification and at least two quotes from independent vendors.

## Article 4 — Travel

4.1 Travel reimbursement under this Contract is restricted to domestic travel within the United States. International travel is not an eligible expenditure under any circumstances.
4.2 Domestic travel must be pre-approved by the Grantee's Finance Director and documented with travel receipts.
4.3 Mileage reimbursement for vehicle travel within Opal City shall be at the current IRS standard mileage rate.
4.4 Public transportation costs within Opal City are eligible at actual cost with receipt.

## Article 5 — Indirect Costs

5.1 Indirect or overhead costs are not listed as an eligible category under this Contract and are accordingly not reimbursable without an explicit written amendment signed by GRANTOR_C_OFFICER.

## Article 6 — Documentation Requirements

6.1 All eligible expenditure must be supported by original receipts, invoices, or equivalent documentation.
6.2 Staff time allocated to OCCF-funded activities must be documented with certified time-and-effort records.
6.3 Programme events must be documented with a sign-in sheet and a post-event narrative summary.
6.4 Procurement above USD 1,000 must be supported by evidence of at least three price comparisons or quotes.

## Article 7 — Reporting

7.1 The Grantee shall submit a mid-year progress and financial report by 15 July 2025.
7.2 The final programme and financial report is due within sixty (60) days of the Contract Period close.
7.3 Reports must be submitted using OCCF's standard reporting format to Darnell Cross (GRANTOR_C_OFFICER).

## Article 8 — Audit and Compliance

8.1 OCCF reserves the right to audit grant expenditure at any time during or after the Contract Period.
8.2 The Grantee shall maintain all financial records and supporting documentation for a minimum of five (5) years.
8.3 Any non-compliance identified during audit must be remediated within thirty (30) days of notification.
8.4 OCCF may recover non-eligible expenditure from future disbursements or require direct repayment.

## Article 9 — Standard Contract Terms

9.1 This Contract incorporates the City of Opal City's standard grant terms and conditions by reference.
9.2 The Grantee must comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws.
9.3 The Grantee must maintain general liability insurance coverage throughout the Contract Period.
9.4 The Grantee must maintain a non-discrimination policy consistent with Opal City ordinance.
9.5 Any modification to this Contract must be made by written amendment signed by both parties.
