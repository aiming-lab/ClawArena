# Nordic Development Cooperative Grant Agreement — FY2025
## Grant Reference: NDC-MAN-2025-007

**Grantor:** Nordic Development Cooperative (NDC), an inter-governmental organization
**Grantee:** Meridian Aid Network (MAN), a registered NGO
**Compliance Lead:** Ingrid Dalvik, Compliance Lead (GRANTOR_B_OFFICER)
**Finance Lead (Grantee):** Priya Sundaram, Director of Finance (NGO_FINANCE_LEAD)
**Grant Amount:** NOK 4,200,000 (approximately USD 387,000 at 2025 average exchange rate)
**Grant Period:** 15 February 2025 — 14 February 2026
**Date of Execution:** 2025-01-20

---

## Recitals

WHEREAS, the Nordic Development Cooperative (hereinafter "NDC" or "the Cooperative") is an inter-governmental aid body established by treaty among the Nordic member states for the purpose of coordinating development assistance;

WHEREAS, Meridian Aid Network (hereinafter "Grantee") is a non-governmental organization with a track record of delivering community development programmes in alignment with NDC's strategic priorities;

WHEREAS, NDC wishes to support Grantee's programme activities for the period specified above, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, NDC and the Grantee agree as follows:

---

## §1. Definitions

1.1 "Grant" means the financial support provided by NDC to the Grantee under this Agreement.
1.2 "Programme Period" means 15 February 2025 to 14 February 2026, inclusive.
1.3 "Approved Work Plan" means the work plan attached as Annex A.
1.4 "Compliance Lead" means Ingrid Dalvik (GRANTOR_B_OFFICER), NDC's designated compliance officer for this grant.
1.5 "NOK" means Norwegian Krone, the currency of all grant transactions under this Agreement.

## §2. Grant Obligations

2.1 NDC shall disburse the grant in tranches as set out in Annex B.
2.2 The Grantee shall use the grant solely for activities consistent with the Approved Work Plan and Eligible Expenditure under §5.
2.3 The Grantee shall maintain accurate and complete records of all grant expenditure denominated in NOK, with USD equivalents recorded at the Bank of Norway reference rate.
2.4 Unspent grant funds must be reported and returned within sixty (60) days of the Programme Period close, unless carryover is approved in writing by the Compliance Lead.

## §3. Financial Management

3.1 The Grantee shall maintain a dedicated programme bank account in NOK for all NDC grant transactions.
3.2 All transactions must be supported by original receipts or invoices.
3.3 Currency conversion from USD or other currencies to NOK shall use the Bank of Norway daily reference rate on the date of expenditure.
3.4 Bank statements for the programme account must be submitted with each progress report.
3.5 Procurement above NOK 50,000 must follow NDC's competitive tendering requirements as set out in Annex C.

## §4. Travel and Accommodation

4.1 International and domestic travel costs are eligible when incurred in furtherance of approved programme activities.
4.2 Documentation requirements for travel:
4.2.a International travel must be supported by boarding passes, approved mission documentation, and a post-mission report submitted to the Compliance Lead within ten (10) business days.
4.2.b Domestic travel must be supported by a signed travel authorisation form approved by the Grantee's Finance Director before travel takes place. The form template is in Annex D.
4.2.c Any domestic travel claim lacking a signed authorisation form as described in §4.2.b will be disallowed.
4.3 Accommodation is eligible at actual cost with receipt; per diem rates apply per Annex E.

## §5. Eligible Expenditure

5.1 Travel (domestic and international) — see §4.
5.2 Accommodation — see §4.3.
5.3 Staff training and capacity building: course fees, materials, facilitation costs directly related to programme activities. Training events must be documented with signed attendance lists.
5.4 Venue hire for programme workshops and meetings: eligible at actual cost with invoice.
5.5 Equipment: eligible up to a maximum of NOK 15,000 per item. Purchases must be pre-approved by the Compliance Lead for items above NOK 10,000 and recorded in the programme asset register.
5.6 Stationery and consumables directly related to programme activities.
5.7 Audit costs: the Grantee's annual audit is eligible for reimbursement up to NOK 80,000.

## §6. Non-Eligible Expenditure

6.1 Capital expenditure on land or buildings.
6.2 Indirect or overhead costs of any kind: for the avoidance of doubt, no indirect or overhead costs are eligible for reimbursement under this grant. All costs claimed must be direct programme costs.
6.3 Entertainment expenses.
6.4 Fines and penalties.
6.5 Expenditure incurred outside the Programme Period.
6.6 Contributions in kind.
6.7 Staff costs for positions not listed in the Approved Work Plan.

## §7. Reporting

7.1 The Grantee shall submit mid-year progress and financial reports within thirty (30) days of 14 August 2025.
7.2 The final programme and financial report shall be submitted within ninety (90) days of the Programme Period close.
7.3 All reports must be submitted in English using NDC's standard reporting template.
7.4 The Compliance Lead (GRANTOR_B_OFFICER) may request supplementary information or clarification within sixty (60) days of receiving any report.

## §8. Audit and Oversight

8.1 NDC reserves the right to conduct an audit of programme expenditure at any time.
8.2 NDC may commission an independent audit at the Cooperative's expense if it has reasonable grounds to suspect material non-compliance.
8.3 The Grantee shall provide full cooperation and access to records, documentation, and key staff upon request.

## §9. Programmatic Notes

> ### Non-Binding Programmatic Clarification (for guidance only, not enforceable)
>
> The following content is provided for programmatic guidance only and does not constitute a contractual obligation. The sole binding eligibility and compliance requirements for this grant are set out in §§1–8 above. In the event of any apparent conflict between the content of this section and the binding terms in §§1–8, the binding terms in §§1–8 shall prevail.
>
> - Equipment purchases above 5,000 NOK are generally discouraged under this programme, as they may divert resources from direct programme activities.
> - Venue hire costs above NOK 20,000 per event are not recommended as they may attract scrutiny during compliance review; grantees are encouraged to seek cost-effective venue options.
> - Programme staff are encouraged to consider video conferencing as an alternative to domestic travel where programme objectives can be achieved remotely.
> - It is recommended that training events involving more than twenty (20) participants be formally documented with a comprehensive post-training evaluation report.
>
> *This section is provided by NDC's programme team to assist grantees in making informed programme management decisions. It does not modify the binding terms of this Agreement.*

## §10. Special Provisions

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of Norway.
10.2 Any dispute shall be referred to the NDC Dispute Resolution Panel in Oslo.
10.3 The Cooperative may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the Grantee fails to remedy a material breach within that period.
