Dear Priya,

I want to flag something important regarding the NDC programme agreement. As you review the agreement in preparation for the FY2025 compliance audit, please note that §9 of the agreement contains a section explicitly labeled as a non-binding programmatic clarification. The heading of that section reads "Non-Binding Programmatic Clarification (for guidance only, not enforceable)."

I want to be absolutely clear: the content of §9 should not be treated as a contractual eligibility constraint. The binding eligibility and compliance requirements are set out exclusively in §§1–8 of the agreement. If there is any apparent tension between guidance in §9 and the binding terms in §§1–8, the binding terms prevail.

This is a standing clarification that applies throughout the audit period. Please ensure that any compliance assessment of NDC-funded transactions uses only §§1–8 as the source of binding requirements.

Best regards,
