Dear Priya,

I am writing to formally notify you of Amendment No. 1 to the Halcyon Foundation Grant Agreement HF-MAN-2025-001.

Following the Foundation's administrative harmonization review across our grantee portfolio, we are updating the indirect cost recovery provision in Appendix III §3.4. The key changes are as follows:

1. **Ceiling increase:** The indirect cost rate ceiling is increased from fifteen percent (15%) to eighteen percent (18%) of total allowable direct costs.

2. **New pre-approval requirement:** All indirect cost claims above twelve percent (12%) of direct costs now require prior written approval from the assigned Program Officer (i.e., myself) before expenditure. This is a new procedural requirement introduced by this amendment.

3. **Retroactive applicability:** This amendment is effective retroactively for the FY2025 reporting period. This means that when you complete your FY2025 compliance assessment, you should apply the eighteen percent (18%) ceiling, not the previous fifteen percent (15%) ceiling.

The amended Appendix III §3.4 text is as follows:

> Section 3.4 — Indirect Cost Recovery: Grantees may claim indirect costs at a rate not exceeding eighteen percent (18%) of total allowable direct costs for the grant period. Indirect cost claims above twelve percent (12%) of direct costs require prior written approval from the assigned Halcyon Program Officer before expenditure. This provision applies retroactively to the FY2025 grant period.

Please update your records and apply these revised terms in your FY2025 compliance assessment. I have attached the revised Appendix III for your files (reflected in the updated `grant_agreements/halcyon_agreement_2025.md`).

Please confirm receipt of this amendment.
