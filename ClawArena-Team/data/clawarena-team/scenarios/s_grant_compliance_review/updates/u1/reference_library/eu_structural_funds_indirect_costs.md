# EU Structural Funds — Indirect Cost Rate Guidance
## Comparative Reference for NGO Multi-Grantor Programmes

**Note:** Synthesized reference document. Does not supersede any binding grant agreement.

---

## Overview

The European Union's Structural Fund regulations provide a range of options for NGO grantees to recover indirect costs. These options are cited here as comparative context for NGOs managing grants from a variety of public and philanthropic sources.

---

## Indirect Cost Recovery Models

### Model 1: Flat Rate (2% of eligible direct costs)
A simplified flat-rate option designed for smaller grants. Documentation requirements are minimal.

### Model 2: Flat Rate (25% of direct staff costs)
A widely used flat-rate option that simplifies overhead allocation by tying it to staff costs only.

### Model 3: Actual Indirect Costs
Full recovery of actual indirect costs based on an approved indirect cost methodology. Subject to audit verification.
