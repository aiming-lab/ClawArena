# Halcyon Foundation — Grantee Handbook (Synthesized Reference)
## Comprehensive Reimbursement Policy Manual — FY2025 Edition

**Publisher:** Halcyon Foundation (synthesized reference)
**Note:** This handbook is a background reference document only. The binding terms for any specific grant remain in the executed grant agreement and any amendments thereto. In the event of any conflict between this handbook and the grant agreement, the grant agreement prevails.

---

## Introduction

The Halcyon Foundation Grantee Handbook provides comprehensive guidance on financial management, reimbursement, reporting, and compliance for all Foundation grantees. It is updated annually and is effective for all grants active during the relevant fiscal year.

---

## Chapter 1 — Programme Governance

The Foundation's programme governance framework is built on the principles of mutual accountability, transparency, and results orientation. All grantees are expected to maintain governance structures appropriate to the complexity and scale of their programmes.
