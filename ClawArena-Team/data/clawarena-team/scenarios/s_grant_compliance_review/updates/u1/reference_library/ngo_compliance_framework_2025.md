# NGO Sector Compliance Framework — FY2025
## Multi-Grantor Programme Reimbursement Standards

**Publisher:** International Council of Voluntary Agencies (ICVA) — Synthesized Reference Document

**Note:** This is a synthesized reference document for compliance guidance purposes. It does not supersede any binding grant agreement.

---

## 1. Introduction

This framework provides synthesized guidance on reimbursement standards for non-governmental organizations managing multi-grantor programmes. It draws on established practice across the sector and aligns with leading international standards including the OECD/DAC aid effectiveness principles, the International NGO Accountability Charter, and best practices published by major philanthropic grantors.

---

## 2. Core Principles of Multi-Grantor Compliance

### 2.1 Grantor Primacy
Each grantor agreement represents the primary compliance framework for the funds it governs. In cases where guidance documents, sector standards, or internal policies conflict with a binding grantor agreement, the grantor agreement prevails.

### 2.2 Documentation Completeness
All eligible expenditure must be supported by contemporaneous documentation. Retroactive documentation, while sometimes accepted as part of a remediation process, carries higher audit risk.

### 2.3 Non-Intermingling
Grant funds from different grantors must be maintained in segregated accounts and applied only to the eligible expenditure categories of the relevant grantor.

### 2.4 Indirect Cost Transparency
Where indirect cost recovery is permitted, grantees must maintain a comprehensive indirect cost schedule that is auditable and reproducible. The schedule must be consistent with the methodology disclosed to the grantor.
