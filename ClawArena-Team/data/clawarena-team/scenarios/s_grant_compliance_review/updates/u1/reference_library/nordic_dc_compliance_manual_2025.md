# Nordic Development Cooperative — Compliance Operations Manual FY2025
## Synthesized Reference for Grantee Compliance Teams

**Note:** Synthesized reference document. Does not supersede any binding grant agreement.

---

## Introduction

The Nordic Development Cooperative Compliance Operations Manual provides detailed guidance on programme compliance monitoring, financial management, and reporting for NDC-funded organizations.

---

## Chapter 1 — Compliance Principles

### 1.1 Results-Based Management
NDC's compliance approach emphasizes results-based accountability. Financial compliance is assessed in the context of programme results delivery.

### 1.2 No Overhead Policy
NDC's no overhead/indirect cost policy reflects the Cooperative's mandate to maximize direct programme impact. All costs claimed against NDC grants must be direct programme costs with a clear programme nexus.
