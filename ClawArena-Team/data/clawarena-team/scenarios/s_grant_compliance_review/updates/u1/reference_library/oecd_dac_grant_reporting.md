# OECD/DAC Principles for Grant Reporting Transparency — Synthesized Reference
## Applicable to Multi-Grantor Aid Programmes

**Note:** Synthesized reference document. Does not supersede any binding grant agreement.

---

## Introduction

The OECD Development Assistance Committee (DAC) has established principles for transparency in official development assistance reporting. While primarily applicable to bilateral and multilateral donors, these principles inform best practices for all aid-funded NGO programmes.

---

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: Comprehensiveness
All expenditure from aid-funded programmes must be reported, including both direct programme costs and shared administrative costs allocated to the programme.

### Principle 2: Comparability
Reporting should use consistent categories across reporting periods to enable year-on-year comparison.

### Principle 3: Timeliness
Reports should be submitted within agreed timeframes to enable grantor monitoring and portfolio management.
