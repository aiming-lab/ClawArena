# Halcyon Foundation — Indirect Cost Justification Form (ICJF) Documentation Standards

**Version:** 1.0 (Effective: 2026-04-15, per Amendment No. 1)
**Note:** Synthesized reference. Applicable to all MAN indirect cost claims above 12% of direct costs.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Indirect Cost Justification Form (ICJF) is required for all indirect cost claims above twelve percent (12%) of total direct costs under any Halcyon Foundation grant. It provides a structured record of the basis for claiming indirect costs above the twelve percent threshold.

## 2. Required Content

A completed ICJF must include:

1. Grant reference number.
2. Reporting period.
3. Total direct costs (USD) for the period.
4. Claimed indirect cost amount (USD) and rate (%).
5. Methodology used to allocate indirect costs (e.g., percentage of direct staff, headcount allocation, square footage).
6. Itemized breakdown of indirect cost components.
7. Explanation of why the claimed rate exceeds 12%.
8. Certification by the Finance Director that the information is accurate and complete.

## 3. Filing Requirements

The ICJF must be filed with MAN's finance records and submitted to the Halcyon Programme Officer with the final financial report. Retroactive filing is accepted during the FY2025 transition period subject to Programme Officer approval.
