# NGO Audit Response Protocols — Sector Guidance
## Responding to Compliance Audit Findings from Grantors

**Publisher:** Synthesized sector guidance
**Note:** Does not supersede any binding grant agreement.

---

## 1. Overview

When grantors identify non-compliance findings during an audit, NGOs must respond promptly, professionally, and constructively. This guidance provides a framework for managing the audit response process.

---

## 2. Initial Response Protocol

### 2.1 Acknowledgment
Upon receiving an audit finding, acknowledge receipt within five (5) business days. Acknowledge without admitting fault unless the finding is clearly correct.

### 2.2 Review and Assessment
Review each finding carefully against the relevant grant terms. Distinguish between:
- **Substantive non-compliance**: actual violation of an eligibility or documentation requirement.
- **Technical non-compliance**: procedural or documentation gap without underlying financial ineligibility.
- **Disputed findings**: items where the grantee believes the finding is incorrect or based on a misreading of the terms.

### 2.3 Management Response
Prepare a formal management response for each finding, addressing:
- Whether the finding is accepted, partially accepted, or disputed.
- The remediation plan and timeline.
- Any contextual information that explains the circumstances of the non-compliance.

---

## 3. Waiver Requests

Where a grantee believes a non-compliance finding should be waived (e.g., transition exception, documentation-only violation, force majeure), a formal waiver request must be submitted to the Programme Officer with supporting documentation.
