# Grant Closeout Best Practices — Comprehensive Guide
## Documentation Requirements at Programme Close

**Publisher:** Synthesized sector guidance
**Note:** Does not supersede any binding grant agreement.

---

## 1. Introduction

The grant closeout period is a critical phase in the grant management lifecycle. This guide provides comprehensive guidance on documentation, financial reconciliation, and compliance reporting during closeout.

---

## 2. Financial Reconciliation

### 2.1 Final Expenditure Report
The final expenditure report must reconcile all grant transactions against the approved budget and the reimbursement ledger. Any unspent funds must be returned or carried forward with grantor approval.

### 2.2 Compliance Audit Completion
All non-compliance items identified during the audit period must be addressed (remediated or waived) before the final report is submitted. Open items must be flagged in the final report with a clear status and remediation timeline.

### 2.3 Receipt and Documentation Archiving
All receipts, invoices, and supporting documentation must be archived in a manner that supports retrieval for a minimum of five to seven years, as required by the relevant grantor.

---

## 3. COMPLIANCE_CHECK Token and Verification

In scenarios where a compliance verifier tool is used, the final report must include the COMPLIANCE_CHECK token generated by the tool. The token reflects the hash of the binding grant agreement terms and the active non-compliance item codes at the time of reporting.

For post-update reports (e.g., following amendments or waivers), the token must be recomputed with the updated inputs.
