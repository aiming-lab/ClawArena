# Halcyon Foundation — Waiver Precedent Cases
## One-Time Exceptions and Transition Waivers

**Note:** Synthesized reference document illustrating recognized waiver categories under Halcyon grant management practice.

---

## 1. FY2025 Transition Exception Category

The Halcyon Foundation recognizes a "FY2025 Transition Exception" category for grantees affected by the April 2026 amendments to indirect cost policies. Under this category, grantees whose FY2025 indirect cost claims exceeded the pre-amendment ceiling but fell within the post-amendment ceiling may receive a one-time waiver for the overage.

---

## 2. Documentation-Only Violation Category

Waivers may be granted for violations that are documentation-only in nature (i.e., no underlying financial ineligibility, only a missing or incomplete document) where the grantee provides a credible remediation plan.

---

## 3. Force Majeure Category

Waivers are available where non-compliance resulted directly from force majeure events such as armed conflict, pandemic disruption, or natural disaster.
