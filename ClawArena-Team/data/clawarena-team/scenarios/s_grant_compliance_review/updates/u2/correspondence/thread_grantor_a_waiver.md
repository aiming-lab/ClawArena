Dear Priya,

I am writing to follow up on the FY2025 compliance report you submitted last month. I have two matters to address.

**1. Formal Waiver of NC-A-001 (Transaction GRA-0041)**

I am writing to formally waive non-compliance finding NC-A-001 (transaction GRA-0041, indirect cost recovery at 18.5%) as a one-time FY2025 transition exception, effective immediately.

This waiver is granted as a one-time FY2025 transition exception, recognizing that the amendment to Appendix III §3.4 (Amendment No. 1, dated 2026-04-15) was communicated after the expenditure period had already closed. The Foundation acknowledges that MAN could not have anticipated the new ceiling at the time the expenditure was incurred.

Please update your compliance report to reflect that NC-A-001 (GRA-0041) is now classified as resolved/waived. The waiver is effective from the date of this letter.

**2. New Documentation Requirement: Indirect Cost Justification Form (ICJF)**

Notwithstanding the above waiver, going forward (and retroactive to FY2025 for documentation purposes), all indirect cost claims above twelve percent (12%) of direct costs must be supported by a completed Indirect Cost Justification Form (ICJF) on file with MAN's finance function.

The ICJF must document:
- The methodology used to calculate the indirect cost rate.
- A breakdown of indirect cost components allocated to the relevant grant.
- Certification by the Finance Director.

I am asking you to confirm whether MAN has an ICJF on file for transaction GRA-0041. If MAN does not currently have this form on file, this constitutes a documentation gap under the amended §3.4 and should be identified as a new non-compliance item in your FY2025 report (suggested code: NC-A-004, to follow sequentially from your existing NC-A-003).

Please update the compliance report accordingly and provide a remediation plan for the ICJF gap.
