## Case Study 1 — Two-Hospital Cardiac Merger (Northeast Region, 2022)

### Background
Two regional hospitals merged their cardiac care centers following accreditation
pressure to consolidate governance under a single Director of Cardiology.

### Staffing Outcome
Tier-1 headcount before merger: 89 combined. After role rationalization: 82 FTE.
The reduction was achieved through attrition over 18 months, with no forced layoffs.

### Equipment Outcome
Duplicate devices identified by serial number: 19. All 19 were disposed of via
the regional equipment authority's auction process. Internal transfer was considered
and rejected due to union opposition.

### Org-Chart Outcome
The director with the shorter reporting chain to the CMO was designated as the
unified Director of Cardiology, consistent with accreditation preference for
direct CMO reporting.
