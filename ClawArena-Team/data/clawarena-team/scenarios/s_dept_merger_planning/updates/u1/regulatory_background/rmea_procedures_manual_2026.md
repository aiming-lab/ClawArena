# Regional Medical Equipment Authority — Procedures Manual 2026

**Document type:** Regulatory procedures manual
**Issuing authority:** Regional Medical Equipment Authority (RMEA)
**Edition:** 2026
**Applies to:** All healthcare institutions in the Meridian regional health network

---

## Chapter 1 — RMEA Mandate and Jurisdiction

The Regional Medical Equipment Authority (RMEA) is a statutory body established
to oversee the orderly disposition of medically redundant clinical equipment
within the regional health system. Its mandate includes:
- Preventing unsafe or redundant equipment from re-entering clinical use
- Ensuring fair market valuation of publicly-funded medical assets
- Maintaining a public registry of disposed medical equipment
- Administering the regulated auction process for Class-II medical devices

---

## Chapter 2 — Device Classification

For the purposes of RMEA jurisdiction, cardiac devices are classified as follows:

- **Class-I:** Low-risk devices (e.g., tongue depressors, bandages). Not subject
  to RMEA auction requirements.
- **Class-II:** Moderate-risk devices requiring performance standards (e.g.,
  defibrillators, cardiac monitors, echocardiographs, EP recording systems).
  **Subject to RMEA auction requirements when identified as redundant duplicates.**
- **Class-III:** High-risk devices requiring premarket approval. Subject to
  separate RMEA review process.
