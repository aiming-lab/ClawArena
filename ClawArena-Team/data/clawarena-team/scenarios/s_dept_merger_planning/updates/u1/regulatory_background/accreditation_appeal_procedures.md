# Joint Commission Accreditation Decision Appeal Procedures

**Document type:** Procedural reference
**Issuing body:** The Joint Commission
**Applicable to:** All accredited healthcare facilities

---

## Overview

This document sets out the procedures for appealing Joint Commission accreditation
decisions, including Requirements for Improvement (RFIs) and Provisional
Accreditation determinations.

---

## Chapter 1 — Grounds for Appeal

The Parties represent and warrant that they have full corporate authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform their obligations hereunder. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the applicable jurisdiction, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by the waiving Party. All workforce decisions arising from the consolidation must comply with applicable employment law, collective bargaining agreements, and Meridian HR Policy v4. The Joint Commission survey preparation team must be briefed on the organizational changes at least 60 days prior to any scheduled accreditation review.
