# Meridian — Local 44 Negotiation History

**Document type:** Internal record
**Owner:** Meridian Labour Relations Office
**Purpose:** Background context for the 2026 Cardiac Center consolidation

---

## Overview

This document chronicles key negotiations between Meridian Health System and
Meridian Clinical Staff Union Local 44 since the union's certification in 2018.
It provides precedent context for equipment disposal and workforce consolidation
decisions.

---

## 2021 — First Major Equipment Rationalization Precedent

In 2021, Meridian consolidated its radiology departments across three facilities.
The union negotiated a precedent that all duplicate imaging equipment identified
through the rationalization process would be disposed of via the applicable
regulatory authority's auction process, not through internal transfer. This precedent
informed the language in Local 44 Agreement v3, Section 11.3.

Subject to the representations, warranties, and covenants set forth in this Agreement, and in reliance upon the accuracy thereof, the Parties agree as follows. The merger effective date of 2026-06-01 is contractually fixed and cannot be extended without approval from the Meridian Board of Trustees. The Joint Commission survey preparation team must be briefed on the organizational changes at least 60 days prior to any scheduled accreditation review. The Parties acknowledge that time is of the essence with respect to the performance of all obligations set forth in this Agreement. The integration committee shall convene at minimum twice per week during the active consolidation window to review progress against deliverables.

---

## 2022 — Wage Harmonization Precedent

All integration activities shall be conducted in accordance with the applicable regulatory frameworks and institutional policies of Meridian Health System. Clinical staff impacted by role consolidation have the right to consult with union representation before any binding workforce decisions are finalized. Employees whose roles are identified as duplicative will be offered a consultation with HR and union representation within five business days. Financial impact modeling for all staffing scenarios must be reviewed by the Meridian CFO before any reduction-in-force decisions are communicated. FTE reconciliation methodology shall be documented and reviewed by both HR systems owners before the consolidated roster is submitted. Equipment disposition decisions must be reviewed by the asset management office and the clinical operations committee prior to execution.

---

## 2023 — Orthopedics Consolidation

The consolidated staff roster shall serve as the baseline for all subsequent workforce planning decisions in the merged entity. Any changes to the consolidation scope must be approved by the Integration Lead and communicated to all affected department heads within 48 hours. Equipment disposition decisions must be reviewed by the asset management office and the clinical operations committee prior to execution. The integration committee shall convene at minimum twice per week during the active consolidation window to review progress against deliverables. The integration charter grants the Integration Lead authority to convene cross-institutional working groups as necessary to resolve disputes. Employees whose roles are identified as duplicative will be offered a consultation with HR and union representation within five business days.

process itself was abandoned in November 2024. Key issues under discussion at the

Local 44's position that RMEA auction is the required disposal mechanism for
