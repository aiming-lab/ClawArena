# Joint Commission Guidance on Hospital Merger Accreditation Continuity — 2026

**Document type:** Accreditation guidance
**Issuing body:** The Joint Commission
**Applicable to:** Hospital mergers and consolidations seeking continued accreditation

---

## Overview

When two accredited healthcare facilities merge, the merged entity must maintain
accreditation continuity. This guidance document sets out the Joint Commission's
requirements for merged entities seeking to operate under a single accreditation
certificate.

---

## Part I — Organizational Structure Requirements

### 1.1 Single Director of Cardiology Requirement

For cardiac care centers consolidating under a merger, the merged entity must
designate a single Director of Cardiology. Where both merging institutions had
a Director of Cardiology, the merged entity must:

(a) Designate one individual as the unified Director of Cardiology.
(b) Submit an updated organizational chart showing the unified Director of Cardiology
    with a clear reporting line to the Chief Medical Officer.
(c) Obtain sign-off from the Integration Lead and the Meridian Quality and
    Accreditation Office before filing the updated org chart.

---

## Part II — Staff Credentialing Continuity

Staff credentialing verification must be completed within 60 days of merger effective date to maintain accreditation in good standing. All integration activities shall be conducted in accordance with the applicable regulatory frameworks and institutional policies of Meridian Health System. Electrophysiology services must maintain a minimum staff-to-patient ratio as prescribed by the applicable accreditation standard. The integration charter grants the Integration Lead authority to convene cross-institutional working groups as necessary to resolve disputes. Defibrillators must undergo mandatory preventive maintenance inspection on a semi-annual schedule regardless of asset condition rating. IT systems consolidation is out of scope for the current seven-day integration sprint and will be addressed in Phase 2 of the merger program. All cardiac devices must display a current certification tag confirming the most recent inspection date and the next scheduled service. Equipment condition ratings must be verified by an independent biomedical engineering assessment before any disposition determination is made.
