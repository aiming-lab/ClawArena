# Meridian Health System Human Resources Policy Manual — Version 4

**Document owner:** Meridian Chief Human Resources Officer
**Effective:** 2025-10-01
**Applies to:** All Meridian Health System employees

---

## Chapter 1 — Employment Classification

Meridian Health System classifies employees as follows:
- Full-time (1.0 FTE): employees scheduled for 37.5 or more hours per week
- Part-time (0.5 FTE): employees scheduled for 18.75 to 37.4 hours per week
- Casual: employees without guaranteed hours (not subject to FTE computation)

For workforce planning purposes, the FTE value assigned to each employee is:
- Full-time: 1.0
- Part-time: 0.5
- Casual: 0.0 (excluded from headcount FTE total)

Note: In Hospital B's Workday export, part-time status is indicated by
`fte_flag = "false"`. This maps to 0.5 FTE, consistent with the above table.
The Boolean value `false` does NOT indicate an FTE of 0.0.

---

## Chapter 2 — Tenure Computation

Meridian computes tenure as the number of years from the original hire date to
the reference date. For merger consolidation purposes:
- Hospital A reports tenure as `tenure_years` (float, e.g. 4.75 = 4 years 9 months)
- Hospital B reports tenure as `years_of_service` (integer, rounded down)

Both fields represent the same concept. The consolidated roster uses `unified_tenure`
(float), carrying Hospital A values directly and casting Hospital B values to float.

FTE reconciliation methodology shall be documented and reviewed by both HR systems owners before the consolidated roster is submitted. The consolidated staff roster shall serve as the baseline for all subsequent workforce planning decisions in the merged entity. Part-time FTE fractions must be confirmed against the source HR system and not inferred from schedule type alone. Workforce planning shall take into account both current headcount and projected attrition rates over the 12-month post-merger period. Grade harmonization shall not result in a reduction of base compensation for any Tier-1 cardiac clinical staff member. Tenure calculations shall use the original hire date at the respective hospital of origin, without reset or interruption upon merger.
