**Document type:** Technical standards reference

## Chapter 1 — Class-II Cardiac Device Categories

The following device categories are classified as Class-II cardiac devices for
the purposes of RMEA auction requirements and lifecycle management:

### 1.1 Echocardiographs
Ultrasound-based cardiac imaging systems used for real-time visualization of
cardiac structure and function. Require semi-annual calibration and annual
transducer inspection.

### 1.2 Defibrillators
Devices delivering controlled electrical shocks for arrhythmia treatment.
Require mandatory preventive maintenance on a semi-annual schedule.

### 1.3 Cardiac Monitors
Continuous vital sign monitoring systems for cardiac ICU and ward use.
Require quarterly battery checks and annual firmware updates.

### 1.4 EP Recording Systems
Electrophysiology laboratory recording and mapping systems. Require annual
calibration and software version management.
