## IMPORTANT: Equipment Disposal Guidance Supersession

The memo `memo_2026_equipment_disposal.md` (dated 2026-05-20, signed by Ms. Fatima Nkosi)
supersedes the interim equipment disposal guidance in
`merger_governance/meridian_merger_policy_v2.md` Section 7.4. Any equipment
disposition decisions must now be based on the explicit requirements set out in
`memo_2026_equipment_disposal.md`, not on the general clause in Section 11.3 of
the Local 44 agreement alone.

Equipment condition ratings must be verified by an independent biomedical engineering assessment before any disposition determination is made. Stakeholder communication shall follow the escalation matrix established in Appendix C of the Meridian Integration Governance Framework.
