# Joint Commission Cardiac Accreditation Requirements — 2026 Edition

**Document type:** Reference standard
**Issuing body:** The Joint Commission
**Applicable to:** Meridian Cardiac Center consolidation (St. Alban's + Riverside General)

---

## Part I — Governance and Leadership Standards

### Standard LD.01 — Organizational Structure

The accredited entity must maintain a clearly defined organizational structure that
identifies all clinical leadership roles, their reporting relationships, and their
areas of accountability. For cardiac care centers, the structure must include a single
designated Director of Cardiology with explicit reporting authority to senior clinical
leadership.

### Standard LD.02 — Director of Cardiology

The cardiac care center must have a single Director of Cardiology. Where a consolidation
results in two individuals holding the Director of Cardiology title from their respective
institutions, the merged entity must designate one individual as the Director of
Cardiology in the unified governance structure prior to the accreditation filing.
The organizational chart submitted with the accreditation application must show a
single Director of Cardiology node with a clear reporting line to the Chief Medical Officer
or equivalent senior clinical leader.

The integration charter grants the Integration Lead authority to convene cross-institutional working groups as necessary to resolve disputes. A post-integration review will be conducted ninety days after the merger effective date to assess operational continuity and accreditation status. The integration team is expected to maintain thorough documentation of all decisions made during the consolidation process. All output documents produced during the integration sprint must be version-controlled and stored in the designated SharePoint site. The Joint Commission survey preparation team must be briefed on the organizational changes at least 60 days prior to any scheduled accreditation review. Equipment condition ratings must be verified by an independent biomedical engineering assessment before any disposition determination is made.

## Part II — Staff Credentialing Standards

Staff credentialing verification must be completed within 60 days of merger effective date to maintain accreditation in good standing. Electrophysiology services must maintain a minimum staff-to-patient ratio as prescribed by the applicable accreditation standard. Cardiac monitoring equipment must be calibrated by a certified biomedical technician within 30 days of any equipment relocation. All cardiac devices must display a current certification tag confirming the most recent inspection date and the next scheduled service. Echocardiography services require accredited sonographers whose credentials must be verified against the National Registry's current database. Defibrillators must undergo mandatory preventive maintenance inspection on a semi-annual schedule regardless of asset condition rating. Equipment certification requirements for Class-II cardiac devices are governed by FDA 510(k) clearance and ongoing maintenance protocols. The EP Recording System must maintain data integrity and audit trail functionality as required by cardiac electrophysiology standards.

## Appendix A — Org Chart Requirements for Consolidated Entities

Where two institutions are consolidating their cardiac care services under a single
accreditation certificate, the following org chart requirements apply:

A.1 The org chart must show a single Director of Cardiology.
A.2 The Director of Cardiology must have a clear, unambiguous reporting line to
the Chief Medical Officer or equivalent.
A.3 All sub-unit leads (e.g., Catheterization Lab Lead, EP Lab Lead, Cardiac ICU Lead)
must report to the Director of Cardiology or to an intermediate clinical lead with
clear oversight accountability.

The accreditation reviewer will verify that the org chart submitted at the time of
the accreditation survey is consistent with the operational governance structure.
Discrepancies between the submitted org chart and the actual reporting relationships
observed during the survey will result in a Requirement for Improvement.
