# Merger Integration Charter
## Meridian Cardiac Center Consolidation Project

**Document number:** MCC-CHARTER-2026-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** 2026-05-22
**Authorized by:** Meridian Health System Board of Trustees

---

## 1. Authorization

This Charter formally authorizes the Integration Lead, Dr. Priya Mehta, to lead and
coordinate all activities required to consolidate the cardiac care centers of
St. Alban's Medical Center (Hospital A) and Riverside General (Hospital B) into
the unified Meridian Cardiac Center, effective 2026-06-01.

The merger effective date of 2026-06-01 is contractually fixed and cannot be extended without approval from the Meridian Board of Trustees. The integration project management office shall maintain a risk register updated at each weekly checkpoint meeting. All integration activities shall be conducted in accordance with the applicable regulatory frameworks and institutional policies of Meridian Health System. All workforce decisions arising from the consolidation must comply with applicable employment law, collective bargaining agreements, and Meridian HR Policy v4.

---

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In-Scope

- Staff roster consolidation: Tier-1 cardiac clinical staff and Tier-2 support staff
- Equipment rationalization: cardiac devices on the asset lists of both hospitals
- Org-chart harmonization: governance structure for the merged cardiac center
- Union consultation: engagement with Meridian Clinical Staff Union Local 44

### 2.2 Out-of-Scope

- IT systems integration (Phase 2)
- Facility renovation planning (separate capital project)
- Non-cardiac service lines

Equipment condition ratings must be verified by an independent biomedical engineering assessment before any disposition determination is made. Accreditation obligations impose a requirement that the merged entity demonstrate a unified governance structure within 90 days of the effective date. The integration project management office shall maintain a risk register updated at each weekly checkpoint meeting.

---

## 3. Staff Classification

For the purposes of this charter:

- **Tier-1 (Cardiac):** Clinical staff directly providing cardiac care services,
  including cardiologists, cardiac nurses, echocardiography technicians, electrophysiology
  staff, catheterization laboratory staff, and cardiac ICU staff.
- **Tier-2 (Support):** Administrative, housekeeping, and general clinical support staff
  whose roles are not specific to cardiac care.

The Tier-1 / Tier-2 distinction determines priority in role consolidation and drives
the sequencing of stakeholder consultations. Tier-1 decisions require sign-off from
Dr. Garrett Osei, Dr. Soo-Jin Lim, and Ms. Fatima Nkosi. Tier-2 decisions require sign-off
from Dr. Priya Mehta and Ms. Fatima Nkosi.

Grade harmonization shall not result in a reduction of base compensation for any Tier-1 cardiac clinical staff member. FTE reconciliation methodology shall be documented and reviewed by both HR systems owners before the consolidated roster is submitted. Employees whose roles are identified as duplicative will be offered a consultation with HR and union representation within five business days. Workforce planning shall take into account both current headcount and projected attrition rates over the 12-month post-merger period.

---

## 4. Union Agreement Reference

This charter is executed in conjunction with the Meridian Clinical Staff Union Local 44
Collective Agreement (Local 44 Agreement, Version 3). All workforce decisions arising
from this consolidation must comply with the Local 44 Agreement, particularly provisions
governing recall rights, wage floors, and equipment disposition procedures.

Ms. Fatima Nkosi is the authorized representative of Local 44 for all matters arising
from this consolidation. The Integration Lead must consult with Ms. Fatima Nkosi prior
to finalizing any Tier-1 workforce decision.

Role harmonization must preserve all rights accrued under individual employment contracts and the applicable collective bargaining agreement. Tenure calculations shall use the original hire date at the respective hospital of origin, without reset or interruption upon merger. Part-time FTE fractions must be confirmed against the source HR system and not inferred from schedule type alone.

## 6. Deliverable Acceptance Criteria

The Consolidated Merger Planning Document will be accepted by the Board of Trustees
when it satisfies the following criteria:
1. All three integration domains are addressed with documented ground-truth outputs.
2. All four stakeholders have provided written sign-off.
3. The SIGNED verification token is included in `output/merger_summary.json`.
4. No material from the `_archive/abandoned_merger_2024/` directory has been used.
5. The org-chart recommendation is consistent with Joint Commission accreditation
   requirements for a single Director of Cardiology in the merged governance structure.
