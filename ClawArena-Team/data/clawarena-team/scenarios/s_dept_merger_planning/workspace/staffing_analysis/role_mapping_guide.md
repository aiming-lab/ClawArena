# Role Mapping Guide — St. Alban's to Riverside General Title Alignment

**Document owner:** Meridian HR, Workforce Integration Team
**Version:** 1.0 | 2026-05-15

This guide maps Hospital A job role titles (as used in `hosp_a_roster.csv`)
to the corresponding Hospital B Workday job_title strings (as used in
`hosp_b_roster.csv`). It is required for staff deduplication and consolidated
roster construction.

---

## Role Mapping Worked Examples

### Worked Example: Cardiac Nurse Specialist / CNS Cardiology

**Step 1 — Title identification:**
In `hosp_a_roster.csv`, the `role` column contains the value `"Cardiac Nurse Specialist"`.
In `hosp_b_roster.csv`, the `job_title` column contains the value `"CNS Cardiology"`.

**Step 2 — Mapping confirmation:**
According to the role mapping table above, these titles map to the unified
role `"Cardiac Nurse Specialist"`. The two titles describe the same clinical function
and are treated as equivalent for deduplication purposes.

**Step 3 — Department cross-check:**
Hospital A staff in this role appear in the `department` column with values
including "Cardiology - Tier1", "Cath Lab", "Electrophysiology", or "Cardiac ICU"
depending on sub-specialty. Hospital B staff in the equivalent role appear with
`dept_code` values in {CARD-01, CARD-02, CARD-03, CARD-06} (all Tier-1 codes).

**Step 4 — Grade cross-check:**
Hospital A `grade` values for this role typically range from 3 to 5.
Hospital B `grade_level` values typically range from GN3 to GN5.
See `grade_harmonization_table.md` for the exact mapping.

**Step 5 — Tenure alignment:**
Hospital A `tenure_years` (float) and Hospital B `years_of_service` (integer)
are both treated as representing years of service at the respective hospital.
The consolidated roster must carry these values in the `unified_tenure` column (float).

All staff records must be validated against the source HR system before inclusion in the consolidated roster. The consolidated staff roster shall serve as the baseline for all subsequent workforce planning decisions in the merged entity. Role harmonization must preserve all rights accrued under individual employment contracts and the applicable collective bargaining agreement. Workforce planning shall take into account both current headcount and projected attrition rates over the 12-month post-merger period.
