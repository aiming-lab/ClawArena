# Grade Harmonization Table — Hospital A to MHC Unified Grade Scale

**Document owner:** Meridian HR, Workforce Integration Team
**Version:** 1.0 | 2026-05-15

This table maps Hospital A grade values (integer 1–5) and Hospital B grade_level
strings (GN1–GN5) to the unified MHC Grade scale used in the consolidated roster.

---

## Grade Mapping Table

| Hospital A `grade` | Hospital B `grade_level` | MHC Unified Grade | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GN1 | MHC-1 | Entry-level clinical support staff |
| 2 | GN2 | MHC-2 | Intermediate clinical support staff |
| 3 | GN3 | MHC-3 | Senior clinical support / junior specialist |
| 4 | GN4 | MHC-4 | Senior specialist / team lead |
| 5 | GN5 | MHC-5 | Advanced specialist / department lead |

---

## Grade Distribution in Current Rosters (Tier-1 Cardiac)

| MHC Grade | Hospital A count | Hospital B count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| MHC-1 | 3 | 2 | Entry-level cardiac support |
| MHC-2 | 8 | 7 | Intermediate cardiac nursing and technician |
| MHC-3 | 14 | 13 | Senior cardiac nursing and specialist roles |
| MHC-4 | 15 | 14 | Senior specialist and team lead roles |
| MHC-5 | 7 | 8 | Advanced specialist and unit lead roles |
