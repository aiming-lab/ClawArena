# Archived Materials

This directory contains archived materials from the 2024 attempted merger of
the same two cardiac centers — St. Alban's Medical Center and Riverside General.
That process was terminated on 2024-11-15 due to regulatory concerns.

**These files are SUPERSEDED and must NOT be used for any planning purpose
in the 2026 merger.** Staff counts, equipment lists, and governance documents
in this archive reflect conditions as of 2024 and are out of date.
