# Merger Agreement — St. Alban's Medical Center and Riverside General
## Draft Version 4 | Prepared by: Meridian Office of General Counsel

**Effective date:** 2026-06-01
**Parties:**
- St. Alban's Medical Center, a member institution of Meridian Health System
- Riverside General, a member institution of Meridian Health System
- Meridian Health System (as guarantor and integration coordinator)

**Integration Lead:** Dr. Priya Mehta

---

## Recitals

WHEREAS, Meridian Health System has determined that consolidation of the cardiac care
centers of St. Alban's Medical Center and Riverside General will advance the
System's strategic objectives of accreditation compliance, capital efficiency, and
clinical excellence;

WHEREAS, both institutions have consented to the consolidation under the terms of
this Agreement;

WHEREAS, the Meridian Clinical Staff Union, Local 44, represented by Ms. Fatima Nkosi,
has been consulted and has acknowledged the integration process;

NOW THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by the waiving Party. The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of any provision. Each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with the negotiation, preparation, and execution of this Agreement, unless otherwise specified.

---

## Article 1 — Effective Date and Transition

1.1 This Agreement becomes effective on 2026-06-01 ("Effective Date").

1.2 The integration team's planning sprint runs from 2026-05-22 to 2026-05-29.

1.3 All assets, staff records, and governance instruments transfer to the merged
entity on the Effective Date unless otherwise specified.

Subject to the representations, warranties, and covenants set forth in this Agreement, and in reliance upon the accuracy thereof, the Parties agree as follows. The Parties represent and warrant that they have full corporate authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform their obligations hereunder. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements. The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of any provision. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

---

## Article 2 — Asset Transfer

2.1 All cardiac equipment currently registered to St. Alban's Medical Center
and Riverside General shall transfer to the merged entity on the Effective Date.

2.2 Duplicate equipment identified through the serial-number matching process shall
be subject to disposition in accordance with the applicable union agreement and
regulatory requirements.

2.3 The equipment valuation report prepared by the independent third-party valuer
serves as background reference for disposition planning but does not constitute
a binding valuation for accounting purposes.

The Parties represent and warrant that they have full corporate authority to enter into this Agreement and to perform their obligations hereunder. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by the waiving Party. Subject to the representations, warranties, and covenants set forth in this Agreement, and in reliance upon the accuracy thereof, the Parties agree as follows.

---

## Article 3 — Staff Continuity

3.1 All employees of both institutions are offered continuity of employment in
the merged entity subject to role rationalization as described in the Merger
Integration Brief.

3.2 No Tier-1 cardiac staff member shall be laid off as a direct result of this
consolidation.

3.3 All employment terms are subject to the Local 44 Collective Agreement.

Employees whose roles are identified as duplicative will be offered a consultation with HR and union representation within five business days. Workforce planning shall take into account both current headcount and projected attrition rates over the 12-month post-merger period. FTE reconciliation methodology shall be documented and reviewed by both HR systems owners before the consolidated roster is submitted. The Parties acknowledge that time is of the essence with respect to the performance of all obligations set forth in this Agreement. All staff records must be validated against the source HR system before inclusion in the consolidated roster. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the applicable jurisdiction, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

---

## Article 4 — Governance

4.1 The merged entity shall operate under a unified governance structure with a
single Director of Cardiology as required by Joint Commission accreditation standards.

4.2 Dr. Priya Mehta is authorized to make the initial governance recommendation,
subject to sign-off from Dr. Garrett Osei and Dr. Soo-Jin Lim.

All legal instruments arising from the merger shall be reviewed by Meridian's Office of General Counsel prior to execution. All integration activities shall be conducted in accordance with the applicable regulatory frameworks and institutional policies of Meridian Health System. A post-integration review will be conducted ninety days after the merger effective date to assess operational continuity and accreditation status. Financial impact modeling for all staffing scenarios must be reviewed by the Meridian CFO before any reduction-in-force decisions are communicated. Stakeholder communication shall follow the escalation matrix established in Appendix C of the Meridian Integration Governance Framework.
