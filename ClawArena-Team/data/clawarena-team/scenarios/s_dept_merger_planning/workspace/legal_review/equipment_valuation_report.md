# Equipment Valuation Report — Cardiac Center Consolidation
## Prepared by: MedAsset Valuations Inc. (Independent Third Party)

**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Client:** Meridian Health System
**Scope:** Cardiac devices at St. Alban's Medical Center and Riverside General

**Note:** This report provides reference valuations for planning purposes only.
Final disposition decisions are governed by applicable regulatory authority
guidelines and the union collective agreement.

---

## Executive Summary

MedAsset Valuations Inc. conducted a desktop valuation of cardiac devices listed
in the asset registers of both hospitals. The total estimated fair market value
of all cardiac devices across both institutions is approximately $4.2 million.
Duplicate devices (those with matching serial numbers) have an estimated aggregate
fair market value of approximately $1.1 million.

---

## Valuation Methodology

Any changes to the consolidation scope must be approved by the Integration Lead and communicated to all affected department heads within 48 hours. Staff credentialing verification must be completed within 60 days of merger effective date to maintain accreditation in good standing. Defibrillators must undergo mandatory preventive maintenance inspection on a semi-annual schedule regardless of asset condition rating. Accreditation obligations impose a requirement that the merged entity demonstrate a unified governance structure within 90 days of the effective date. Echocardiography services require accredited sonographers whose credentials must be verified against the National Registry's current database.
