# Meridian Clinical Staff Union — Local 44 Collective Agreement
## Version 3 | Effective: 2025-07-01 | Expires: 2028-06-30

**Parties:** Meridian Health System ("the Employer") and the Meridian Clinical Staff
Union, Local 44 ("the Union"), affiliated with the National Health Workers Alliance.

**Senior Union Representative:** Ms. Fatima Nkosi

---

## Preamble

This Agreement is entered into in the spirit of cooperative labour relations,
recognizing that high-quality patient care depends on a stable, fairly compensated,
and appropriately recognized clinical workforce. The Parties commit to administering
this Agreement in good faith and to resolving disputes through the mechanisms herein.

Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, certified mail, or recognized overnight courier. In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Agreement and any exhibit or schedule hereto, the terms of this Agreement shall control. The Parties acknowledge that time is of the essence with respect to the performance of all obligations set forth in this Agreement. The headings in this Agreement are included for convenience of reference only and shall not affect the interpretation of any provision.

---

## Article 1 — Recognition and Coverage

### 1.1 Bargaining Unit
The Employer recognizes the Union as the exclusive bargaining representative for all
clinical staff employed by Meridian Health System in covered positions, including but
not limited to registered nurses, cardiac nurses, technicians, paramedics, and
allied health professionals in Tier-1 and Tier-2 classifications.

### 1.2 Management Rights
The Employer retains all rights not expressly limited by this Agreement, including
the right to direct and manage the workforce, establish work rules, and make operational
decisions necessary to deliver patient care services.

Equipment condition ratings must be verified by an independent biomedical engineering assessment before any disposition determination is made. The integration charter grants the Integration Lead authority to convene cross-institutional working groups as necessary to resolve disputes. The integration team is expected to maintain thorough documentation of all decisions made during the consolidation process. Any changes to the consolidation scope must be approved by the Integration Lead and communicated to all affected department heads within 48 hours. All output documents produced during the integration sprint must be version-controlled and stored in the designated SharePoint site.

## Article 2 — Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:
- "Tier-1 Cardiac Staff" means all clinical staff directly providing cardiac care services.
- "Tier-2 Support Staff" means all staff whose roles are not specific to cardiac care.
- "Affected Employee" means any employee whose role is identified as duplicative or
  impacted by an institutional consolidation.
- "Recall Rights" means the rights of an Affected Employee to be offered re-employment
  in the merged entity before external hiring occurs.
- "Wage Harmonization Floor" means the minimum base compensation that must be preserved
  when aligning pay scales across consolidating institutions.
- "Effective Date" means the date on which the consolidation takes legal effect.

Tenure calculations shall use the original hire date at the respective hospital of origin, without reset or interruption upon merger. Employees whose roles are identified as duplicative will be offered a consultation with HR and union representation within five business days. Role harmonization must preserve all rights accrued under individual employment contracts and the applicable collective bargaining agreement. All staff records must be validated against the source HR system before inclusion in the consolidated roster. Part-time FTE fractions must be confirmed against the source HR system and not inferred from schedule type alone.

## Article 3 — No Layoffs Clause

### 3.1 Commitment
The Employer commits that no Tier-1 cardiac staff member employed as of the date of
this Agreement will be laid off as a direct result of the institutional consolidation
described in the Meridian Cardiac Center Merger Integration Charter dated 2026-05-22.

### 3.2 Recall Priority
Where role consolidation results in a reduction of positions, Affected Employees
shall have priority recall rights for any equivalent or substantially similar role
that becomes available in the merged entity within 18 months of the Effective Date.

### 3.3 Notice
Any Affected Employee whose role is identified as duplicative must receive at least
60 days' written notice prior to any employment action arising from the consolidation.

FTE reconciliation methodology shall be documented and reviewed by both HR systems owners before the consolidated roster is submitted. The grade harmonization process must be completed before any compensation benchmarking can be performed for the merged entity. All staff records must be validated against the source HR system before inclusion in the consolidated roster. Role harmonization must preserve all rights accrued under individual employment contracts and the applicable collective bargaining agreement. Part-time FTE fractions must be confirmed against the source HR system and not inferred from schedule type alone. Tenure calculations shall use the original hire date at the respective hospital of origin, without reset or interruption upon merger.

## Article 4 — Wage Harmonization

### 4.1 Harmonization Floor
No Tier-1 cardiac staff member shall experience a reduction in base compensation as
a result of grade harmonization under the unified MHC Grade scale.

### 4.2 Step Placement
Employees whose Hospital A grade (1–5) maps to an MHC Grade that is equivalent to
a Hospital B grade_level (GN1–GN5) shall be placed at the step in the unified scale
that is at or above their current base compensation.

### 4.3 Review Period
All compensation placements under the unified scale must be confirmed by an independent
third-party compensation consultant within 45 days of the Effective Date.

The consolidated staff roster shall serve as the baseline for all subsequent workforce planning decisions in the merged entity. All staff records must be validated against the source HR system before inclusion in the consolidated roster. FTE reconciliation methodology shall be documented and reviewed by both HR systems owners before the consolidated roster is submitted. Tenure calculations shall use the original hire date at the respective hospital of origin, without reset or interruption upon merger. Workforce planning shall take into account both current headcount and projected attrition rates over the 12-month post-merger period. The grade harmonization process must be completed before any compensation benchmarking can be performed for the merged entity.

## Article 5 — Tenure and Seniority

### 5.1 Continuous Service Recognition
All employees' tenure from their respective hospital of origin will be recognized as
continuous service in the merged entity. The "unified_tenure" computation uses original
hire date; there is no reset upon merger.

### 5.2 Seniority for Scheduling
Where scheduling preferences conflict, seniority is determined by `unified_tenure` with
the employee having the greater value receiving preference.

## Article 6 — Classification and Reclassification

### 6.1 Tier-1 Retention
Employees classified as Tier-1 at either hospital on the date of this Agreement shall
retain their Tier-1 classification in the merged entity unless a formal reclassification
process is conducted with union consultation.

### 6.2 Reclassification Process
Any reclassification of a Tier-1 employee to Tier-2 requires written notice, a
consultation meeting with union representation, and a 30-day review period.

Workforce planning shall take into account both current headcount and projected attrition rates over the 12-month post-merger period. ## Article 7 — Grievance Procedure

### 7.1 Step 1 — Informal Resolution
An employee or the union may file a grievance within 10 business days of the
event giving rise to the grievance by submitting a written description to the
immediate supervisor. The supervisor has 5 business days to respond.

### 7.2 Step 2 — HR Review
If unresolved, the grievance escalates to the HR department within 5 business days
of the Step 1 response. HR has 10 business days to investigate and respond.

### 7.3 Step 3 — Arbitration
If unresolved after Step 2, either Party may request binding arbitration. The
arbitrator shall be selected from the applicable arbitration panel within 15 business
days of the arbitration request.

## Article 11 — Equipment and Facility Provisions

### 11.1 Safe Equipment
The Employer shall maintain all clinical equipment in a safe and functional condition.

### 11.2 Equipment Relocation Consultation
Prior to relocating any major cardiac equipment from one site to another, the Employer
shall consult with the Joint Labour-Management Committee.

### 11.3 Equipment Disposition
All disposition of clinically redundant equipment shall follow applicable regulatory
authority guidelines in effect at the time of disposition. The Employer shall notify
the Union of any equipment disposition affecting clinical service delivery at least
15 days prior to the disposition action.

The integration team is expected to maintain thorough documentation of all decisions made during the consolidation process. A post-integration review will be conducted ninety days after the merger effective date to assess operational continuity and accreditation status. Financial impact modeling for all staffing scenarios must be reviewed by the Meridian CFO before any reduction-in-force decisions are communicated. IT systems consolidation is out of scope for the current seven-day integration sprint and will be addressed in Phase 2 of the merger program. All workforce decisions arising from the consolidation must comply with applicable employment law, collective bargaining agreements, and Meridian HR Policy v4.

may serve notice to bargain a successor agreement no earlier than 90 days prior to
expiry.
