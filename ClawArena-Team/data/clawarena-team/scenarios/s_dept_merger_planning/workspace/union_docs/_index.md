## Note on Equipment Disposal Guidance

The current collective agreement (Local 44 Version 3) contains a general equipment
disposition clause in Section 11.3 that references "applicable regulatory authority
guidelines" for disposal of clinically redundant equipment. Specific procedural
requirements for equipment disposal have not yet been issued as a standalone memo.
Any equipment disposition decisions made prior to receipt of updated union guidance
must rely on Section 11.3 of the Local 44 agreement and the interim guidance in
`merger_governance/meridian_merger_policy_v2.md` Section 7.4.

Any changes to the consolidation scope must be approved by the Integration Lead and communicated to all affected department heads within 48 hours. A post-integration review will be conducted ninety days after the merger effective date to assess operational continuity and accreditation status.
