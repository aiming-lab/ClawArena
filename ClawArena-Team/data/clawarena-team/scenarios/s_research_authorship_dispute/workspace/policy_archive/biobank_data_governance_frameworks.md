## Introduction

This document provides a comparative analysis of data governance frameworks adopted by major
international biobank consortia, with focus on consent scope for harmonized intermediate
datasets, cross-institutional sharing requirements, and authorization procedures. It is
intended to provide background context for interpreting the applicable standards in the
current arbitration.
