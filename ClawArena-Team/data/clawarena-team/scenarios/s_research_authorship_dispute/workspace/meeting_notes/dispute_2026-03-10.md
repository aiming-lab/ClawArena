## Background

This meeting was called following Aoife Ní Mhurchadha's formal written objection to the submission of
manuscript v3 on the grounds of unauthorized dataset use (harmonized_gwas_v2/) and disputed
author ordering.

## Item 1 — Dataset Authorization Dispute

Aoife Ní Mhurchadha formally objected to the integration of `harmonized_gwas_v2/` into the main
pipeline, stating that this was done without her written consent in violation of what she
understands to be the consent scope in the signed IRB consent form. She stated: "I raised this
in writing in November 2024 and received no response. The integration went ahead without my
approval, and that is a violation of our agreement."

Liang Jiewen and Tariq Saleem defended the integration as falling within the scope of the
approved protocol and as having been approved by Tariq Saleem via email. Liang Jiewen stated:
"This is a technical necessity, not a breach of consent."

Tariq Saleem stated: "I gave Mina explicit go-ahead for the integration. The approval process
has been satisfied."

Aoife Ní Mhurchadha disputed this: "Your go-ahead to Mina does not constitute the written approval of
all three PIs required by the consent form."

No resolution was reached. The parties agreed to refer the matter to Dr. Chisom Ezenwachi for binding
arbitration.
