## 1. Project Overview

Liang Jiewen opened the meeting by presenting the project overview and the proposed 18-month
timeline for completing the cross-ancestry GWAS collaboration. The goal is to produce a landmark
multi-cohort validation paper by early 2026. The three cohorts to be used are UKBB (European lead:
Aoife Ní Mhurchadha), BBJ + additional East Asian cohorts (computational lead: Liang Jiewen), and the
Toronto Biobank (clinical lead: Tariq Saleem).

## 4. Authorship Principles

Liang Jiewen proposed the following authorship model: first authorship should be assigned to the
PI making the lead intellectual contribution to the computational and analytical core. He indicated
that he expected to be the natural candidate based on his expertise. Aoife Ní Mhurchadha did not comment
on the authorship proposal. Tariq Saleem made a verbal remark to the effect of "we'll sort this
out at submission time," which was interpreted by some attendees as implicit acceptance and by
others as deferral.
