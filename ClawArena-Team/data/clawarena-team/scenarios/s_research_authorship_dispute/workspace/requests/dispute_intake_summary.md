# Dispute Intake Summary — Per-PI Position Statements

**Reference:** OUO-2026-ARB-0047
**Prepared by:** Office of the Inter-University Ombudsperson
**Date:** 2026-05-15

---

## Position of Prof. Liang Jiewen (Tsinghua University)

Liang Jiewen maintains that he is the rightful first author of the Study on the grounds that he
made the largest intellectual contribution to the computational genomics methodology, designed the
core polygenic risk score pipeline, and contributed the highest number of commits to the shared
codebase. He cites a commit count of 89 from the project repository's contribution summary as
primary evidence. He does not formally contest Tariq Saleem's claim to corresponding-author
status, but reserves the right to revisit this if the arbitration finds the git record has been
materially altered. Liang Jiewen denies any wrongdoing in connection with the amended commit.

## Position of Prof. Aoife Ní Mhurchadha (University College Dublin)

Aoife Ní Mhurchadha does not assert a claim to first authorship. She objects to the current v3 manuscript
author ordering on the grounds that the authorship determination process did not follow ICMJE
criteria and that her contributions to the biostatistical design are under-recognised. More
critically, she formally alleges that the harmonized intermediate dataset `harmonized_gwas_v2/`
was incorporated into the analysis pipeline without her explicit written consent, in violation of
the data-sharing consent scope that she understands to be stated in the signed IRB consent form.
She states that she raised this concern as early as November 2024 and received no satisfactory
response. She requests a full review of the consent form and a determination on dataset
authorization.

## Position of Prof. Tariq Saleem (University of Toronto)

Tariq Saleem claims corresponding-author status on the basis of his role as IRB sponsor for the
Toronto-based clinical cohort, his institutional accountability for the clinical data, and an
alleged verbal agreement reached at the 2024 project kickoff meeting. He defends the integration
of `harmonized_gwas_v2/` as authorised through implied consent and a subsequent written exchange
with graduate student Mina Takahashi in March 2025, in which he gave explicit go-ahead for the
integration. He does not contest Liang Jiewen's claim to first authorship. He disputes that any
written consent requirement applied to the intermediate dataset in question.
