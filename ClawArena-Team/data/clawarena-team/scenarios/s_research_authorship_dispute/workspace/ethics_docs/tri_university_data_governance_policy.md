## Section 7 — Definition of Written Consent

For the purposes of this Policy and the Joint Research Agreement, "written consent" means either:
(a) a signed document in which the consenting PI explicitly authorizes the relevant action; or
(b) a documented email exchange in which the consenting PI explicitly and unambiguously approves
the relevant action by name, identifying the specific dataset or action being approved. A reply
to a third-party email by a single PI does not constitute written consent of all Principal
Investigators unless all three PIs are party to the exchange and each PI has explicitly approved
the relevant action.
