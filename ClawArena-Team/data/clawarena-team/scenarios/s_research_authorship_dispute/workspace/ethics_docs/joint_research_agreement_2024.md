## Section 3 — Authorship

### Section 3.1 — Authorship Criteria

Authorship of all publications arising from the Study shall be determined in accordance with
the ICMJE (International Committee of Medical Journal Editors) 2023 authorship criteria. The
ICMJE criteria require that each author: (a) makes a substantial contribution to the conception
or design of the work, or to the acquisition, analysis, or interpretation of data; (b) drafts
or critically revises the work for important intellectual content; (c) approves the final version
for publication; and (d) is accountable for all aspects of the work. First-author and
corresponding-author designation shall be determined by the three PIs by consensus, with
reference to the ICMJE criteria, no later than six weeks before the submission deadline. In the
absence of consensus, the matter shall be referred to the inter-university ombudsperson for
binding arbitration.

### Section 3.2 — Verbal Agreements

Verbal agreements or representations made at meetings, including the project kickoff meeting,
do not constitute binding commitments for the purposes of this Agreement. Any agreement on
authorship, data use, or other material matters must be documented in writing (email, letter,
or signed amendment to this Agreement) to be binding.

## Section 5 — Dataset Sharing

### Section 5.1 — General Principles

All dataset sharing between the three institutions shall be conducted in accordance with the
applicable IRB consent requirements, the institutional data governance policies of each party,
and the terms of this Agreement.

### Section 5.2 — Written Consent Requirement

All dataset sharing between the institutions in connection with the Study — including the
transfer, copying, incorporation, or use of datasets or derivative files generated by one
institution's team in the pipelines or analyses of another institution's team — requires the
written consent of the data-originating Principal Investigator prior to such sharing, transfer,
or use. Written consent for the purposes of this section means a signed document or a documented
email exchange in which the data-originating PI explicitly approves the sharing or use in
question. Implied consent is not sufficient.
