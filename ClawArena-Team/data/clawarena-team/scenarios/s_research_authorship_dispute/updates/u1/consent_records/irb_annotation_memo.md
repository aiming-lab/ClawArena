## Purpose

This memorandum documents the IRB's formal review and acceptance of the handwritten correction
appearing on Clause 4.1 of the Joint IRB Consent Form (IRB-2024-GWAS-001), executed on 2024-09-28 by
all three Principal Investigators and the IRB Chair.

## Description of the Correction

During the execution ceremony on 2024-09-28, all three Principal Investigators identified a
typographical error in Clause 4.1 of the form. The phrase "secondary use" was struck through
by hand and replaced with "tertiary use" above the strikethrough, with the initials LJ (Prof.
Liang Jiewen), ANM (Prof. Aoife Ní Mhurchadha), and TS (Prof. Tariq Saleem) placed adjacent
to the correction. The corrected form was then signed by all three PIs and the IRB Chair.

## IRB Determination

The IRB has reviewed the handwritten correction and determined as follows:

1. **The correction is valid.** The handwritten substitution of "tertiary use" for "secondary
   use" in Clause 4.1 was initialed by all three Principal Investigators and occurred before
   the execution date. The correction is accepted as a valid modification to the text of
   Clause 4.1 per addendum IRB-2024-GWAS-001-A, effective 2024-10-03.

2. **The correction does not alter Clause 4.2.** The Clause 4.1 correction relates solely to
   the classification of permitted use categories in that clause. It has no bearing on the
   data-sharing scope restriction set out in Clause 4.2, which governs the permissible use
   of harmonized intermediate datasets generated under this collaboration. The scope restriction
   in Clause 4.2 — which requires prior written approval of all three Principal Investigators
   for any incorporation of harmonized datasets into derivative pipelines or third-party
   analyses — remains fully in effect as originally executed.

3. **Clause 4.2 is unaffected.** For the avoidance of doubt: the IRB's acceptance of the
   Clause 4.1 correction does not introduce any ambiguity into the scope of Clause 4.2, does
   not expand or restrict the written-approval requirement stated in Clause 4.2, and does not
   modify the definition of authorized use set out in that clause in any way.

## Scope of This Memorandum

This memorandum is issued solely to document the IRB's determination regarding the validity of
the Clause 4.1 correction. It does not purport to interpret, modify, or supersede any other
provision of the consent form, including Clause 4.2. Parties seeking to understand the full
scope of the data-use restrictions agreed to by the three PIs should refer to the signed consent
form image, which is the authoritative record.
