## I. Introduction

This document synthesizes a representative sample of ombudsperson decisions, institutional
review outcomes, and academic arbitration precedents addressing disputes over authorship credit
in multi-institutional collaborative research. The cases presented are anonymized composites
and do not represent specific identifiable disputes. They are intended to provide background
context for arbitration proceedings and to illustrate how established principles have been
applied in practice.
