## Synthesized Reference Document

**Prepared for:** Inter-University Ombudsperson Reference Library
**Date:** 2025-06-01
**Scope:** Survey of data governance norms in large-scale multi-ancestry genomic studies

## Executive Summary

This survey examines the data governance practices adopted by major multi-institutional genomic
consortia with respect to harmonized intermediate dataset management, consent scope enforcement,
and cross-site data integration. The survey draws on publicly available governance frameworks
from major biobank consortia and synthesizes common practice norms relevant to dispute resolution
in collaborative GWAS settings.
