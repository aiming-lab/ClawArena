## Introduction

This document presents five anonymized inter-institutional authorship arbitration outcomes from
the period 2018–2025. These cases have been selected as precedents most relevant to disputes
involving: (a) computational contribution weighting, (b) IRB consent scope for harmonized
datasets, (c) the evidentiary weight of git commit histories, and (d) the effect of retracted
preprints on current author-order determinations.
