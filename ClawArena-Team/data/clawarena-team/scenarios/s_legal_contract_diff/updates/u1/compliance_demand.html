Operative demand

Following an internal compliance review prompted by the European Cyber Resilience Act, GoldenLeaf Industries hereby requires that all active and prospective vendor agreements meet a minimum termination cap of 12 (twelve) months of Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), calculated on a trailing twelve month basis. This minimum supersedes any lower cap previously negotiated in earlier drafts (including, but not limited to, v3 (6 months) and v4 (9 months) of the Master Services Agreement currently before our respective legal teams).

The termination cap is the only material economic point being modified by this letter. All other v4 terms (notice period, SLA uptime, data processing addendum reference) remain as negotiated.
