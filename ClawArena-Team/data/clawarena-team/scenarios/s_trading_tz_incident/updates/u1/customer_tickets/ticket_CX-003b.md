## Firm's Position on Timezone

Gamma Investments LLC operates from New York. While our business hours are in
Eastern Time, our trading systems are configured to display and record all
timestamps in UTC without exception. The timestamps provided in this ticket and
in our original filing are UTC.

We note that FinClear Asia's reconciliation team has queried whether our timestamps
might reflect EDT (UTC-4). We reject this characterization. Our order management
system logs show UTC timestamps. We have verified this with our technology team.

However, we acknowledge that if FinClear Asia's records show a systematic 4-hour
discrepancy for our orders, this should be investigated thoroughly. If such a
discrepancy exists in the matching engine records, we would expect FinClear Asia
to use the matching engine fill timestamps as the authoritative source, not our
stated dispatch timestamps.
