# 6. Conclusion

We introduced ClusterMoE, an inference framework for mixture-of-experts language models that combines activation-similarity-based expert clustering with a pipeline-parallel scheduler. By co-locating experts that tend to be activated together and overlapping the residual cross-device communication with local compute, ClusterMoE cuts median end-to-end inference latency by 35% on a 70B-parameter, 64-expert model at typical operating points, without any change to the model's parameters or downstream quality. The benefit is largest in bandwidth-constrained deployments — including cross-node InfiniBand and budget PCIe configurations — where it widens to over 40%. ClusterMoE is implemented as a drop-in replacement for the standard expert-parallel runtime, can be applied to any pretrained MoE model, and is released as open source. We hope it lowers the operational cost of MoE deployment and makes the architecture practical for a wider range of production scenarios.
