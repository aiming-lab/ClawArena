# 6. Conclusion

We introduced DistillAlign, an alignment framework that distills the constitutional self-critique loop into a lightweight reward model and then uses that reward model in a standard RLHF training procedure. DistillAlign reduces toxicity by 52% relative to standard RLHF on the ToxicBench suite, matches vanilla Constitutional AI on alignment quality while training 3× faster, and integrates cleanly with existing RLHF infrastructure. The system is released as open source, along with the trained reward model and the synthetic preference dataset, to support reproduction and adaptation by other organisations. By making Constitutional AI cheaper without sacrificing its labelling efficiency, we hope DistillAlign accelerates the broader adoption of principle-based alignment in production language models.
