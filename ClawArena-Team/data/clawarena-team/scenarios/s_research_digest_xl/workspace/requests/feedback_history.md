## Standing Reminders (applies to all future digests)

1. **Watch the video, do not just read the slides.** The narration frequently contains
   framing and emphasis that is NOT captured in the slide text. Missing audio-only
   content is an automatic deduction.
2. **Do not use `notes_old/` or `refs/` as source material.** These are archived from
   previous projects and will mislead the analysis.
3. **File naming is exact.** If the spec says `output/notes/papers_summary.md`, that
   is the path. Do not create `output/paper_summaries.md` or similar variants.
4. **Parallelise where possible.** Reading four papers sequentially when they are
   independent is inefficient and will be noted.
