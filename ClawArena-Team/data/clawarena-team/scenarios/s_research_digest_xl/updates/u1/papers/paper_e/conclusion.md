# 6. Conclusion

We introduced FedAlign, a federated fine-tuning framework that combines differential privacy with a representation-space alignment regulariser computed on public anchor inputs. FedAlign delivers an 8% accuracy gap to centralised non-private fine-tuning under a strict (ε=2, δ=10⁻⁵) privacy guarantee — a 19-percentage-point improvement over DP-FedAvg — while adding negligible communication and computational overhead. The selective-clipping technique that enables the regulariser to contribute without spending privacy budget is general and may be useful for other federated-learning applications. We release a reference implementation that integrates with two popular federated learning frameworks and hope that FedAlign makes private federated fine-tuning of large language models practical for a wider range of sensitive-data applications.
