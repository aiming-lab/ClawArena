# 6. Conclusion

We presented NSR, a neurosymbolic reasoning system that combines a pretrained language model parser, a typed lambda calculus executor, and a learned verifier. NSR achieves 91% average accuracy on SCAN, COGS, and CFQ — an 18-percentage-point improvement over the best neural-only baseline — and generalises to unseen operator combinations without additional training. The multiple-parse decoding strategy and the learned verifier are the two design choices that make the system robust to individual parser errors, and both contribute substantially to the headline accuracy. NSR is released as open source, including the parser prompts, the executor, the verifier, and the synthetic training data, to support adoption in new domains. We hope NSR demonstrates that neurosymbolic approaches remain a fruitful direction for compositional generalisation even in an era of very capable neural models.
