## Causal interpretation

Migration v23 acquired a row-exclusive lock on orders.shipments at 14:22:58 and
held it for the full table range. Subsequent shipment-status updates from
payments-api blocked, retried, and exhausted the payments-api connection pool.
Each blocked request retained its request body in memory, driving payments-api
rss past 94% and triggering OOMKill at 14:23:51. The redis-fleet-2 eviction
storm at 14:24:33 was caused by payments-api retrying every cached entry as it
came back online from the restart.

**Therefore the true root cause is the unbatched migration v23 statement, not
memory pressure on payments-api and not the cache eviction storm.**
