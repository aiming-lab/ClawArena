# Charter Drafting and Amendment Guide — Version 4
# Synthesised Reference Document
# Covers: Version control, supersession rules, amendment procedures

---

## Overview

This guide provides practical guidance on corporate charter drafting, amendment,
and version control for Canadian listed companies. It is relevant as background
to the charter version management context of the governance pack.

---

## Chapter 1 — Charter Version Control Principles

Corporate charters should be version-controlled with: (a) a version number;
(b) an effective date; (c) an authorising board resolution reference;
(d) a clear supersession statement.

### Supersession Rules

When a new charter version is ratified, the supersession of the prior version
should be documented: (a) in the ratifying board resolution; (b) on the
signature page of the new charter; (c) in the corporate records and
governance document index.

The Corporate Secretary should ensure that archived versions are clearly
labelled "SUPERSEDED" and are not available in locations where they could be
mistaken for the operative document.

### Charter Ratification Record

The authoritative record of a charter ratification consists of:
(a) The ratifying board resolution (identifying the version, ratification date,
    and vote);
(b) The signed signature page of the new charter (identifying the board chair
    signatory, date of execution, and resolution reference);
(c) The filed copy with the corporate registry.

For questions about the operative charter version and ratification date, the
signed signature page is the primary authoritative source.
