# TSX Governance Requirements — 2026 Update
# Toronto Stock Exchange
# Synthesised Reference Document

---

## Overview

The Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX) imposes governance requirements on listed
issuers through the TSX Company Manual, national instruments, and TSX guidance
bulletins. This document summarises the key governance requirements effective
for fiscal year 2026.

---

## Part I — Board Composition

Listed issuers must maintain a majority of independent directors and an
independent board chair or lead independent director. Director independence
assessments must be disclosed in the annual proxy circular.

## Part II — Meeting Administration

The TSX expects listed issuers to maintain effective meeting administration
practices, including timely circulation of governance packs, clear agenda
structures, and formal minuting of all board decisions.

## Part III — Conflict of Interest Procedures

Listed issuers must have in place formal procedures for managing director
conflicts of interest, including disclosure, recusal, and board resolution
of disputed classifications.
