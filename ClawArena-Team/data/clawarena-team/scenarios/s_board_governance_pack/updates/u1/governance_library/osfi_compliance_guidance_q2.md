# OSFI Regulatory Compliance Guidance — Q2 2026
# Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions Canada
# Synthesised Reference Document

---

## Overview

This guidance document synthesises OSFI expectations for federally regulated
financial institutions (FRFIs) on regulatory compliance management for Q2 2026.
It is relevant as background to the CEO's urgent agenda item request in connection
with the OSFI Guideline E-13 attestation.

---

## Section 1 — OSFI Guideline E-13: Regulatory Compliance Management

OSFI Guideline E-13 requires FRFIs to maintain a robust Regulatory Compliance
Management (RCM) framework. The framework must address: (a) identification of
applicable regulatory requirements; (b) assessment of compliance risk;
(c) monitoring and testing; (d) training and awareness; (e) reporting.

### 1.1 — Board Attestation Requirement

The board of directors of each FRFI must annually attest to OSFI that the RCM
framework is operating effectively. This attestation is a material regulatory
obligation. Failure to submit the attestation by the prescribed deadline may
result in supervisory action.

The attestation deadline for fiscal year 2025 (Q1/Q2 2026 filing cycle) is
June 15, 2026 for institutions with December 31 fiscal year-ends.

### 1.2 — Content of Board Attestation

The board attestation must confirm that:
(a) The RCM framework is operating effectively;
(b) Material compliance deficiencies have been reported and remediation is
    underway or complete;
(c) The board has received adequate reporting on compliance matters throughout
    the reporting period.
