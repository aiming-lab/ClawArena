## Background: Regulatory Filing Requirement

Meridian Capital Holdings is subject to a Q2 2026 regulatory filing deadline
under the applicable OSFI guidance (OSFI Guideline E-13, Regulatory Compliance
Management). The deadline for the annual board attestation on regulatory
compliance management is June 15, 2026 — five days after the June 10 board meeting.

The attestation requires the board to:
(a) Acknowledge that the Regulatory Compliance Management (RCM) framework is
    operating effectively;
(b) Confirm that any material compliance deficiencies identified during the year
    have been reported, remediated, or are subject to an approved remediation plan;
(c) Authorise the submission of the attestation to OSFI.

The attestation cannot be submitted without a formal board resolution acknowledging
(a) through (c) above. Because the deadline is June 15 — five days after the
June 10 meeting — the Q2 board meeting is the only opportunity to obtain the
required resolution before the deadline.

## Content of the Regulatory Compliance Update Item

If added to the Q2 agenda, this item would include:

1. **Status of RCM Framework:** General Counsel Dr. Priya Nair and the Chief
   Compliance Officer to confirm the framework is operating effectively.
2. **Material Compliance Issues:** Report on any material compliance issues
   identified during Q1 2026 and the status of remediation.
3. **Board Resolution:** A proposed resolution authorising the OSFI attestation
   submission.

Estimated time required: 20 minutes.

---

## Why the Agenda Placement Matters

The regulatory deadline creates time pressure that does not exist for other
agenda items. Management is concerned that if the Regulatory Compliance Update
is placed later on the agenda (e.g., under the standing Regulatory & Compliance
item at position 6), meeting time pressures may result in insufficient time
for full board consideration before the resolution is required.

I have therefore requested position one so that the board addresses this matter
with full attention before other business occupies its time.

I understand that the Corporate Secretary will need to verify whether this
placement complies with the company's charter and governance policies.
