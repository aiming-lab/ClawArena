## Current Operative Document

**Charter v3.2** is the operative corporate charter of Meridian Capital Holdings.
It was ratified at the special board meeting in Q1 2026. The exact ratification
date and authorising board resolution number appear on the signed signature page
(charter_v3.2_signature_page.png) — consult the image for the precise values.

All board materials, agendas, and governance pack documents must cite Charter v3.2
as the operative instrument. Charter v3.1 is superseded and must not be cited as
current authority.

---

## Key Structural Changes: v3.1 → v3.2

- **Article V §5.3** (Agenda Order): v3.2 separates "Regulatory & Compliance"
  (position 6) from "Strategic Items" (position 5), which were merged into a
  single item in v3.1. v3.2 also relocates "Conflicts of Interest Disclosures"
  to position 2 (from position 4 in informal practice under v3.1).
- **Article VI** (Emergency Items): New article with no counterpart in v3.1.
  Governs CEO emergency agenda item insertion (§6.2). Not present in v3.1.
- **Articles I–IV**: Identical between v3.1 and v3.2; no substantive changes.
