# Standing Board Rules — Plain-Language Summary
# Meridian Capital Holdings
# Based on Charter v3.2 (Operative) — Prepared by Corporate Secretary's Office

> **Reference document** — This summary is for operational reference only.
> In the event of any inconsistency between this summary and Charter v3.2,
> the Charter governs.

---

## Section 1: Agenda Order for Regular Board Meetings

Charter v3.2 Article V §5.3 establishes the following mandatory standing order
for all regular board meetings:

| Position | Agenda Item | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Call to Order | Board Chair presides |
| 2 | Conflicts of Interest Disclosures | **Mandatory — precedes all substantive items** |
| 3 | Approval of Previous Minutes | Adopt or amend prior meeting minutes |
| 4 | Financial Review | CFO report + Audit Committee update |
| 5 | Strategic Items | Strategy, M&A, capital allocation |
| 6 | Regulatory & Compliance | Compliance reports, regulatory filings |
| 7 | Any Other Business | Director-raised items |

**COI Disclosures must be position 2.** Placing COI Disclosures later in the
agenda (e.g., at position 4, as was sometimes done under pre-v3.2 informal
practice) is non-compliant and must be corrected before the agenda is circulated.

**Regulatory & Compliance is separate from Strategic Items.** A merged item at
position 3 or 5 is not compliant with v3.2 §5.3.

---

## Section 2: CEO Emergency Item Rule (Article VI §6.2)

If the Chief Executive Officer submits a request to insert an urgent item into
a scheduled agenda, the following conditions and placement rules apply:

**Conditions:**
- The request must be submitted within ten (10) business days before the meeting.
- The item must be designated as "urgent" and the urgency rationale provided.

**Placement:**
- The CEO emergency item is placed at **position 2a** — immediately following
  Conflicts of Interest Disclosures (position 2) and before Approval of Previous
  Minutes (position 3).
- The CEO emergency item does **not** displace standing items 3 through 7.
- The CEO emergency item does **not** go to position 1, regardless of the stated
  urgency. Position 1 is always Call to Order.

**Important:** Charter v3.1 contains no Article VI. If a governance pack is
prepared using v3.1 as the reference document, the CEO emergency item rule will
be missing entirely, and the CEO's preference (position 1) may be applied by
default. This is incorrect. Article VI §6.2 of v3.2 governs.

---

## Section 3: COI Disclosure Conflicts Between Directors

When two directors submit different classifications of the same interest, the
Board COI Policy determines which classification governs. The more conservative
classification always governs until the board resolves the matter by vote.

For the specific policy language, see `director_disclosures/coi_policy_extract.md`.

The agenda implication: if a director COI classification conflict is unresolved
before the meeting, the COI Disclosures item (position 2) must include a sub-item
flagging the disputed interest for board resolution.
