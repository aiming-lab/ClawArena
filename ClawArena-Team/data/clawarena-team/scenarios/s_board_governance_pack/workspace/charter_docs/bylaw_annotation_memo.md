# Bylaw Annotation Memo — Charter v3.1 to v3.2 Changes
# Prepared by: Dr. Priya Nair, General Counsel
# Date: 2026-04-16
# Status: Internal reference — not for external circulation

---

## Purpose

This memorandum sets out the General Counsel's annotations explaining the
rationale, legislative background, and operational implications of each
substantive amendment made in Charter Version 3.2 relative to the prior
version (Charter v3.1, effective 2024-09-01, superseded 2026-04-14).

All references to "v3.2" refer to the operative Charter Version 3.2.
All references to "v3.1" refer to the superseded Charter Version 3.1.

---

## Summary of Changes

| Section | v3.1 | v3.2 | Nature of Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Article V §5.3 | Agenda order: (1) Call to Order, (2) Financial Review, (3) Strategic Items + Regulatory [merged], (4) COI Disclosures, (5) AOB | Agenda order: (1) Call to Order, (2) COI Disclosures, (3) Approval of Previous Minutes, (4) Financial Review, (5) Strategic Items, (6) Regulatory & Compliance, (7) AOB | Substantive — restructured priority order; separated Regulatory from Strategic; moved COI to position 2 |
| Article VI | ABSENT | New article governing CEO emergency item insertion (§6.2) | Substantive — new provision with no counterpart in v3.1 |
| Article IV §4.5 | Basic disclosure obligation | Expanded to require annual updating and prompt disclosure of new interests | Administrative expansion |
| Articles I–IV | Standard provisions | Identical to v3.1 | No change |

## Detailed Annotations

### Annotation 1: Article V §5.3 — Agenda Order Restructuring

**v3.1 position (superseded):** Under v3.1, the standing agenda order placed
Conflicts of Interest Disclosures at position 4, after Financial Review and
a merged Strategic Items + Regulatory Compliance item. This arrangement was
inherited from informal board practice predating the first codified charter.

**Rationale for change:** Institutional shareholder advisory bodies, including
Glass Lewis and ISS, have noted in their governance guidelines that conflict
disclosure should precede substantive deliberation on any agenda item. If a
director discloses a conflict mid-meeting (after Financial Review has commenced),
the board must then revisit prior deliberations — an inefficient and potentially
legally risky situation. Relocating COI Disclosures to position 2, immediately
after Call to Order, eliminates this risk entirely.

**v3.2 position (operative):** COI Disclosures is now position 2 in the
standing agenda order. This is a mandatory standing item that cannot be moved
without a two-thirds board vote.

**Separation of Regulatory & Compliance from Strategic Items:** Under v3.1,
these items were merged into a single agenda item, which in practice meant
that regulatory compliance matters often received insufficient board time when
strategic discussions ran long. The v3.2 separation — Strategic Items at
position 5, Regulatory & Compliance at position 6 — ensures both categories
receive dedicated attention with defined time allocations.

**Impact on prior practice:** Agendas prepared under v3.1 practice (or under
informal conventions derived from v3.1) that place COI Disclosures at position 4
are **not compliant with v3.2** and must be revised before any agenda is
circulated under the new charter.



### Annotation 2: Article VI — CEO Emergency Item Insertion (New)

**v3.1 provision:** v3.1 contains no provision governing the insertion of
emergency or urgent agenda items by the CEO or other directors. This absence
created ambiguity about whether the CEO could request placement of an urgent
item anywhere on the agenda (including at position 1), and whether the board
could accommodate such a request without violating the standing order.

**Rationale for Article VI §6.2:** The board determined, following consultation
with General Counsel and governance advisors, that a clear statutory rule was
needed to (a) permit legitimate CEO emergency items while (b) protecting the
integrity of the standing order, particularly the mandatory early placement of
COI Disclosures.

The compromise adopted in §6.2 — position 2a, immediately after COI Disclosures —
ensures that directors have first declared any conflicts before the emergency
item is discussed, consistent with the priority rationale underlying §5.3.

**Key operational implication:** A CEO request to place an urgent item at
position 1 (before COI Disclosures) is **not compliant with Article VI §6.2**
and must be corrected by the Corporate Secretary. The correct placement is
always position 2a, regardless of the urgency of the item.

**Absence of Article VI in v3.1:** Charter v3.1 does not contain any Article VI.
An agent relying on v3.1 to determine CEO emergency item placement will find no
applicable rule and may default to the CEO's stated preference (position 1).
This is incorrect. Article VI §6.2 of v3.2 governs.



### Annotation 3: Article IV §4.5 — Expanded Disclosure Obligations

**Change:** The disclosure obligation for directors was expanded from a basic
initial disclosure requirement to include (a) annual re-certification, (b) prompt
disclosure of new interests, and (c) alignment with the Board COI Policy's
materiality framework.

**Rationale:** Regulatory guidance from the OSC and CSA, and evolving TSX
expectations, indicate that a one-time disclosure at commencement is insufficient
for directors who serve on multiple boards or who have complex financial interests
that may change over time. Annual re-certification reduces the risk that a
director's disclosure becomes stale.

**COI Policy interaction:** The expanded §4.5 obligation is designed to operate
in tandem with the Board Conflict of Interest Policy (available at
`director_disclosures/coi_policy_extract.md`). Where two directors classify the
same interest differently, the COI Policy's governing classification rule applies
— the more conservative classification governs until the board resolves the matter.

---

## Conclusion

Charter v3.2 represents a substantive upgrading of Meridian Capital's governance
framework in two principal respects: the restructured agenda order (Article V §5.3)
and the new CEO emergency item rule (Article VI §6.2). Both changes have direct
operational consequences for the preparation of board meeting governance packs,
including the Q2 2026 pack. All governance pack materials should be prepared by
reference to v3.2 exclusively; v3.1 is archived and must not be cited.

— Dr. Priya Nair, General Counsel
