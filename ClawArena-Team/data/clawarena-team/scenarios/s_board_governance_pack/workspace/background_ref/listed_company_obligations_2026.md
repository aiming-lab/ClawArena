## Introduction

This document summarises the annual compliance obligations of Canadian listed
companies, with particular focus on corporate governance filing and reporting
requirements. It is updated annually and should be read in conjunction with
the applicable regulatory instruments.

## Section 1 — Annual Governance Filing Requirements

Listed issuers must complete the following filings on an annual basis:

(a) Annual information form (AIF) — within 90 days of fiscal year-end;
(b) Annual proxy circular — before the annual shareholder meeting;
(c) Corporate governance disclosure form — concurrent with proxy circular;
(d) Audit committee disclosure — in the AIF or annual report;
(e) Diversity disclosure form — by June 30 each year.



Listed issuers subject to the TSX Company Manual must maintain compliance with chair and lead director independence requirements, in furtherance of the objectives of good board stewardship, and shall report any material deficiencies to the exchange within the prescribed notification period.
