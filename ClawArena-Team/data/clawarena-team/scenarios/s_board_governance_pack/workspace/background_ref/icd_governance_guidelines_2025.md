## Foreword

The Institute of Corporate Directors (ICD) publishes guidance for directors and
corporate secretaries on current governance expectations in Canada and
internationally. This synthesised reference summarises key themes from ICD
guidance relevant to board governance pack preparation and meeting administration.

## Chapter 1 — Board Composition and Independence

Effective boards are characterised by an appropriate mix of skills, experience,
and independence. The ICD emphasises that independence is not merely a technical
status but a state of mind: independent directors must be able and willing to
challenge management and exercise independent judgment on all matters before the board.

### 1.1 — Independence Framework

The ICD applies the following principles to assess director independence:

(a) No material relationship with the issuer, management, or controlling shareholders;
(b) No current or recent employment relationship with the issuer;
(c) No family relationship with management or controlling shareholders;
(d) No material financial relationship other than director compensation.
