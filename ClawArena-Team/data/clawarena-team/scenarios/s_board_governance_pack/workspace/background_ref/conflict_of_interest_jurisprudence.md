## Introduction

This document compiles synthesised case law and regulatory precedent on director
conflict of interest classification in Canadian financial services governance.
It provides background for the materiality threshold analysis and the governing
classification rule applied when directors disagree.

---

## Part I — Materiality Standard

### Case 1 — Re Greenfield Holdings (2019)

The Ontario Securities Commission affirmed that the materiality standard for
director COI classification is an objective one: the question is whether a
reasonable person in the director's position would recognize the interest as
material. Self-classification is the starting point but is not determinative.

### Case 2 — Alderton v. Pacific Rim Trust (2021)

The British Columbia Court of Appeal held that where two directors classify the
same interest differently, the board is entitled to — and in some circumstances
required to — resolve the classification by vote. The more conservative
classification should be applied as an interim measure pending resolution.
