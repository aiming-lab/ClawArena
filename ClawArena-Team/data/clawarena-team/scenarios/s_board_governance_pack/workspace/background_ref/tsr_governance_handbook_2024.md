# TSX Governance Handbook — 2024 Edition
# Synthesised Reference Document (Non-authoritative)
# Background reference for listed company governance

---

## Introduction

This handbook provides a comprehensive overview of corporate governance expectations
for companies listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange. It covers board composition,
committee mandates, shareholder rights, continuous disclosure, and meeting procedures.

---

## Part I — Listed Issuer Obligations Overview

Listed companies are subject to a complex array of governance obligations arising
from applicable corporate law, securities legislation, TSX listing requirements,
and market practice. The Corporate Secretary plays a central coordinating role in
ensuring that all these obligations are met on a timely basis.
