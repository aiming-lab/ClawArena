## Part I — Board Composition Requirements

### Section 1.1 — Independent Directors

Listed issuers must have a majority of independent directors on the board.
For the purposes of this requirement, "independent" has the meaning ascribed in
National Instrument 52-110 (Audit Committees) and applicable TSX guidance.

### Section 1.2 — Board Chair Independence

The Board Chair must be an independent director, or the issuer must designate
an independent Lead Director with clearly defined responsibilities.

### Section 1.3 — Director Qualifications

The TSX expects listed issuers to have in place a policy governing director
qualifications, including skills matrices, independence assessments, and
procedures for assessing prospective nominees. The policy must be disclosed
in the annual proxy circular.

---

## Part II — Audit Committee Requirements

### Section 2.1 — Composition

The audit committee must comprise at least three members, all of whom must be
independent directors. At least one member must be financially literate; the
TSX strongly encourages all members to be financially literate.

### Section 2.2 — Mandate

The audit committee's mandate must cover, at minimum: (a) oversight of the
external auditor relationship; (b) review and approval of financial statements
prior to board approval; (c) oversight of internal audit; (d) oversight of risk
management processes as they relate to financial reporting; (e) review of
related-party transactions.

---

## Part III — Board Meeting and Agenda Requirements

### Section 3.1 — Meeting Frequency

Listed issuers must hold at least four board meetings per calendar year. The
TSX expects that all board meetings be formally minuted and that minutes be
retained in accordance with applicable corporate law.

### Section 3.2 — Agenda and Materials

The TSX expects boards to adopt a practice of distributing meeting materials
sufficiently in advance of meetings to allow directors adequate preparation time.
The TSX does not prescribe a minimum advance circulation period but notes that
institutional investor expectations and governance advisory guidelines typically
call for at least five business days.

### Section 3.3 — Agenda Order and Conflict of Interest

The TSX does not prescribe a specific standing agenda order for listed issuers.
However, the TSX expects boards to have in place adequate procedures for managing
director conflicts of interest. In particular:

(a) Directors must disclose conflicts before deliberating on conflicted matters;
(b) The board's constitutional documents (charter, bylaws, or equivalent)
    should specify the procedures for conflict disclosure and management;
(c) Where the board's own charter specifies a mandatory conflict disclosure
    agenda position, the charter governs, and any governance pack must reflect
    the charter-mandated order.

---

## Part IV — Continuous Disclosure Obligations

### Section 4.1 — Material Change Reporting

Listed issuers must issue a press release and file a material change report within
ten calendar days of a material change in the issuer's business, operations, or
capital. Directors are responsible for ensuring that they do not trade in the
issuer's securities while in possession of undisclosed material information.
