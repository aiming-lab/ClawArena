## Part I — Governance Framework

### 1.1 — Purpose

This Governance Policy establishes the framework for the governance of Meridian
Capital Holdings, including board structure, meeting procedures, information
management, and stakeholder accountability. The Policy is designed to complement
the Corporation's operative charter (currently Charter v3.2) and applicable law.

### 1.2 — Relationship to Charter

In the event of any inconsistency between this Policy and the operative charter,
the charter governs. This Policy v2 was approved contemporaneously with the
ratification of Charter v3.2 and is designed to be fully consistent with it.

### 1.3 — Governance Pack Composition Requirements

The Corporate Secretary is responsible for assembling the governance pack for
each regular board meeting. The pack must include all of the following:

(a) Confirmed agenda (reflecting Charter v3.2 §5.3 standing order);
(b) Charter version confirmation (identifying operative charter by version,
    ratification date, and board resolution number);
(c) Director COI disclosure reconciliation;
(d) Any CEO urgent item documentation;
(e) Supporting materials for each agenda item;
(f) Pack completeness certification.

### 1.4 — Pack Circulation Deadline

The governance pack must be circulated to all directors at least **five business
days** before the scheduled board meeting. Failure to meet this deadline must be
reported to the Board Chair and the Governance Committee.

---

## Part II — Director Conduct

### 2.1 — Fiduciary Duties

Directors must exercise their powers and discharge their duties honestly, in good
faith, and with a view to the best interests of the Corporation. Directors are
subject to the fiduciary duties prescribed by the Canada Business Corporations Act
and applicable corporate law.

### 2.2 — Conflicts of Interest

The Board Conflict of Interest Policy governs all matters related to director
conflicts. Directors must comply with that policy in addition to the disclosure
requirements of this Governance Policy and the Charter.

Where two directors classify the same outside interest differently, the Corporate
Secretary must flag the discrepancy in the governance pack and ensure it is
addressed as a sub-item under the COI Disclosures agenda item.
