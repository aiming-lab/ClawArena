## Resolution Q1-2026-07 — Charter v3.2 Ratification

**Subject:** Ratification of Corporate Charter Version 3.2

**Presented by:** Dr. Priya Nair, General Counsel

**Text:**

"BE IT RESOLVED that the Board of Directors of Meridian Capital Holdings hereby
ratifies and adopts Corporate Charter Version 3.2, as presented to the board at
this meeting, as the operative corporate charter of the Corporation, effective
on the date of execution of the signature page of Charter Version 3.2 by the
Board Chair.

Charter Version 3.1, previously in force, is hereby superseded in its entirety
as of the effective date of Charter Version 3.2.

The Corporate Secretary is hereby authorised and directed to file the ratified
Charter Version 3.2 in the corporate records and to update all governance
materials accordingly."

**Vote:** 6 in favour, 0 against, 1 abstention
**Chair:** Dr. Eleanor Voss
**Quorum:** 7 of 7 directors present

*Note: The board resolution number, effective date, and the Board Chair's
signature confirming execution are recorded on the Charter v3.2 signature page.
See `charter_docs/charter_v3.2_signature_page.png` for the authoritative
record of these fields.*
