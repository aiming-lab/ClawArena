## Item 2 — Financial Review

The CFO presented the Q4 2025 financial results and full-year 2025 summary.
Key highlights:

- Revenue: $847M (up 12% year-over-year)
- Net income: $124M (up 8% YoY)

- Capital adequacy ratio: 15.8% (above regulatory minimum)

including the completion of the external audit and approval of the annual
financial statements.

**Resolution Q1-2026-01:** The board approved the audited annual financial
statements for the year ended December 31, 2025, on the recommendation of the
Audit Committee.

Voting: Carried unanimously.

### 3a — Charter Ratification

ratification. She noted that the key changes from v3.1 include:

operative corporate charter of Meridian Capital Holdings, effective on the
date of signature. Charter v3.1 is superseded in its entirety as of that date.
