## 2. Pack Circulation Deadline

The complete governance pack must be circulated to all seven board members no
later than **five business days before the meeting date**.

Circulation deadline: **2026-06-03**

Late circulation is a material breach of the company's obligations under the
TSX listing requirements and the internal Governance Policy v2.

## 3. Required Pack Sections

The governance pack must include all of the following sections:

1. **Confirmed Agenda** — final agenda reflecting the correct item order per the
   in-force corporate charter; include position numbers for all items.
2. **Charter Version Confirmation** — a written confirmation identifying the
   operative charter version, its ratification date, and the board resolution
   authorising ratification; the confirmation must cite the actual signed document.
3. **Director Conflict-of-Interest Disclosures** — a reconciliation of all
   submitted COI disclosure forms; if any classification conflicts exist between
   directors, the reconciliation must identify the governing classification under
   the COI policy and the required next step.
4. **CEO Urgent Item Documentation** (if applicable) — if a CEO urgent agenda
   item has been submitted within the qualifying window, the pack must include the
   item brief, the charter provision governing its placement, and the resulting
   position in the revised agenda.
5. **Pack Completeness Certification** — a signed or system-verified attestation
   that all required sections are present, the charter version is current, and
   the circulation deadline has been met.

## 4. Notes for Corporate Secretary

- Use the agenda template in `agenda_working/agenda_template_v2.md` as the basis
  for the confirmed agenda; edit the template rather than drafting from scratch.

  classification of the same interest, the COI policy governs which classification
  applies; consult `director_disclosures/coi_policy_extract.md`.
