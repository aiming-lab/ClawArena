## Part B — Outside Interests Disclosure

I, Raymond Chen, hereby disclose the following outside interests that may
be relevant to my duties as a director of Meridian Capital Holdings:

### Interest 1: Nexus Fintech Partners LP

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Entity Name | Nexus Fintech Partners LP |
| Nature of Interest | Limited partnership unit holding (investment) |
| Percentage of Interest | < 0.5% of fund net asset value (NAV) |
| Date of Acquisition | 2024-11-30 |
| Relationship to Meridian Capital | Limited indirect relationship — see rationale |

**Classification:** NON-MATERIAL

**Rationale for Non-Material Classification:**

My holding in Nexus Fintech Partners LP represents less than 0.5% of the fund's
net asset value. This is well below the 5% threshold established in the Board
COI Policy as indicative of a potentially material interest. The fund is a
broadly diversified fintech venture fund and does not hold any direct equity,
debt, or warrant interest in Meridian Capital Holdings or any of its direct
subsidiaries to the best of my knowledge.

My holding does not give me any control, board representation, or advisory rights
in the fund or in any of its portfolio companies. I do not receive any
compensation or other benefit from the fund beyond investment returns.

Given the above, I classify this interest as non-material and do not consider
it to require recusal from any agenda item at the Q2 meeting.
