# Q2 2026 Regulatory Compliance Memo
# Meridian Capital Holdings — General Counsel's Office
# Date: 2026-05-01
# Prepared by: Dr. Priya Nair, General Counsel

---

## Purpose

This memo summarises the key regulatory compliance matters requiring board
attention at the Q2 2026 meeting and provides background on the applicable
regulatory framework. It is intended to support preparation of the governance
pack and should be read in conjunction with the relevant agenda items.

---

## Section 1 — OSC and CSA Regulatory Updates

The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) and the Canadian Securities Administrators
(CSA) have issued the following guidance relevant to Meridian Capital Holdings
in Q1/Q2 2026:

(a) Updated guidance on continuous disclosure obligations for financial services
    issuers — effective March 1, 2026;
(b) Revised expectations for board-level oversight of cybersecurity risk;
(c) Enhanced disclosure requirements for director independence assessments in
    annual proxy circulars (effective for meetings after July 1, 2026).

## Section 2 — TSX Compliance Obligations

The TSX has notified Meridian Capital Holdings of the following compliance
matters requiring board acknowledgment at the Q2 meeting:

(a) Filing of the annual diversity disclosure form (due June 30, 2026);
(b) Confirmation that the audit committee composition meets the updated
    independence requirements;
(c) Filing of the corporate governance disclosure form for FY 2025 annual report.

## Section 3 — OSFI Guidance (Federally Regulated Entity Provisions)

As a financial services holding company, Meridian Capital is subject to relevant
OSFI guidance on corporate governance for federally regulated financial institutions.
The following OSFI guidance items require board-level review in Q2 2026:

(a) OSFI Guideline E-13 (Regulatory Compliance Management) — annual attestation
    required at the Q2 meeting;
(b) OSFI expectations on board risk committee oversight — the Risk Committee
    must report to the full board on its Q2 risk review at the June 10 meeting.
