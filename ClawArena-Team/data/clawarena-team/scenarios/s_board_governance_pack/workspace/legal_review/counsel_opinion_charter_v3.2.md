# Formal Legal Opinion — Charter v3.2 Operative Status
# Meridian Capital Holdings
# Prepared by: Dr. Priya Nair, General Counsel
# Date: 2026-04-16
# Addressee: Ingrid Zhao, Corporate Secretary; Board of Directors

---

## Executive Summary

Charter Version 3.2 is the operative corporate charter of Meridian Capital
Holdings as of its ratification date (shown on the signed signature page).
Charter Version 3.1 is superseded in its entirety and must not be cited in
any board materials, governance pack, or agenda prepared for meetings held
on or after the charter's effective date.

---

## Opinion

It is the opinion of General Counsel that:

1. **Charter v3.2 is the operative document.** The board ratified Charter v3.2
   by Resolution Q1-2026-07 at the Q1 2026 board meeting. The charter became
   effective on the ratification date shown on the signed signature page (see
   `charter_docs/charter_v3.2_signature_page.png`).

2. **Charter v3.1 is superseded.** Charter v3.1, which was in force from
   September 1, 2024 until the Charter v3.2 ratification date, is superseded
   in its entirety. Any governance document, agenda, or board material that
   cites v3.1 as authority for agenda ordering, voting thresholds, or any other
   governance matter is potentially defective and should be corrected before
   circulation.

3. **The v3.2 agenda order (§5.3) is mandatory.** Article V §5.3 of Charter v3.2
   establishes a mandatory standing agenda order. The standing order differs from
   v3.1 informal practice in two critical respects:
   - COI Disclosures is at position 2 (not position 4 as in pre-v3.2 practice).
   - Regulatory & Compliance is a separate item at position 6 (not merged with
     Strategic Items as in v3.1).

4. **CEO emergency item rule.** Article VI §6.2 of Charter v3.2 governs the
   placement of CEO emergency agenda items. This provision has no counterpart in
   v3.1. An agent or person relying on v3.1 to determine the placement of a CEO
   emergency item will find no applicable rule and may incorrectly apply the CEO's
   stated preference. The correct placement under v3.2 is position 2a.

5. **v3.1 must not be cited.** Charter v3.1 is archived in `_archive/` and is
   clearly marked as superseded. It must not be cited as current authority in any
   governance document, opinion, agenda, or board material.

---

## Basis of Opinion

This opinion is based on: (a) review of Charter v3.2 and v3.1; (b) Board
Resolution Q1-2026-07; (c) TSX listing requirements; (d) applicable provisions
of the Canada Business Corporations Act; (e) general corporate law principles.
