== Summary ==

The payment-svc microservice in namespace payments began returning HTTP 504
at 03:11 UTC and triggered an OOM cascade across three adjacent pods. The
root cause is suspected to be an interaction between:

  1. A newly deployed admission webhook (v2.4.1) introduced at 02:50 UTC.
  2. A helm upgrade of the payment-svc chart to version 1.18.3 at 02:55 UTC.
  3. A node kernel patch applied to node pool eu-west-2a during the maintenance
     window at 02:30 UTC.
